# Open Questions — the analytical agenda

*Owner-defined. The daily agent updates the **Current read** and **Evidence log** of each question every run, never the question itself. New questions: owner adds them here. Retired questions: owner moves them to the bottom with a one-line resolution.*

**Rules for the agent:** For each question, state a current read with a confidence (low / medium / high), cite the newest dated evidence for and against, and name what would change the read. A question with no new evidence today keeps yesterday's read and its date. Never pad. The briefing leads with the two or three questions where the read moved.

---

## Q1. How much oil and product is actually leaving the Gulf, and by what route?
*AIS transit counts at Hormuz are a floor, not a measurement: escorted or dark transits do not appear. Reconcile visible transits against arrivals at destination ports (Sikka, Vadinar, Ningbo, Qingdao, Ulsan), loadings at Fujairah and Yanbu, and exporter-country tanker export tonnage. Divergence between arrivals and visible transits is the dark-flow signal.*

**Current read:** The export system is re-plumbed outside the strait (Fujairah as primary loading point), while November has given back October's recovery — Kpler put Hormuz throughput at ~10 mb/d on Nov 10 against 13.4 on Nov 1. The Hormuz AIS tanker series is now so thin (0.4 tankers/day) that its +476% DWT jump is a hull-mix artefact, not a volume signal, and should be read for direction only. Confidence high on the artefact reading, medium-high on the November decline; read changed 2026-11-23.
**Evidence log:**
- 2026-11-23: PortWatch week to Nov 15 — Hormuz **2.3 transits/day (prior 3.9)**, tankers **0.4/day (prior 1.0)** but tanker DWT **18k/day, +476% w/w** and −99% vs 2025: fewer, larger hulls. Suez tanker DWT −16% w/w on rising total transits — same substitution. Against UKMTO's 263 transits in the week to Nov 18, AIS visibility is **0.9%** ([Maritime Executive Nov 23](https://maritime-executive.com/article/two-more-tankers-hit-off-oman-as-houthis-threaten-saudi-red-sea-ports); PortWatch, agent arithmetic).
- 2026-11-22: Kpler via Bloomberg — Hormuz oil flows **13.4 → ~10.0 mb/d, Nov 1–10 (−25%)**; MEG VLCC loadings 7.4 mb/d in the first half of November vs 9.3 across October; Banchero Costa puts Arabian Gulf crude shipments −27.6% y/y Jan–Oct ([House of Saud Nov 21](https://houseofsaud.com/hormuz-oil-volumes-fall-november-2026)). Goldman dates the surplus to October's 2 mb/d gain ([BusinessToday Nov 22](https://www.businesstoday.in/markets/story/goldman-sachs-sees-brent-easing-to-78-by-late-2027-despite-ongoing-west-asia-tensions-heres-why-568921-2026-11-22)).
- 2026-11-18: PortWatch week to Nov 13 — **Ras Tanura 0 kt exports (prior 261), Ras Laffan 108 (428), Jebel Ali 302 (277) = 410 kt vs 966, −58%**; **Fujairah 1,198 kt on 39 tanker calls vs 874 kt prior week (+37%)**; Yanbu zero for a third week (PortWatch, agent arithmetic).
- 2026-11-17: AD Ports and Gulftainer move **~3,500 TEU/day** inland from Fujairah, Khor Fakkan and Dibba; ships wait **~9 days for a berth off Fujairah plus 15 days to clear dockside** ([Maritime Executive Nov 15](https://maritime-executive.com/article/arabian-sea-ports-are-booming-as-the-conflict-drags-on)).
- 2026-11-19: Fujairah bunker fuel-oil imports **103k bbl/d in October against 218k in February**; August bunker sales **−65% y/y**; recovery to pre-war sales not expected before 4Q 2027 ([EnterpriseAM Nov 19](https://enterpriseam.com/infrastructure/2026/11/19/fujairah-has-found-new-fuel-for-its-bunker-trade-but-winning-back-the-ships-that-refuelled-there-could-take-years)).
**Would change the read:** A Kpler print back above 12 mb/d, or Fujairah exports falling below ~900 kt in a week while inside-Gulf terminal loadings stay suppressed.

## Q2. What is really happening inside Iran: goods, borders, rail, people?
*Below the social-media layer. Border crossings and truck traffic at Bazargan/Gürbulak (Turkey), Shalamcheh and Mehran (Iraq), Mirjaveh/Taftan (Pakistan), Islam Qala/Dogharoun (Afghanistan), Astara (Azerbaijan), Sarakhs/Incheh Borun (Turkmenistan). Port calls at Caspian ports (Anzali, Amirabad, Nowshahr) versus Gulf ports (Shahid Rajaee, Bushehr, Khomeini). Rail: INSTC, Iran–Turkey, Iran–Turkmenistan. People: outbound flows to Turkey, Armenia, Iraq, Pakistan; internal displacement; flight schedules at IKA and Mashhad.*

**Current read:** The Caspian "inversion" is a vessel-count artefact: by tonnage the Gulf never stopped being the main door (345 kt vs 26 kt in the week to Nov 13) and Iranian Gulf calls recovered to 30 from 19. The land blockade bites through congestion rather than policy, and the domestic distribution failure is now producing organised labour action — a nationwide truckers' strike from Nov 22. Confidence medium; read unchanged since 2026-11-22.
**Evidence log:**
- 2026-11-23: The Union of Truck Drivers and Trucking Associations announced support for a **nationwide strike from Nov 22**, with stoppages reported in Shiraz, Chabahar and Dezful; a driver cites 620m rials (~$230) for oil and filters against a **166m rial (~$62) minimum monthly wage** ([Iran International Nov 22](https://www.iranintl.com/en/202611222553)). Bread purchase controls now run in **seven provinces** against ~280–290k flagged ID numbers, fuel-card restrictions in **11 provinces**, two-day petrol queues reported in Hormozgan; bread-and-cereal inflation above 120% ([NCRI Nov 21](https://www.ncr-iran.org/en/news/iran-protests/irans-bread-restrictions-and-fuel-shortages-fuel-unrest-as-regime-warns-of-deeper-crises)).
- 2026-11-22: PortWatch week to Nov 13 — **Iranian Gulf calls 30 vs 19 prior (+58%)**, Caspian 37 vs 40; Gulf imports **345 kt against the Caspian's 26 kt**; Bandar Khomeini 0 → 221 kt; Shahid Rajaee 4 → 14 calls and 148 kt of exports vs 56 kt in 2025 (PortWatch, agent arithmetic).
- 2026-11-16: Fuel distribution failure: Bandar Abbas residents blocked a road near a Rajaei-district station over empty pumps; a vice-president says "we really have a supply problem" ([Iran International Nov 15](https://www.iranintl.com/en/202611156648)).

**Would change the read:** A dated Bazargan truck count showing the queue clearing, or a second consecutive week of Iranian Gulf calls above 25 with tonnage confirming it.
