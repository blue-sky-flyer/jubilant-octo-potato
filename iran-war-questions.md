# Open Questions — the analytical agenda

*Owner-defined. The daily agent updates the **Current read** and **Evidence log** of each question every run, never the question itself. New questions: owner adds them here. Retired questions: owner moves them to the bottom with a one-line resolution.*

**Rules for the agent:** For each question, state a current read with a confidence (low / medium / high), cite the newest dated evidence for and against, and name what would change the read. A question with no new evidence today keeps yesterday's read and its date. Never pad. The briefing leads with the two or three questions where the read moved.

---

## Q1. How much oil and product is actually leaving the Gulf, and by what route?
*AIS transit counts at Hormuz are a floor, not a measurement: escorted or dark transits do not appear. Reconcile visible transits against arrivals at destination ports (Sikka, Vadinar, Ningbo, Qingdao, Ulsan), loadings at Fujairah and Yanbu, and exporter-country tanker export tonnage. Divergence between arrivals and visible transits is the dark-flow signal.*

**Current read:** The Iranian attack campaign is now measurably suppressing Gulf throughput: Kpler puts Hormuz at ~68% of normal, a third consecutive weekly fall (10.1 → 8.5 mb/d → 68%), and the AIS loading record agrees in direction — the four monitored Gulf terminals exported 1,622 kt against 5,648 kt in the same 2025 week, 29%. This contradicts Bloomberg's Oct 13 sourcing that loadings showed no clear change; I believe the AIS at the loading point and Kpler on level, with the gap being dark/STS tonnage. Confidence high; read changed 2026-10-16.
**Evidence log:**
- 2026-10-16: Kpler via ING — Hormuz oil flows ~68% of usual due to intensified Iranian attacks ([UNN/Reuters](https://unn.ua/en/amp/oil-prices-rise-due-to-the-situation-in-the-middle-east-not-ukraines-strikes-on-russian-oil-refineries-reuters)). PortWatch week to Oct 9, agent arithmetic: Ras Laffan 358 kt vs 1,433 kt 2025 (25%); Ras Tanura 313 vs 1,118 (28%); Jebel Ali 219 vs 1,964 (11%); Fujairah 732 vs 1,133 (65%); aggregate 1,622 vs 5,648 = 29%. Yanbu 22 kt on 1 tanker call vs 1,442 kt on 25 (1.5%). Hormuz AIS to Oct 11: 4.1 transits/day, 0.6 tankers, 24k DWT/day, −99% vs 2025, +49% w/w.
- 2026-10-15: Kpler Hormuz crude 8.5 mb/d in the week to Oct 12, ~40% below pre-war; 3 mb/d on Monday Oct 12 against CENTCOM's claim of >19m bbl the same day ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/14/container-ship-sinks-strait-hormuz-as-iran-war-attacks.html); [Anadolu](http://www.aa.com.tr/en/americas/us-rejects-iran-s-claim-of-control-over-strait-of-hormuz/4087883)). Agent arithmetic: AIS-visible tanker capacity is 2.1% of the Kpler figure.
- 2026-10-13: Bloomberg — attacks surging, more than a dozen VLCCs and several Suezmaxes turned around near Fujairah, longer STS waiting times, but "no clear evidence of change in oil loadings" from top producers ([Insurance Journal/Bloomberg](https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/international/2026/10/13/888757.htm)). **Contradicted by the Oct 16 terminal arithmetic.**
- 2026-10-08: Kpler prior week 10.1 mb/d, −27% w/w ([Aegis](https://aegis-hedging.com/insights/daily-first-look/2026-10-08)).
- 2026-10-03: G7 releases 100m bbl over four months (50m EU diesel + 50m IEA crude); Trump rules out a US diesel export ban ([Business Recorder/Reuters](https://www.brecorder.com/news/40442436/oil-settles-lower); [Sharjah24/AFP](https://sharjah24.ae/en/Articles/2026/10/03/Trump-says-rules-out-diesel-export-ban-after-European-release)). Agent arithmetic: 0.83 mb/d total, 0.42 mb/d product = 19% cover of the 2.2 mb/d distillate gap.
**Would change the read:** Kpler printing above 80% of normal, or the four-terminal export aggregate clearing 50% of its 2025 week.

## Q2. What is really happening inside Iran: goods, borders, rail, people?
*Below the social-media layer. Border crossings and truck traffic at Bazargan/Gürbulak (Turkey), Shalamcheh and Mehran (Iraq), Mirjaveh/Taftan (Pakistan), Islam Qala/Dogharoun (Afghanistan), Astara (Azerbaijan), Sarakhs/Incheh Borun (Turkmenistan). Port calls at Caspian ports (Anzali, Amirabad, Nowshahr) versus Gulf ports (Shahid Rajaee, Bushehr, Khomeini). Rail: INSTC, Iran–Turkey, Iran–Turkmenistan. People: outbound flows to Turkey, Armenia, Iraq, Pakistan; internal displacement; flight schedules at IKA and Mashhad.*

**Current read:** The northern pivot holds but is not scaling, and the land borders remain the binding constraint on marginal import capacity. The latest week narrows the Caspian lead sharply (32 calls vs 28 Gulf, from 47 vs 17) with Bandar Shahid Rajaee taking its first tanker calls of the series — one week on small absolute counts, not yet a reversal. Confidence medium-high; read unchanged since 2026-09-30.
**Evidence log:**
- 2026-10-04: Iran port-call split, week to Sep 19–25 — Caspian 32 vs Gulf 28 (2025: 14 vs 30). Bandar Shahid Rajaee 7 calls of which 4 tanker, exports 42 kt; Bandar Khomeini imports 117 kt; Chabahar imports 76 kt; Kharg 2 calls, 0 tanker, 0 kt exports. Caspian totals falling: Amirabad 14 calls (from 22), Anzali 13 (from 15), Nowshahr 5 (from 10) (PortWatch, agent arithmetic).
- 2026-10-02: Five Fatemiyoun militia members reported killed by roadside bomb and gunfire near Zahedan; IRGC confirms a bombing but acknowledges one death; Haalvsh reports hundreds of Fatemiyoun deployed around Zahedan, Mirjaveh, Rask, Saravan, Zabol, Chabahar, Konarak and Iranshahr ([Iran International](https://www.iranintl.com/en/202610028639)) — internal-security manpower substitution on the Pakistan-border corridor.
- 2026-09-30: Iran turning to Russia via the Caspian to bypass the blockade, but northern ports cannot replace southern gateways ([The Economy](https://economy.ac/news/2026/09/202609295753)) — corroborates the capacity-ceiling read.
- 2026-09-29: Iran's rail network reaches the borders at Shamtigh (Afghanistan), Mirjaveh (Pakistan) and Shalamcheh (Iraq); rail vulnerabilities documented in the north ([Iran International exclusive](https://exclusive.iranintl.com/iran-logistics-network)).
- 2026-09-20: Red tape and gridlock are clogging the land routes Iran is using to bypass the Hormuz blockade ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/09/20/iran-economy-blockade-gridlock-land-routes-trucking-us-navy-hormuz/)) — a capacity ceiling on the overland substitute.
**Would change the read:** A second consecutive week of Gulf port calls at or above Caspian, or truck-count data from Bazargan/Gürbulak, Astara or Mirjaveh.

Under the two-observation rule adopted Oct 15, the discounts are now signal rather than noise: households are not bidding for dollars or gold. Confidence high; read changed 2026-10-16.
**Evidence log:**

## Q4. What does the view from space say about Iranian industrial activity?
*Thermal anomalies (VIIRS/MODIS) at Assaluyeh/South Pars, Abadan, Bandar Abbas refinery, Isfahan, Arak, Kharg, Tabriz, Mobarakeh steel: flaring and furnace heat as a proxy for throughput; strike fires. Nighttime lights where reported. Commercial satellite imagery as reported by press and OSINT.*

**Current read:** Twenty-sixth consecutive run with no thermal feed, so industrial throughput stays unmeasured, but commercial imagery has moved the nuclear file: ISIS reads September–October imagery as showing Iran moving beyond rubble clearance toward *restoring operations* at the Minzadayi compound Israel struck on March 3, alongside the Oct 13 anti-infiltration earthworks at Natanz and Pickaxe Mountain. Kharg records a fifth consecutive week of zero exports. Confidence low on throughput, medium on the imagery reporting; read changed 2026-10-16.
**Evidence log:**
- 2026-10-16: FIRMS thermal anomalies not collected (FIRMS_MAP_KEY not set) — twenty-sixth run without a flaring or furnace proxy. Kharg Island week to Oct 9: 4 calls, 1 tanker call, **0 kt exports**, fifth consecutive zero week (PortWatch).
- 2026-10-10: ISIS, from high-resolution September and October imagery, reports repairs to a water tank and a new small building at the **Minzadayi** compound northeast of Tehran, continued excavation above underground structures, and passenger vehicles and trucks returning — suggesting Iran seeks "not just to clean up the site" but to resume regular activities; Israel identified the site as covert nuclear weapons development and struck it on March 3 ([Iran International](https://www.iranintl.com/en/202610109658)).
- 2026-10-13: ISIS/Vantor imagery — makeshift dirt barriers built last month around Natanz and Pickaxe Mountain to stop vehicles and personnel reaching deeply buried tunnel entrances ([WSJ](https://www.wsj.com/world/middle-east/iran-is-fortifying-nuclear-sites-and-rallying-its-people-for-a-return-to-fighting-c4cc3c28); [ISIS](https://isis-online.org/isis-reports/imagery-update-passive-defenses-identified-at-natanz-and-pickaxe-mountain)).
