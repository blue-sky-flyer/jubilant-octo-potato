# Open Questions — the analytical agenda

*Owner-defined. The daily agent updates the **Current read** and **Evidence log** of each question every run, never the question itself. New questions: owner adds them here. Retired questions: owner moves them to the bottom with a one-line resolution.*

**Rules for the agent:** For each question, state a current read with a confidence (low / medium / high), cite the newest dated evidence for and against, and name what would change the read. A question with no new evidence today keeps yesterday's read and its date. Never pad. The briefing leads with the two or three questions where the read moved.

---

## Q1. How much oil and product is actually leaving the Gulf, and by what route?
*AIS transit counts at Hormuz are a floor, not a measurement: escorted or dark transits do not appear. Reconcile visible transits against arrivals at destination ports (Sikka, Vadinar, Ningbo, Qingdao, Ulsan), loadings at Fujairah and Yanbu, and exporter-country tanker export tonnage. Divergence between arrivals and visible transits is the dark-flow signal.*

**Current read:** The export system has re-plumbed outside the strait: inside-Gulf terminal loadings collapsed 58% in the week to Nov 13 while Fujairah's exports rose 37% to 1,198 kt, above its own 2025 week — the bypass is now the primary loading point rather than an aggregation node. Hormuz AIS remains a floor (18k DWT/day tanker tonnage, −99% vs 2025). Confidence high; read changed 2026-11-18.
**Evidence log:**
- 2026-11-18: PortWatch week to Nov 13 — **Ras Tanura 0 kt exports (prior 261), Ras Laffan 108 (428), Jebel Ali 302 (277) = 410 kt vs 966, −58%**, and 2.4% of 2025's 3,375 kt; **Fujairah 1,198 kt on 39 tanker calls vs 874 kt prior week (+37%) and 986 kt in 2025**; Yanbu zero for a third week; Sikka/Jamnagar 14 calls / 409 kt vs 8 / 223 kt in 2025 (PortWatch, agent arithmetic).
- 2026-11-17: AD Ports and Gulftainer move **~3,500 TEU/day** inland from Fujairah, Khor Fakkan and Dibba, +30% within Q3; DP World has shifted **more than 1m TEU** east-coast to Jebel Ali since the war began; ships wait **~9 days for a berth off Fujairah plus 15 days to clear dockside** against 3 days at Duqm ([Maritime Executive Nov 15](https://maritime-executive.com/article/arabian-sea-ports-are-booming-as-the-conflict-drags-on)).
- 2026-11-16: PortWatch port endpoint — **Fujairah 40 tanker calls on 997 kt** in the week to Nov 6, against 68 calls in 2025; Hormuz daily detail shows **zero tanker transits on Nov 4** and a maximum of 2 on any day (PortWatch, agent arithmetic).
- 2026-11-15: Against UKMTO's ~65%-below-baseline (≈48/day) and Lloyd's List's ~20% (≈28/day), AIS-visible 3.9/day implies **86–92% of traffic dark or escorted**, against ~42% dark when CENTCOM last published a split on Jun 21 (PortWatch, agent arithmetic).
- 2026-10-21: Kpler — Gulf producers excluding Iran averaged **24.6 mb/d in the week to Oct 18 vs ~23.3 mb/d pre-war**, via Red Sea facilities and offshore STS ([Oil & Gas 360](https://www.oilandgas360.com/middle-east-oil-exports-recover-but-shipping-risks-keep-crude-markets-volatile/)); Maritime Executive describes a **"Hormuz Shuttle Service"** of short-haul tankers to Fujairah ([Maritime Executive](https://maritime-executive.com/article/report-despite-iran-s-attacks-gulf-oil-exports-exceed-prewar-levels)).
**Would change the read:** Fujairah exports falling back below ~900 kt in a week while inside-Gulf terminal loadings stay suppressed, or a Kpler print showing Gulf exports back below pre-war.

## Q2. What is really happening inside Iran: goods, borders, rail, people?
*Below the social-media layer. Border crossings and truck traffic at Bazargan/Gürbulak (Turkey), Shalamcheh and Mehran (Iraq), Mirjaveh/Taftan (Pakistan), Islam Qala/Dogharoun (Afghanistan), Astara (Azerbaijan), Sarakhs/Incheh Borun (Turkmenistan). Port calls at Caspian ports (Anzali, Amirabad, Nowshahr) versus Gulf ports (Shahid Rajaee, Bushehr, Khomeini). Rail: INSTC, Iran–Turkey, Iran–Turkmenistan. People: outbound flows to Turkey, Armenia, Iraq, Pakistan; internal displacement; flight schedules at IKA and Mashhad.*

**Current read:** The land blockade is biting through congestion rather than policy — neighbours are refusing US closure demands while the crossings jam or close themselves — and the maritime half has now inverted completely: Caspian port calls run 40 a week against 19 in the Gulf, the mirror image of 2025. Confidence medium-high; read unchanged since 2026-11-15, data extended 2026-11-16.
**Evidence log:**
- 2026-11-16: PortWatch week to Nov 6 — **Iran Caspian 40 calls vs Gulf 19** (2025: Caspian 23, Gulf 42); Nowshahr 13 calls vs 4 in 2025, Anzali 12 vs 8, Amirabad 15 vs 11; **Bandar Khomeini registered 0 calls and 0 kt imports** against 5 calls / 232 kt the prior week and 565 kt in 2025 (PortWatch, agent arithmetic). Fuel distribution failure continues: Bandar Abbas residents blocked a road near a Rajaei-district station over empty pumps; a vice-president says "we really have a supply problem" ([Iran International Nov 15](https://www.iranintl.com/en/202611156648)).
- 2026-11-15: Bazargan has gone from a five-day queue to **Iranian trucks waiting three weeks** to enter Turkey, one week inbound; Shib reopened after three weeks as Sumar shut indefinitely; Gabd-Rimdan, Taftan, Mand and Chagai closed or intermittent ([Maritime Executive Nov 13](https://maritime-executive.com/article/naval-blockade-fuels-mass-micro-smuggling-into-pakistan)). Agent arithmetic: 16 days of backlog added in five calendar days implies arrivals at ~4× throughput.
- 2026-11-13: TV7 reports Turkey, Pakistan, Iraq and other Iranian neighbours pushing back on American demands to close their border crossings ([TV7 Israel Nov 13](https://www.tv7israelnews.com/vod/series/563?episode-id=KrCfed6NALY)) — single carrier.
- 2026-11-11: ILNA reports workers in Tehran advertising kidneys, liver tissue and bone marrow for sale; a parliamentary committee chair says **about eight million people have fallen into absolute poverty** ([Iran International Nov 11](https://www.iranintl.com/en/202611118553)).
- 2026-11-07: CENTCOM establishes **Task Force Iron Gate** to disrupt land and Caspian transit, ~20 staff in Tampa ([Reuters Nov 7](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/expanding-naval-blockade-us-military-looks-restrict-iranian-land-commerce-2026-11-07)); the same investigation finds Iran imported **at least $2bn** in hardware since January 2025 via the Caspian, Central Asian trucking and Chinese freight aircraft.
**Would change the read:** A dated Bazargan truck count showing the queue clearing, or Iranian Caspian port calls falling back below ~25 a week.
