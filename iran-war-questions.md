# Open Questions — the analytical agenda

*Owner-defined. The daily agent updates the **Current read** and **Evidence log** of each question every run, never the question itself. New questions: owner adds them here. Retired questions: owner moves them to the bottom with a one-line resolution.*

**Rules for the agent:** For each question, state a current read with a confidence (low / medium / high), cite the newest dated evidence for and against, and name what would change the read. A question with no new evidence today keeps yesterday's read and its date. Never pad. The briefing leads with the two or three questions where the read moved.

## Q1. How much oil and product is actually leaving the Gulf, and by what route?
*AIS transit counts at Hormuz are a floor, not a measurement: escorted or dark transits do not appear. Reconcile visible transits against arrivals at destination ports (Sikka, Vadinar, Ningbo, Qingdao, Ulsan), loadings at Fujairah and Yanbu, and exporter-country tanker export tonnage. Divergence between arrivals and visible transits is the dark-flow signal.*

**Current read:** A Kpler-based series now puts crude clearing Hormuz at ~6.5 mb/d in September with ~2.5 mb/d transferred STS in the Gulf of Oman, which matches CENTCOM's ">900M bbl since early May" (≈6.7 mb/d) and definitively falsifies the "1bn in two months" version (16.4 mb/d). The AIS-visible share is therefore ~1.5% of actual flow, not the ~4% previously carried, and the strait's cost shows up in freight — $30/bbl MEG-China versus $20 outside Hormuz. Confidence high; read changed 2026-09-22.
**Evidence log:**
- 2026-09-22: Kpler via ShipUniverse — Hormuz crude ~6.5 mb/d in September, highest since the June ceasefire; ~2.5 mb/d STS in the Gulf of Oman, up from 1.4 mb/d in August (~40% of flow); 17 trackable commodity vessels crossed Sep 19–20 versus 37 the prior weekend; MEG–China VLCC freight ~$30/bbl versus ~$20 loading outside Hormuz ([ShipUniverse](https://www.shipuniverse.com/news/hormuz-shipping-remains-severely-constrained-as-costly-tanker-shuttle-system-takes-over)). Agent arithmetic: 13k DWT/day AIS-visible ≈ 0.1 mb/d laden-equivalent ≈ 1.5% of 6.5 mb/d.
- 2026-09-22: Breakwave — total Saudi crude exports just over 4 mb/d in September to date versus 2.4 mb/d in August; Yanbu 2.9 mb/d in early September (from 1.5 mb/d in August) then no observed loadings since Sep 16; Aramco reportedly suspends contracted supply to Indian refiners; tankers still crossing with AIS off ([Breakwave](https://www.breakwaveadvisors.com/insights/980026wetreportkju458-ook57-j4w3y)).
- 2026-09-21: Shuttle/STS mechanism documented — each STS off Sohar or Fujairah takes 24–40 hours, brokers estimate ~15% of the global VLCC fleet tied up ([EnergyNewsBeat/Bloomberg](https://energynewsbeat.co/crude-oil/the-global-oil-market-is-short-vlcc-tankers-and-needs-them-to-rebalance)). Iran's forces spokesman calls the CENTCOM claim "fabricated and ridiculous" ([WANA](https://wanaen.com/general-shekarchi-centcom-commanders-recent-claim-is-false-and-ridiculous)).
- 2026-09-21: Bloomberg reports Adm. Cooper's actual words — Hormuz oil and LNG in the past two weeks at "the highest level in six months," lanes clear of mines, over 2,000 commercial transits assisted, allies shipped "more than 1 billion barrels... in the last couple months" ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-19/hormuz-oil-shipments-hit-six-month-high-us-commander-says)).
- 2026-09-20: Hormuz week to Sep 13 — 5.3 transits/day, 1.4 tankers, 13k DWT/day; −99% vs 2025. Kharg Island zero tanker calls again (PortWatch). No new pull 2026-09-22 (API quota exceeded).
**Would change the read:** A Kpler or Vortexa print below 4 mb/d, or destination-port arrival data at Ningbo/Qingdao/Ulsan inconsistent with 6.5 mb/d of departures.

## Q2. What is really happening inside Iran: goods, borders, rail, people?
*Below the social-media layer. Border crossings and truck traffic at Bazargan/Gürbulak (Turkey), Shalamcheh and Mehran (Iraq), Mirjaveh/Taftan (Pakistan), Islam Qala/Dogharoun (Afghanistan), Astara (Azerbaijan), Sarakhs/Incheh Borun (Turkmenistan). Port calls at Caspian ports (Anzali, Amirabad, Nowshahr) versus Gulf ports (Shahid Rajaee, Bushehr, Khomeini). Rail: INSTC, Iran–Turkey, Iran–Turkmenistan. People: outbound flows to Turkey, Armenia, Iraq, Pakistan; internal displacement; flight schedules at IKA and Mashhad.*

**Current read:** The northern pivot is measurable and quantified — Caspian port calls (51) exceed Gulf calls (23), an exact inversion of 2025, with the Caspian share of visible seaborne imports up from 4% to 20% — but at 0.9 kt per call it offsets only about a fifth of the tonnage the Gulf lost and carries essentially no exports. The overland channel is now being reinforced institutionally (new Iran-Turkey crossings under negotiation) while the air channel is being severed by sanctions. Confidence medium-high; read reaffirmed 2026-09-22.
**Evidence log:**
- 2026-09-22: US Treasury announces secondary sanctions cutting Iranian airlines off from the global distribution system under Operation Economic Outcast ([JPost](https://www.jpost.com/middle-east/iran-news/2026-09-22/live-updates-909229)); Iranian civil ADS-B falls to 64 aircraft from 92 a week ago, −30% (OpenSky). IODA 11,271 reachable /24s, 0.7% above the six-day mean — no network stress.
- 2026-09-19: Iran and Turkey in talks in Ankara to activate two new border crossings and raise rail capacity, aimed at easing cargo flow from Turkish ports into Iran ([Adalytica](https://adalytica.com/news/iran-turkey-new-border-crossings-91874225)) — corroborates the overland-substitution thesis at the policy level; no truck counts.
- 2026-09-20: Iran port-call split, week to Sep 11 — Caspian 51 vs Gulf 23 (same week 2025: 22 vs 46). Caspian imports 46 kt on 51 calls vs Gulf 185 kt on 23 calls; total visible imports −35% YoY; Caspian gain offsets 19% of the Gulf loss (PortWatch, agent arithmetic).
- 2026-09-18: FT reports Iran switching from ships to trucks to evade the US blockade — single-sourced ([FT](https://www.ft.com/content/f69943f7-b173-444c-a79e-c9396a0380bd?syn-25a6b1a6=1)).
- 2026-09-17: Iran-Iraq trade crossings reopen after Iraq's Sep 12 drone-attack closures; Al-Sheeb shut pending a Sep 17 restart ([Streamline](https://streamlinefeed.co.ke/news/iran-iraq-trade-crossings-reopen-after-drone-attack-closures-al-sheeb-shut)).
**Would change the read:** Caspian import tonnage per call rising above ~3 kt, or truck-count data from Bazargan/Gürbulak, Astara or Mirjaveh.

## Q4. What does the view from space say about Iranian industrial activity?
*Thermal anomalies (VIIRS/MODIS) at Assaluyeh/South Pars, Abadan, Bandar Abbas refinery, Isfahan, Arak, Kharg, Tabriz, Mobarakeh steel: flaring and furnace heat as a proxy for throughput; strike fires. Nighttime lights where reported. Commercial satellite imagery as reported by press and OSINT.*

**Current read:** No thermal feed this run (FIRMS key unset), so the read rests on commercial imagery reporting: Iran is visibly reconstructing hardened nuclear-adjacent infrastructure at Parchin and fortifying Pickaxe Mountain, while Kharg shows zero tanker calls in the AIS record. Confidence low on industrial throughput, medium on the reconstruction reporting; read set 2026-09-19.
**Evidence log:**
- 2026-09-21: FIRMS thermal anomalies not collected for a third run (FIRMS_MAP_KEY not set) — no flaring or furnace proxy.
- 2026-09-19: Kharg Island records zero tanker calls for a third consecutive week; one non-tanker call (PortWatch).
- 2026-09-18: Vantor imagery from Sep 13, via the Institute for Science and International Security, shows Taleghan 2 at Parchin being rebuilt — tarp over the hardened buried facility, concrete caps over bunker-buster penetrations, dump trucks, bulldozers, concrete pump trucks and cranes across the site; work began in June ([Iran International](https://www.iranintl.com/en/202609178092); [JPost](https://www.jpost.com/middle-east/iran-news/article-909027)).
- 2026-09-16: CSIS imagery shows continued construction and fortification at Pickaxe Mountain; the IAEA said on Sep 10 it identified construction but cannot determine activity inside ([Alma](https://israel-alma.org/the-iranian-arena-monthly-review-august-september-2026)).
**Would change the read:** A restored FIRMS feed showing sustained thermal anomalies at Assaluyeh, Bandar Abbas or Mobarakeh, or new commercial imagery of refinery or petrochemical throughput.
