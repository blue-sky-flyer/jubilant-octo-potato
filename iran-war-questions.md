# Open Questions — the analytical agenda

*Owner-defined. The daily agent updates the **Current read** and **Evidence log** of each question every run, never the question itself. New questions: owner adds them here. Retired questions: owner moves them to the bottom with a one-line resolution.*

**Rules for the agent:** For each question, state a current read with a confidence (low / medium / high), cite the newest dated evidence for and against, and name what would change the read. A question with no new evidence today keeps yesterday's read and its date. Never pad. The briefing leads with the two or three questions where the read moved.

---

## Q1. How much oil and product is actually leaving the Gulf, and by what route?
*AIS transit counts at Hormuz are a floor, not a measurement: escorted or dark transits do not appear. Reconcile visible transits against arrivals at destination ports (Sikka, Vadinar, Ningbo, Qingdao, Ulsan), loadings at Fujairah and Yanbu, and exporter-country tanker export tonnage. Divergence between arrivals and visible transits is the dark-flow signal.*

**Current read:** The export system is re-plumbed outside the strait, but this week inverts the pattern: inside-Hormuz terminals restarted (Ras Tanura 109 kt from zero, Ras Laffan 212 from 30, Yanbu 109 from zero) while Fujairah fell 15% to 956 kt, and the Hormuz AIS tanker series went to numerical zero (1k DWT/day, −100% vs 2025) against Kpler's ~half-of-pre-war clearance. The AIS floor is now uninformative; use terminal tonnage. Confidence high on the artefact reading, medium-high on the terminal rotation; read changed 2026-11-27.
**Evidence log:**
- 2026-11-27: PortWatch week to Nov 20 — Hormuz **2.7 transits/day, 0.3 tankers, 1k DWT/day (−100% vs 2025, −94% w/w)**; Suez tanker DWT **715k/day, +4% vs 2025, +9% w/w** — the only chokepoint above its 2025 baseline. Inside-Gulf loadings: Ras Tanura 109 kt (prior 0), Ras Laffan 212 (30), Jebel Ali 190 (337); Fujairah **956 kt on 44 tanker calls, −15% w/w**; Yanbu 109 kt (prior 0). **Kharg Island 1 tanker call and 295 kt of exports** against zero prior week and zero in 2025 — flagged, not adopted (PortWatch, agent arithmetic).
- 2026-11-25: Kpler via marketcalls — AIS-visible Hormuz traffic ~**9% of pre-crisis**, oil clearance ~**half of pre-war**; October Hormuz crude exports averaged **11.1 mb/d, −2% vs September**, despite 30+ vessel attacks; late-October week to Oct 28 fell to **7.3 mb/d** ([marketcalls Nov 25](https://www.marketcalls.in/commodities/strait-of-hormuz-november-2026.html); [Gulf News Nov 24](https://gulfnews.com/business/energy/brent-drops-to-88-wti-at-79-why-oil-is-sliding-despite-irans-tanker-attacks-1.500758802)).
- 2026-11-23: PortWatch week to Nov 15 — Hormuz **2.3 transits/day (prior 3.9)**, tankers **0.4/day (prior 1.0)** but tanker DWT **18k/day, +476% w/w**: fewer, larger hulls. Against UKMTO's 263 transits in the week to Nov 18, AIS visibility is **0.9%** ([Maritime Executive Nov 23](https://maritime-executive.com/article/two-more-tankers-hit-off-oman-as-houthis-threaten-saudi-red-sea-ports); PortWatch, agent arithmetic).
- 2026-11-22: Kpler via Bloomberg — Hormuz oil flows **13.4 → ~10.0 mb/d, Nov 1–10 (−25%)**; MEG VLCC loadings 7.4 mb/d in the first half of November vs 9.3 across October; Banchero Costa puts Arabian Gulf crude shipments −27.6% y/y Jan–Oct ([House of Saud Nov 21](https://houseofsaud.com/hormuz-oil-volumes-fall-november-2026)).
- 2026-11-19: Fujairah bunker fuel-oil imports **103k bbl/d in October against 218k in February**; August bunker sales **−65% y/y**; recovery to pre-war sales not expected before 4Q 2027 ([EnterpriseAM Nov 19](https://enterpriseam.com/infrastructure/2026/11/19/fujairah-has-found-new-fuel-for-its-bunker-trade-but-winning-back-the-ships-that-refuelled-there-could-take-years)).
**Would change the read:** A second consecutive Kharg export print, or Ras Tanura returning to zero while Fujairah stays below 1,000 kt.

## Q2. What is really happening inside Iran: goods, borders, rail, people?
*Below the social-media layer. Border crossings and truck traffic at Bazargan/Gürbulak (Turkey), Shalamcheh and Mehran (Iraq), Mirjaveh/Taftan (Pakistan), Islam Qala/Dogharoun (Afghanistan), Astara (Azerbaijan), Sarakhs/Incheh Borun (Turkmenistan). Port calls at Caspian ports (Anzali, Amirabad, Nowshahr) versus Gulf ports (Shahid Rajaee, Bushehr, Khomeini). Rail: INSTC, Iran–Turkey, Iran–Turkmenistan. People: outbound flows to Turkey, Armenia, Iraq, Pakistan; internal displacement; flight schedules at IKA and Mashhad.*

**Current read:** The government now publicly claims the land corridor is compensating for the blockade, and the data contradicts it: Caspian calls (46) exceed Gulf calls (21) but carry ~44 kt against the Gulf's 231 kt, queues run to 24 days at Gürbulak and 26 miles at Yalama, and the truckers' strike is at 20 cities on day six. Throughput is falling while call counts rise. Confidence medium-high; read changed 2026-11-27.
**Evidence log:**
