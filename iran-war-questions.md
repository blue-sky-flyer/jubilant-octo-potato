# Open Questions — the analytical agenda

*Owner-defined. The daily agent updates the **Current read** and **Evidence log** of each question every run, never the question itself. New questions: owner adds them here. Retired questions: owner moves them to the bottom with a one-line resolution.*

**Rules for the agent:** For each question, state a current read with a confidence (low / medium / high), cite the newest dated evidence for and against, and name what would change the read. A question with no new evidence today keeps yesterday's read and its date. Never pad. The briefing leads with the two or three questions where the read moved.

## Q1. How much oil and product is actually leaving the Gulf, and by what route?
*AIS transit counts at Hormuz are a floor, not a measurement: escorted or dark transits do not appear. Reconcile visible transits against arrivals at destination ports (Sikka, Vadinar, Ningbo, Qingdao, Ulsan), loadings at Fujairah and Yanbu, and exporter-country tanker export tonnage. Divergence between arrivals and visible transits is the dark-flow signal.*

**Current read:** Gulf throughput is suppressed to roughly a quarter to two-thirds of normal depending on the instrument, and the Saudi Red Sea bypass is offline rather than absorbing the diversion. The marginal price-setter is no longer Gulf supply: Brent fell 1.3–1.8% on Oct 19 on a Russian diesel waiver, which sizes the distillate leg of the war premium at roughly $1.50/bbl per non-Gulf supply event. Confidence medium-high; read changed 2026-10-19.
**Evidence log:**
- 2026-10-19: Brent $107.88–108.43, WTI $100.25–100.88, both down 1.3–1.8% after Trump waived Graham Act sanctions and tariffs to admit Russian diesel; Brent had settled at $109.86 on Oct 16, the highest since May 19 ([Nation Thailand/Reuters](https://www.nationthailand.com/news/world/40072461); [PUKmedia/Reuters](https://www.pukmedia.com/EN/Details/83161)). Qatar's deputy PM at Doha: energy shockwaves "have not reached the peak" ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/19/qatar-warns-global-energy-shockwaves-from-iran-war-have-yet-to-peak)).
- 2026-10-18: Misbar, from MarineTraffic records on Bahri hulls, reports East–West pipeline pumping halted days after the Sep 10 attack, Yanbu loadings suspended, and Saudi crude shifted to Gulf ports with AIS gaps and probable STS ([Misbar](https://www.misbar.com/en/editorial/2026/10/16/after-east%E2%80%93west-pipeline-attack-misbar-tracks-saudi-tankers-moving-oil-covertly-through-hormuz)); Reuters counts at least 135 drone and missile attacks on at least 84 Gulf infrastructure sites since Feb 28, including Fujairah routing systems and two Ras Laffan cold boxes ([BOE Report/Reuters Oct 15](https://boereport.com/2026/10/15/irans-precision-strikes-picked-apart-gulf-infrastructure-blunting-us-war-plans/)).

**Would change the read:** A dated Saudi confirmation that East–West pumping has restarted, or a second non-Gulf supply event that fails to move Brent.

## Q2. What is really happening inside Iran: goods, borders, rail, people?
*Below the social-media layer. Border crossings and truck traffic at Bazargan/Gürbulak (Turkey), Shalamcheh and Mehran (Iraq), Mirjaveh/Taftan (Pakistan), Islam Qala/Dogharoun (Afghanistan), Astara (Azerbaijan), Sarakhs/Incheh Borun (Turkmenistan). Port calls at Caspian ports (Anzali, Amirabad, Nowshahr) versus Gulf ports (Shahid Rajaee, Bushehr, Khomeini). Rail: INSTC, Iran–Turkey, Iran–Turkmenistan. People: outbound flows to Turkey, Armenia, Iraq, Pakistan; internal displacement; flight schedules at IKA and Mashhad.*

**Current read:** The overland substitute is now being constrained from the outside, not just by congestion: Pakistan suspended commercial traffic at Taftan–Mirjaveh and Gabd–Rimdan on Oct 18, stranding hundreds of trucks, with the first measurable cost landing on Pakistani LPG supply. The northern pivot has meanwhile reversed — PortWatch shows Gulf 40 calls vs Caspian 25, with Bandar Shahid Rajaee at 257 kt of exports. Confidence medium-high; read changed 2026-10-19.
**Evidence log:**
- 2026-10-18: Pakistan suspends commercial operations at **Taftan–Mirjaveh and Gabd–Rimdan**, citing security and smuggling; hundreds of freight vehicles stranded, traders halt fresh orders, LPG supply and price risk flagged inside Pakistan; Tehran cites "technical problems" ([Firstpost](https://www.firstpost.com/world/pakistan-suspends-trade-at-two-iran-border-crossings-over-security-smuggling-concerns-ws-e-14053670.html); [Telegraph India/PTI](https://www.telegraphindia.com/world/pakistan-shuts-iran-trade-crossings-over-security-concerns-stranding-trucks-and-risking-lpg-supply/cid/2185325); [India Today](https://www.indiatoday.in/world/story/pakistan-iran-border-trade-suspension-freight-vehicles-lpg-supplies-3020286-2026-10-18)). **Would change the read:** A dated reopening of Taftan–Mirjaveh, or truck-count data from Bazargan/Gürbulak or Astara.
