# Open Questions — the analytical agenda

*Owner-defined. The daily agent updates the **Current read** and **Evidence log** of each question every run, never the question itself. New questions: owner adds them here. Retired questions: owner moves them to the bottom with a one-line resolution.*

**Rules for the agent:** For each question, state a current read with a confidence (low / medium / high), cite the newest dated evidence for and against, and name what would change the read. A question with no new evidence today keeps yesterday's read and its date. Never pad. The briefing leads with the two or three questions where the read moved.

---

## Q1. How much oil and product is actually leaving the Gulf, and by what route?
*AIS transit counts at Hormuz are a floor, not a measurement: escorted or dark transits do not appear. Reconcile visible transits against arrivals at destination ports (Sikka, Vadinar, Ningbo, Qingdao, Ulsan), loadings at Fujairah and Yanbu, and exporter-country tanker export tonnage. Divergence between arrivals and visible transits is the dark-flow signal.*

**Current read:** Crude is solved and products are not: Kpler/JPMorgan put Gulf crude at 16.5 mb/d (98% of a 16.8 mb/d base, or 87% of a 19 mb/d base) while Middle East refined-product exports run ~3 mb/d, about 58% of pre-conflict, leaving a ~2.2 mb/d distillate gap that the G7's 100m-bbl release covers by only 19%. The visible AIS series remains a behavioural indicator — 18k DWT/day, 0.8% of normal, confirmed by two independent collectors. Confidence high; read changed 2026-10-03.
**Evidence log:**
- 2026-10-04: Hormuz week to Sep 27 — 3.1 transits/day, 1.3 tankers, 18k DWT/day; tanker DWT −99% vs 2025 but +1,152% w/w (nine tanker transits vs four). Ras Laffan exports 243 kt on 6 tanker calls (13% of the 2025 week); Jebel Ali 308 kt (20%); Ras Tanura 155 kt (12.5%); Fujairah 887 kt on 33 tanker calls = 26.9 kt/call vs 17.9 in 2025 (PortWatch, agent arithmetic). Independent AIS collector agrees: 16 tanker crossings, 0.8% of normal tonnage ([hormuz.now](https://hormuz.now/oil)).
- 2026-10-03: G7 agrees to release 100m bbl of crude and fuel over four months (50m EU diesel + 50m IEA crude), part of the diesel inside 20 days; Trump rules out a US diesel export ban. Brent settled $102.25 (−0.06%), WTI $91.11 (−1.90%); Brent-WTI widened $9.44 → $11.14 ([Business Recorder/Reuters](https://www.brecorder.com/news/40442436/oil-settles-lower); [Sharjah24/AFP](https://sharjah24.ae/en/Articles/2026/10/03/Trump-says-rules-out-diesel-export-ban-after-European-release)). Agent arithmetic: 0.83 mb/d total, 0.42 mb/d product = 19% cover of the 2.2 mb/d gap.
- 2026-10-01: Kpler 16.5 mb/d Gulf exports in September, >70% ending in STS; Saudi crude 6.4 mb/d; JPMorgan 98% of pre-war; Iraqi exporters selling up to $37/bbl below benchmark ([Maritime Executive](https://maritime-executive.com/article/gulf-oil-exports-rebound-to-prewar-levels-despite-iranian-attacks)). Crude through Hormuz 13.5 mb/d 7-day average; products 677,000 b/d vs 3.6 mb/d pre-war ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/30/iran-war-strait-hormuz-gulf-oil-fuel.html)).
- 2026-09-30: Analyst survey of 30 forecasters — Brent to average $89.05 in 2026, WTI $83.90, range $77.27–$97.60; HSBC assumes a "structurally impaired" Hormuz; Goldman puts Gulf exports including dark at 23.3 mb/d ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/analysts-raise-2026-oil-forecasts-prolonged-gulf-disruption-2026-09-30)).
- 2026-09-29: Kpler — Hormuz crude including Gulf-of-Oman STS at 9 mb/d, up from a 2.2 mb/d late-July low; Middle East crude exports just under 80% of pre-conflict. ADNOC/KPC naphtha to Asia 1.6 Mt in Aug–Sep vs ~5 Mt in 2025 ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/uae-kuwait-boost-naphtha-exports-asia-sidestepping-hormuz-sources-say-2026-09-28)).
**Would change the read:** A second reserve tranche moving Brent more than 3%, or Middle East product exports printing above 4 mb/d.

## Q2. What is really happening inside Iran: goods, borders, rail, people?
*Below the social-media layer. Border crossings and truck traffic at Bazargan/Gürbulak (Turkey), Shalamcheh and Mehran (Iraq), Mirjaveh/Taftan (Pakistan), Islam Qala/Dogharoun (Afghanistan), Astara (Azerbaijan), Sarakhs/Incheh Borun (Turkmenistan). Port calls at Caspian ports (Anzali, Amirabad, Nowshahr) versus Gulf ports (Shahid Rajaee, Bushehr, Khomeini). Rail: INSTC, Iran–Turkey, Iran–Turkmenistan. People: outbound flows to Turkey, Armenia, Iraq, Pakistan; internal displacement; flight schedules at IKA and Mashhad.*

**Current read:** The northern pivot holds but is not scaling, and the land borders remain the binding constraint on marginal import capacity. The latest week narrows the Caspian lead sharply (32 calls vs 28 Gulf, from 47 vs 17) with Bandar Shahid Rajaee taking its first tanker calls of the series — one week on small absolute counts, not yet a reversal. Confidence medium-high; read unchanged since 2026-09-30.
**Evidence log:**
- 2026-10-04: Iran port-call split, week to Sep 19–25 — Caspian 32 vs Gulf 28 (2025: 14 vs 30). Bandar Shahid Rajaee 7 calls of which 4 tanker, exports 42 kt; Bandar Khomeini imports 117 kt; Chabahar imports 76 kt; Kharg 2 calls, 0 tanker, 0 kt exports. Caspian totals falling: Amirabad 14 calls (from 22), Anzali 13 (from 15), Nowshahr 5 (from 10) (PortWatch, agent arithmetic).
- 2026-10-02: Five Fatemiyoun militia members reported killed by roadside bomb and gunfire near Zahedan; IRGC confirms a bombing but acknowledges one death; Haalvsh reports hundreds of Fatemiyoun deployed around Zahedan, Mirjaveh, Rask, Saravan, Zabol, Chabahar, Konarak and Iranshahr ([Iran International](https://www.iranintl.com/en/202610028639)) — internal-security manpower substitution on the Pakistan-border corridor.
- 2026-09-30: Iran turning to Russia via the Caspian to bypass the blockade, but northern ports cannot replace southern gateways ([The Economy](https://economy.ac/news/2026/09/202609295753)) — corroborates the capacity-ceiling read.
- 2026-09-29: Iran's rail network reaches the borders at Shamtigh (Afghanistan), Mirjaveh (Pakistan) and Shalamcheh (Iraq); rail vulnerabilities documented in the north ([Iran International exclusive](https://exclusive.iranintl.com/iran-logistics-network)).
- 2026-09-20: Red tape and gridlock are clogging the land routes Iran is using to bypass the Hormuz blockade ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/09/20/iran-economy-blockade-gridlock-land-routes-trucking-us-navy-hormuz/)) — a capacity ceiling on the overland substitute.
**Would change the read:** A second consecutive week of Gulf port calls at or above Caspian, or truck-count data from Bazargan/Gürbulak, Astara or Mirjaveh.

## Q3. What are capital flows telling us?
*Open-market rial, Tether premium on Iranian exchanges, Tehran gold coin price, crypto exchange volumes and Tether freezes, Iranian property purchases in Turkey and Dubai, Armenian and Georgian banking, hawala reports. Direction and speed of capital flight is the regime-confidence indicator that no statement can fake.*

**Current read:** This is a supply-side FX collapse, not capital flight: the rial is 13% weaker on the week at 2,664,950 and has roughly doubled year-on-year, yet Tether sits at +0.4% over cash and Tehran gold prints ~$4,101/oz against a higher world ounce. Fortune supplies the mechanism — the regime is running out of oil to sell and cannot collect from the customers it has — which removes the offer side of the dollar book without any household liquidation. Confidence medium-high; read changed 2026-10-04.
**Evidence log:**
- 2026-10-04: Rial 2,664,950 IRR/USD, +13% vs Sep 27; Tether 2,674,960 = +0.4% over cash (vs +0.33% Oct 3); Emami 2,737M = $1,027.0, +0.9% in dollars; 18k 263.5M → ~$4,101/oz fine (tgju, agent arithmetic). Independent bracket: NCRI logs 2,685,000 on Oct 3 and 2,630,000 on Oct 2 ([NCRI](https://www.ncr-iran.org/en/news/iran-protests/rial-hits-268500-as-lawmaker-says-drug-crisis-is-killing-patients-and-gas-deliveries-fall)).
- 2026-10-03: Fortune — Iran's currency is collapsing because the regime is about to run out of oil to sell and cannot get paid by the customers it has ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/10/03/iran-currency-rial-dollar-record-low-blockade-oil-revenue-payments)). Mechanism for the supply-side reading.
- 2026-10-03: Euro crosses 3,000,000 rials for the first time; pound ~3.52m, dirham above 730,000; dollar +8% in under a week from ~2.45m and more than doubled from ~1.1m a year ago; official September CPI +89.8% y/y, 12-month average 73.6% ([Iran International](https://www.iranintl.com/en/202610032594)).
- 2026-10-01: The "trustee" ecosystem — intermediaries, companies, currency dealers and politically connected businesspeople entrusted with selling petroleum and repatriating proceeds — is now discussed publicly inside Iran ([Amwaj Media](https://amwaj.media/en/article/who-are-irans-trustees-really-allied-to)).

**Would change the read:** Tether sustained more than 3% over the cash dollar, or a Tehran gold-coin premium above 10%.
