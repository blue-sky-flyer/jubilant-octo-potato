# Open Questions — the analytical agenda

*Owner-defined. The daily agent updates the **Current read** and **Evidence log** of each question every run, never the question itself. New questions: owner adds them here. Retired questions: owner moves them to the bottom with a one-line resolution.*

**Rules for the agent:** For each question, state a current read with a confidence (low / medium / high), cite the newest dated evidence for and against, and name what would change the read. A question with no new evidence today keeps yesterday's read and its date. Never pad. The briefing leads with the two or three questions where the read moved.

## Q1. How much oil and product is actually leaving the Gulf, and by what route?
*AIS transit counts at Hormuz are a floor, not a measurement: escorted or dark transits do not appear. Reconcile visible transits against arrivals at destination ports (Sikka, Vadinar, Ningbo, Qingdao, Ulsan), loadings at Fujairah and Yanbu, and exporter-country tanker export tonnage. Divergence between arrivals and visible transits is the dark-flow signal.*

**Current read:** The migration out of the strait is complete: AIS-visible Hormuz tanker tonnage has fallen to 1k DWT/day — 0.1% of Kpler's 6.5 mb/d — while Fujairah has returned to within 4% of 2025 export tonnage using half the tanker calls at double the parcel size. Destination data corroborates (Sikka 16 tanker calls vs 4 in 2025; Gulf-to-India back to 1.52 mb/d), and the IEA's 7.6 mb/d August total-oil figure brackets the crude estimate from above. Confidence high; read changed 2026-09-23.
**Evidence log:**
- 2026-09-23: Hormuz week to Sep 20 — 3.1 transits/day, 0.6 tankers, 1k DWT/day; tanker DWT −100% vs 2025, −89% w/w. Fujairah exports 1,063 kt (+46% w/w, −4% vs 2025) on 33 tanker calls vs 71 in 2025 → 32.2 kt/call vs 15.6. Ras Laffan exports 81 kt vs 1,233 kt in 2025 (−93%). Sikka/Jamnagar 16 tanker calls, 494 kt exports vs 124 kt in 2025 (PortWatch, agent arithmetic).
- 2026-09-23: Kpler — 3 mb/d of rerouted Saudi crude requires 36–40 additional VLCCs (25 if lightering stays in the Gulf, up to 58 via Malaysia); Gulf of Oman STS at record levels and near capacity ([Kpler](https://www.kpler.com/blog/saudi-export-rerouting-amid-gulf-of-oman-sts-bottlenecks-amplify-vlcc-intensity-of-meg-flows)). Gulf crude to India 1.52 mb/d in September vs 1.03 mb/d in June, led by Iraq, UAE and Kuwait ([NDTV Profit/Kpler](https://www.ndtvprofit.com/markets/exclusive-gulf-oil-supplies-to-india-surge-to-1-52-mb-d-as-sellers-absorb-transit-risks-12075786)). VLCC AG–Asia rates broke $1m/day ([Seatrade](https://www.seatrade-maritime.com/tankers/trafigura-launches-14-ship-vlcc-venture-into-record-market)).
- 2026-09-23: IEA put August Hormuz oil flows at 7.6 mb/d, 13.1 mb/d below pre-war, cumulative losses approaching 2.8bn bbl; White House told AFP 10–11 mb/d; Energy Secretary Wright ~10 mb/d on Sep 13 ([EnergyNewsBeat](https://energynewsbeat.co/crude-oil/hormuz-oil-shipments-hit-six-month-high-states-us-commander)).
- 2026-09-22: Kpler via ShipUniverse — Hormuz crude ~6.5 mb/d in September; ~2.5 mb/d STS in the Gulf of Oman, up from 1.4 mb/d in August; 17 trackable vessels crossed Sep 19–20 vs 37 the prior weekend; MEG–China VLCC freight ~$30/bbl vs ~$20 outside Hormuz ([ShipUniverse](https://www.shipuniverse.com/news/hormuz-shipping-remains-severely-constrained-as-costly-tanker-shuttle-system-takes-over)).
- 2026-09-22: Breakwave — total Saudi crude exports just over 4 mb/d in September to date versus 2.4 mb/d in August; Aramco reportedly suspends contracted supply to Indian refiners; tankers still crossing with AIS off ([Breakwave](https://www.breakwaveadvisors.com/insights/980026wetreportkju458-ook57-j4w3y)).
**Would change the read:** Fujairah weekly export tonnage falling below ~700 kt, or a Kpler/Vortexa Hormuz print below 4 mb/d.

## Q2. What is really happening inside Iran: goods, borders, rail, people?
*Below the social-media layer. Border crossings and truck traffic at Bazargan/Gürbulak (Turkey), Shalamcheh and Mehran (Iraq), Mirjaveh/Taftan (Pakistan), Islam Qala/Dogharoun (Afghanistan), Astara (Azerbaijan), Sarakhs/Incheh Borun (Turkmenistan). Port calls at Caspian ports (Anzali, Amirabad, Nowshahr) versus Gulf ports (Shahid Rajaee, Bushehr, Khomeini). Rail: INSTC, Iran–Turkey, Iran–Turkmenistan. People: outbound flows to Turkey, Armenia, Iraq, Pakistan; internal displacement; flight schedules at IKA and Mashhad.*

**Current read:** The northern pivot holds but is not scaling: Caspian calls 47 vs Gulf 17 with the efficiency ratio stuck at 0.9 kt/call for a fourth week, while total visible seaborne imports fell another 24% to 176 kt. The Caspian is gaining share by default, not by capacity, so Iran's marginal import capacity is now set at the land borders — where Fortune reports red tape and gridlock are the binding constraint, and where new Iran-Turkey crossings are under negotiation. Confidence medium-high; read reaffirmed 2026-09-23.
**Evidence log:**
- 2026-09-23: Iran port-call split, week to Sep 18 — Caspian 47 vs Gulf 17 (2025: 15 vs 44). Caspian imports 43 kt on 47 calls (0.91 kt/call) vs Gulf 133 kt on 17 calls (7.8 kt/call); total visible imports 176 kt, −24% w/w; Caspian share 24%. Zero tanker calls at every Iranian Gulf port; Kharg zero for a fourth week (PortWatch, agent arithmetic).
- 2026-09-23: Iran cancels Baghdad and Muscat flights as US aviation sanctions take effect ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/22/iran-flights-to-baghdad-muscat-cancelled-ahead-of-us-aviation-sanctions)); Turkish Airlines suspends all Iran flights until March 2027 ([Iran International](https://www.iranintl.com/en/202609212495)); travellers shift to overland routes via Turkey and Armenia ([bne IntelliNews](https://www.intellinews.com/iran-travellers-turn-to-turkey-armenia-overland-routes-as-sanctions-cut-air-links-469355?source=iran)). **Would change the read:** Caspian import tonnage rising above ~3 kt per call, or truck-count data from Bazargan/Gürbulak, Astara or Mirjaveh.
