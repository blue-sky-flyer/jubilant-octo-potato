# Open Questions — the analytical agenda

*Owner-defined. The daily agent updates the **Current read** and **Evidence log** of each question every run, never the question itself. New questions: owner adds them here. Retired questions: owner moves them to the bottom with a one-line resolution.*

**Rules for the agent:** For each question, state a current read with a confidence (low / medium / high), cite the newest dated evidence for and against, and name what would change the read. A question with no new evidence today keeps yesterday's read and its date. Never pad. The briefing leads with the two or three questions where the read moved.

## Q1. How much oil and product is actually leaving the Gulf, and by what route?
*AIS transit counts at Hormuz are a floor, not a measurement: escorted or dark transits do not appear. Reconcile visible transits against arrivals at destination ports (Sikka, Vadinar, Ningbo, Qingdao, Ulsan), loadings at Fujairah and Yanbu, and exporter-country tanker export tonnage. Divergence between arrivals and visible transits is the dark-flow signal.*

**Current read:** The ~4% AIS-visible share holds after replacing the single-sourced 2.8 mb/d satellite claim with Al Jazeera's loadings series: Saudi ~2.1 mb/d ≈ 320k DWT/day laden against PortWatch's 13k DWT/day. CENTCOM's "1 billion barrels in two months" (16.4 mb/d) is not a flow measurement and contradicts its own Sep 16 figure of >900M since early May (~6.5 mb/d). Confidence medium-high on the 4%, high that the CENTCOM tally cannot be read as throughput; read changed 2026-09-20.
**Evidence log:**
- 2026-09-20: CENTCOM tells AP it has aided transit of 1bn barrels through Hormuz in two months while Iran "exported zero" — implies 16.4 mb/d, ~4/5 of pre-war throughput, irreconcilable with the IEA's 5.7 mb/d supply gap and Brent above $100 ([Fortune/AP](https://fortune.com/2026/09/19/central-command-us-military-transit-1-billion-barrels-oil-hormuz-iran-exports-naval-blockade)).
- 2026-09-20: Hormuz week to Sep 13 revised — 5.3 transits/day, 1.4 tankers, 13k DWT/day; −99% vs 2025, −45% w/w. Kharg Island zero tanker calls again, the one element supporting CENTCOM's "exported zero" (PortWatch).
- 2026-09-17: Saudi total crude loadings 7.5 mb/d Jan–Feb → ~2.3 mb/d August → ~2.1 mb/d first half of September (−72%); rerouting via dark shipments and ship-to-ship transfers off Oman; analysts warn dark shuttle tankers are undercounted ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/17/from-yanbu-to-sohar-tracking-saudi-arabias-alternative-oil-routes)).
- 2026-09-18: IRGC-struck vessel identified as Togo-flagged product tanker *Trend*, 7,119 dwt; IMO has verified 80 attacks on merchant vessels in/around Hormuz since Feb 28 ([Splash247](https://splash247.com/iran-claims-fresh-hormuz-tanker-strike-italy-readies-red-sea-escorts)).
- 2026-09-16: Reuters vessel data — four Hormuz transits on Tuesday versus seven the day before, against a 10-day average of 18; no VLCCs or LNG carriers; ships without transponders not counted ([MarineLink](https://www.marinelink.com/blogs/blog/data-shows-that-the-number-of-ships-crossing-the-strait-of-hormuz-105506)).
**Would change the read:** A Kpler or Vortexa laden-departure series for the Gulf, or a CENTCOM vessel-by-vessel escort tally reconcilable to destination-port arrivals.

## Q2. What is really happening inside Iran: goods, borders, rail, people?
*Below the social-media layer. Border crossings and truck traffic at Bazargan/Gürbulak (Turkey), Shalamcheh and Mehran (Iraq), Mirjaveh/Taftan (Pakistan), Islam Qala/Dogharoun (Afghanistan), Astara (Azerbaijan), Sarakhs/Incheh Borun (Turkmenistan). Port calls at Caspian ports (Anzali, Amirabad, Nowshahr) versus Gulf ports (Shahid Rajaee, Bushehr, Khomeini). Rail: INSTC, Iran–Turkey, Iran–Turkmenistan. People: outbound flows to Turkey, Armenia, Iraq, Pakistan; internal displacement; flight schedules at IKA and Mashhad.*

**Current read:** The northern pivot is now measurable and quantified: Caspian port calls (51) exceed Gulf calls (23), an exact inversion of 2025, and the Caspian share of visible seaborne imports has gone from 4% to 20% — but at 0.9 kt per call it offsets only about a fifth of the tonnage the Gulf lost and carries essentially no exports. The state remains functional. Confidence medium-high; read changed 2026-09-20.
**Evidence log:**
- 2026-09-20: Iran port-call split, week to Sep 11 — Caspian 51 vs Gulf 23 (same week 2025: 22 vs 46). Caspian imports 46 kt on 51 calls vs Gulf 185 kt on 23 calls; total visible imports −35% YoY; Caspian gain offsets 19% of the Gulf loss; six Caspian tanker calls vs one in 2025 (PortWatch, agent arithmetic).
- 2026-09-20: IODA 10,980 reachable /24s, 1.3% below the prior six-day mean (24h min 10,660); BGP prefixes 41,681, flat. OpenSky shows 83 Iranian aircraft versus 92 a week ago (−10%) — neither at a stress threshold.
- 2026-09-18: FT reports Iran switching from ships to trucks to evade the US blockade — single-sourced ([FT](https://www.ft.com/content/f69943f7-b173-444c-a79e-c9396a0380bd?syn-25a6b1a6=1)).
- 2026-09-17: Iran-Iraq trade crossings reopen after Iraq's Sep 12 drone-attack closures; Al-Sheeb shut pending a Sep 17 restart ([Streamline](https://streamlinefeed.co.ke/news/iran-iraq-trade-crossings-reopen-after-drone-attack-closures-al-sheeb-shut)).
- 2026-09-16: Security forces deployed as shops struck in western Iran on the Mahsa Amini anniversary ([Iran International](https://www.iranintl.com/en/202609167981)).
**Would change the read:** Caspian import tonnage per call rising above ~3 kt, or truck-count data from Bazargan/Gürbulak, Astara or Mirjaveh.

## Q3. What are capital flows telling us?
*Open-market rial, Tether premium on Iranian exchanges, Tehran gold coin price, crypto exchange volumes and Tether freezes, Iranian property purchases in Turkey and Dubai, Armenian and Georgian banking, hawala reports. Direction and speed of capital flight is the regime-confidence indicator that no statement can fake.*

**Current read:** This is a managed bleed, not a run. The Tehran gold-coin premium is approximately zero and Tether trades at a small discount to the cash dollar, so the entire adjustment is running through the exchange rate rather than a hard-asset scramble; the inference is that households liquidating gold to fund consumption are absorbing flight demand. Confidence medium; read set 2026-09-19.
**Evidence log:**
- 2026-09-20: Rial 2,302,750 IRR/USD, 1% weaker day-on-day — the first move off two flat sessions; Tether 2,286,470 (−0.7% vs cash dollar); Emami coin 2,375M IRR; 18k gram 238.8M IRR (tgju).
- 2026-09-20: Agent arithmetic — Emami coin ≈ $1,031 versus ~$1,014 intrinsic: premium ≈ 1.7%, up from ~0%. The coin rose 2.4% in rial against 1.0% currency depreciation — first hard-asset outperformance in the series, still an order of magnitude below stress levels.
- 2026-09-19: Agent arithmetic — coin premium ≈ 0%; 18k gram at a slight discount to world gold (tgju).
- 2026-09-18: US Treasury says a Tehran crypto exchange handled part of Iran's $2M Hormuz passage fees ([eGamers/Treasury](https://egamers.io/treasury-tehran-crypto-exchange-handled-part-of-irans-2-million-hormuz-passage-fees)).
- 2026-09-13: Monthly base wage 166.25M rials (~$72) against private-school tuition of 4–10bn rials — five years of base wages ([Iran International](https://www.iranintl.com/en/202609139683)).
**Would change the read:** A Tehran gold-coin premium above 10%, or Tether sustained more than 3% over the cash dollar.

## Q4. What does the view from space say about Iranian industrial activity?
*Thermal anomalies (VIIRS/MODIS) at Assaluyeh/South Pars, Abadan, Bandar Abbas refinery, Isfahan, Arak, Kharg, Tabriz, Mobarakeh steel: flaring and furnace heat as a proxy for throughput; strike fires. Nighttime lights where reported. Commercial satellite imagery as reported by press and OSINT.*

**Current read:** No thermal feed this run (FIRMS key unset), so the read rests on commercial imagery reporting: Iran is visibly reconstructing hardened nuclear-adjacent infrastructure at Parchin and fortifying Pickaxe Mountain, while Kharg shows zero tanker calls in the AIS record. Confidence low on industrial throughput, medium on the reconstruction reporting; read set 2026-09-19.
**Evidence log:**
- 2026-09-19: FIRMS thermal anomalies not collected (FIRMS_MAP_KEY not set) — no flaring or furnace proxy this run.
- 2026-09-19: Kharg Island records zero tanker calls for a third consecutive week; one non-tanker call (PortWatch).
- 2026-09-18: Vantor imagery via the Institute for Science and International Security shows Taleghan 2 at Parchin being rebuilt — tarp over the hardened buried facility, concrete caps over bunker-buster penetrations, a new concrete apron; work began in June ([JPost](https://www.jpost.com/middle-east/iran-news/article-909027)).
- 2026-09-16: CSIS imagery shows continued construction and fortification at Pickaxe Mountain; the IAEA said on Sep 10 it identified construction but cannot determine activity inside ([Alma](https://israel-alma.org/the-iranian-arena-monthly-review-august-september-2026)).
**Would change the read:** A restored FIRMS feed showing sustained thermal anomalies at Assaluyeh, Bandar Abbas or Mobarakeh, or new commercial imagery of refinery or petrochemical throughput.

## Q7. What is the Houthi front actually changing?
*Red Sea and Bab el-Mandeb transits by vessel type, Saudi Red Sea export loadings at Yanbu, strikes on Saudi territory, US and Israeli strikes on Yemen, and the effect on Saudi decision-making.*

**Current read:** Bab el-Mandeb is impaired, not closed: tanker tonnage is at 69% of 2025 and rose 29% week-on-week, while Suez had its busiest month since December 2023. The Houthi advance erased the Hormuz-diversion increment but is specifically suppressing Saudi-affiliated traffic, consistent with the Houthis' stated exemption of all non-Saudi shipping. Confidence high; read changed 2026-09-19 (prior "structural near-zero" withdrawn).
**Evidence log:**
- 2026-09-20: Bab el-Mandeb week to Sep 13 revised — 27.3 transits/day, 8.1 tankers, 438k DWT/day; tanker DWT −31% vs 2025 and **+29% w/w** (yesterday's pull showed −11%). Suez 37.9/day, 15.9 tankers, 687k DWT/day, −18% vs 2025 (PortWatch).
- 2026-09-20: Yanbu King Fahd exports 37 kt in the fully reported week to Sep 11 versus 794 kt in the same week of 2025 (−95%); one tanker call (PortWatch).
- 2026-09-18: Lloyd's List Intelligence — August Suez transits 1,232, +28% YoY, busiest since Dec 2023; Bab el-Mandeb 1,165 voyages, +6% YoY but −49% MoM; 39 ultra-large containerships through BEM since July versus one in all of 2025; activity still 70% below pre-crisis ([Lloyd's List](https://www.lloydslistintelligence.com/resources/blog/red-sea-brief-18-september-2026)).
- 2026-09-16: Houthis told US officials in Muscat they will not attack American, Israeli or other commercial vessels — only Saudi-linked ships — and remain committed to the 2025 ceasefire ([Iran International/Reuters](https://www.iranintl.com/en/202609169530)).
- 2026-09-17: Five killed as Saudi Arabia and the Houthis trade attacks; first Saudi death of the month in Taif; al-Houthi calls the Mecca targeting claim "a heinous lie" ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/17/five-killed-as-saudi-arabia-and-yemens-houthis-trade-attacks)).
**Would change the read:** A Houthi attack on a non-Saudi-affiliated vessel in Bab el-Mandeb, or Bab el-Mandeb tanker DWT falling below roughly 200k/day.
