- **The contradiction worth naming.** PortWatch reports Yanbu at absolute zero for three weeks while two dated reports put the same terminal near its maximum. I have resolved this in favour of the reporting, but note the weakness honestly: both Asharq items are plausibly syndicated from a common originator and their article IDs suggest different vintages. If a dated Kpler or Vortexa Saudi Red Sea loading figure contradicts ~5 mb/d, the Q1/Q7 move reverses and the AIS zero becomes the better measurement. This is the single most consequential open uncertainty in today's briefing.

A higher assumed dollar *lowers* the computed bubble, so the direction of that bias favours my conclusion rather than undermining it — but I have one day of bubble data for the Emami and no prior print, so "widening" is inferred from the Bahar Azadi series and from the coin falling less than the gram, not measured directly on the Emami.
