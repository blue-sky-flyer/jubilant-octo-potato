## Daily Briefing - 2026-11-15

**Day 261 / Week 38.** Bahrain's own defence force — not the IRGC — confirmed Iranian aerial attacks on its territory this morning, the second in three days and the first authenticated by the host state, which is precisely the trigger this file named on Nov 13 for the Gulf's status-quo preference to break. Separately the land-trade story inverted: the neighbours are refusing to close crossings, and the crossings are closing themselves.

### The calls — where the read moved

**Q2 · Inside Iran — the land blockade is biting through congestion rather than policy; the "loosening at the margin" read is withdrawn.**
Bazargan has gone from a five-day queue (Nov 8 imagery) to **Iranian trucks waiting three weeks** to enter Turkey, Turkish trucks one week inbound; Iraq's Shib reopened after a three-week closure only for Sumar to shut indefinitely, while Gabd-Rimdan, Taftan, Mand and Chagai are closed or intermittent ([Maritime Executive, Nov 13](https://maritime-executive.com/article/naval-blockade-fuels-mass-micro-smuggling-into-pakistan)). *Agent arithmetic: 16 days of backlog added in five calendar days implies arrivals running at roughly four times throughput.* This reconciles with the NYT/Ynet finding that Ankara, Baghdad and Islamabad are refusing US closure demands — **Iron Gate does not need their cooperation to work.** Second-order: fuel is being smuggled out by jerrycan while Tehran's own pumps run hours-long rationing queues ([NY Post, Nov 14](https://nypost.com/2026/11/14/world-news/hours-long-lines-protests-at-iranian-gas-stations-reach-breaking-point-on-eve-of-bloody-november/)) — distribution failure on top of a glut. Confidence medium-high; a dated Bazargan truck count showing the queue clearing reverses it.

**Q5 · US/Gulf — the Bahrain attack series is now confirmed by the host state, not only claimed by the IRGC.**
The IRGC says it launched six drones at a US air-defence radar at Riffa Air Base; sirens sounded, the interior ministry told residents to shelter, the **Bahrain Defence Force said its air defences thwarted several Iranian aerial attacks**, and the US said it intercepted two ([Al Jazeera, Nov 15](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/15/iran-says-it-targets-us-radar-in-bahrain-what-do-we-know)); Bahrain issued a nationwide threat alert ([The National, Nov 15](https://www.thenationalnews.com/news/mena/2026/11/15/iran-war-live-bahrain-nationwide-threat-alert)). *Arithmetic: claimed launches 2 (Nov 13) → 6; US-acknowledged intercepts 2 → 2, widening the claimed-minus-acknowledged gap from 0 to 4.* Attacks are authenticated; impact is not. Agent inference (medium): probability of a major US strike on Iranian territory within 14 days rises from ~15% to **~20%**. A Gulf state publicly conceding damage, or invoking Mecca Pact Article 1, moves it further.

**Q6 · Israel — the missile estimate used to argue for a strike jumped 47% in twelve days, published on cabinet morning.**
[Israel Hayom, Nov 15, 07:04 GMT](https://www.israelhayom.com/2026/11/15/iran-rebuilds-missile-arsenal-intelligence-estimates-up-to-2500-missiles) reports intelligence estimating Iran has rebuilt to **up to 2,500 ballistic missiles** — headline-level, single carrier. Against the Nov 3 Israeli assessment of **1,700** and ~50 built monthly ([Alma, Nov 12](https://israel-alma.org/irans-missile-arsenal-erosion-rebuilding-renewed-erosion-and-a-further-attempt-to-rebuild)): *1,700 + 50 × 0.4 months ≈ 1,720 expected today; 2,500 requires ~2,000/month.* The two figures cannot be measuring the same stock — either the 1,700 baseline is superseded, or "up to 2,500" counts a wider set than Israel-ranged systems. Agent inference (medium): a number moving 47% without a production path, released on the morning of the first Security Cabinet since Oct 5, is an advocacy input, not a measurement update. The underlying assessment with a dated baseline would settle it.

### Qwen
I differ on Q5: the Bahrain attack does not raise strike probability to ~20%. CENTCOM’s denial of radar damage and only two intercepts (matching IRGC’s claim) suggests Iran is signaling, not escalating. The host-state confirmation (BDF) merely validates IRGC’s claim — it doesn’t imply new intent. The 6 vs 2 gap is not a “widening” of unacknowledged attacks; it’s IRGC inflating, and the U.S. matching its own intercept count. No damage, no escalation. Probability of a major U.S. strike remains ~15%. The data supports tactical provocation, not strategic escalation.  

Also: Q6’s 2,500-missile claim is not “advocacy.” Alma’s Nov 12 estimate of 1,700 was post-strike, pre-rebuild; Iran’s 50/month production + 800+ missiles fired since Feb 2026 + known stockpile recovery (per Alma) makes 2,500 plausible. The number is consistent with trajectory.
