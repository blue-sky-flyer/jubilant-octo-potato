**Q1 · Flows — the bypass bridge is lengthening: Gulf crude now reaches Korea with a second transshipment leg.**
Ulsan took **5.512 Mt of crude in October, +7.5% m/m**, with new arrivals from India's **Vadinar** and Egypt's **Sidi Kerir**, and the share from the established alternatives Yanbu and Fujairah down to **46.9%** ([Seoul Economic Daily, Nov 9](https://en.sedaily.com/finance/2026/11/09/ulsan-crude-imports-shift-to-india-egypt-as-mideast-crisis)). The terminal record corroborates from the other end: **Sikka/Jamnagar exports 463 kt on 16 tanker calls vs 277 kt on 8 in 2025 — +67% tonnage on double the calls — and Ulsan imports 2,266 kt vs 1,707 kt, +33%** (PortWatch, agent arithmetic). Read: this is not Gulf crude displaced by other barrels, it is Gulf crude arriving with an extra leg, and each leg adds freight days plus a counterparty. **Agent inference (medium): the detour is being institutionalised by Asian refiners, so a Hormuz reopening would not restore direct flows quickly.** Confidence high on the measurement; the Yanbu/Fujairah share recovering above 60% would break it.

## Model cross-check

- **Resolved — the Nov 9 new-moon window.** Logged not adopted on Nov 8 from [WSWS (Nov 7)](https://www.wsws.org/en/articles/2026/11/07/jnxh-n07.html). The date is today and no strike on Iranian territory has been reported. The item is retired, and the general rule stands: lunar phase is a planning convenience and has never once functioned as a dated indicator in this file.
- **Resolved — yesterday's Iranian ADS-B collapse.** I reported 69 aircraft against 105 a week earlier, −34%. Today: **82 against 84**. That was a single-session artefact. **Correction to method, not fact: the OpenSky civil proxy has too much intraday variance to be read at daily frequency; from today it is used on a weekly mean only.**
- **Named contradiction of the day, and its consequence.** The president says three carriers are "here"; the Roosevelt was alongside in Singapore three days ago and her own service expects her to be a **relief**, not an addition. If USNI is right, the end-November three-CSG package that has organised the posture file since Oct 20 never materialises as three simultaneous decks. **That is the single most consequential unresolved question in Q5 and it resolves itself by mid-November without any new reporting.**
- **Attributed, not adopted.** Trump put Iran's retained capacity at **10% of missiles and 15% of drones** (Iran International, Nov 8). This is irreconcilable with ISW's ~70% stockpile baseline, with the Oct 10 Haaretz account of IDF officials assessing replenishment and accelerating production, and with the Iranian claim of >75%. All three are claims; none is measurement. Logged as a presidential claim and excluded from scenario weights.
- **Open, unresolved — the Yanbu attribution.** Seoul Economic Daily puts Yanbu plus Fujairah at 46.9% of Ulsan's October barrels while PortWatch has recorded **zero Yanbu tanker calls for three consecutive weeks**. Most likely a timing or origin-attribution difference (cargoes lifted earlier in the month, or barrels booked to the originating terminal after an STS). Not reconciled; neither series is adjusted.
- **Partially resolved — the Hormuz exemption list.** The Nov 8 note that no second originator existed for the India/China/Qatar/Iraq exemptions is upgraded: Al Jazeera published its own explainer on [Nov 6](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/6/why-is-iran-exempting-india-china-qatar-iraq-from-hormuz-restrictions). Two carriers now, possibly one originator; still not adopted into flow arithmetic.
- **Watch for contradiction next run:** (1) whether the next PortWatch week holds Fujairah above 900 kt or shows the Oct 24–30 print was backlog clearing; (2) whether any mediator aircraft reaches Tehran, which is now the cleanest single observable in the whole diplomatic file; (3) whether the Nov 8 rockets/missiles alert recurs, which would be the first genuine Israeli-front movement since late September.
