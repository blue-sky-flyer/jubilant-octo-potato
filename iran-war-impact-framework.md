## Daily Briefing - 2026-09-12

**Overall situation:** Day 197 / Week 29. The defining development of the last 24 hours is the Houthi capture of Mayun Island (Perim Island) at the mouth of Bab el-Mandeb, confirmed by AP/NPR as of Sep 12, combined with the Saudi shutdown of its East-West crude pipeline as a precaution after it came under attack — together constituting the most severe dual-chokepoint stress event of the conflict to date. The Iraqi prime minister confirmed the drone attack on Saudi Arabia's East-West pipeline originated in Iraq and dismissed a regional army commander, introducing a new Iraq-Saudi friction vector. Hormuz transits remain near record lows with Bab el-Mandeb simultaneously collapsing toward near-zero.

**What changed in the last 24 hours:**

1. **Houthis capture Mayun (Perim) Island at Bab el-Mandeb mouth — Saudi East-West pipeline shut as precaution** ([NPR/AP, Sep 12](https://www.npr.org/2026/09/12/g-s1-143044/yemens-houthis-capture-red-sea-island); [Al Jazeera liveblog, Sep 12](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/liveblog/2026/9/12/iran-war-live-houthis-control-red-sea-coast-saudi-pipeline-shut-down) — double-sourced): Houthi forces seized Mayun Island, confirmed by a senior Yemeni military official and a Houthi official speaking to AP. Houthi spokesman Yahya Saree stated "maritime navigation is safe for all companies except for Saudi vessels," but markets are treating this as a near-total Bab el-Mandeb interdiction event: J.Post/Kpler data from the same window shows only **one commodity vessel** crossed Bab el-Mandeb on the relevant day (down from 20 the prior day). Saudi Arabia separately shut its East-West crude pipeline — which carries Saudi oil bypassing Hormuz to the Red Sea at Yanbu — "as a precaution after it came under attack" (Al Jazeera Sep 12). Iraqi PM Ali al-Zaidi confirmed the pipeline drone attack originated in Iraq and dismissed a regional army commander. Combined effect: both Hormuz (transits near single digits) and Bab el-Mandeb (near zero) are simultaneously at functional collapse, with Saudi Arabia's primary Hormuz bypass route now also shutdown. This is the most severe simultaneous chokepoint disruption of the conflict.

2. **IEA Sep 11 OMR: 2026 supply gap to widen further; recovery pushed to 2027; inventories "shrinking"** ([Reuters, Sep 11](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/global-2026-oil-supply-gap-deepen-delayed-return-normal-gulf-flows-iea-says-2026-09-11) — single-sourced at Sep 11 level; consistent with IEA Aug OMR trend): The IEA's September Oil Market Report states global oil supply and demand will "fall further than previously thought" in 2026, as lack of progress in ending the Iran war delays return of normal Middle East flows into 2027. The IEA warned: "Inventories have so far played a crucial role in balancing the market. With buffers shrinking and the global refining system stretched to the limit, the need for progress in resolving the conflict in the Middle East is greater than ever to avoid further market tightening." This is the IEA's most explicit public warning of the conflict on inventory depletion trajectory. Also: Quartz/IEA (undated Facebook post — not cited as primary source; Reuters Sep 11 is the sourced item) referenced a 5.7 mb/d full-year supply decline figure. ⚠️ Single-sourced at Sep 11 level (Reuters); consistent with Aug OMR direction.
