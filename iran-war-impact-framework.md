### The calls — where the read moved

Ynet, citing Israeli defence officials, reports **no major US military buildup detected**: additional US forces in southern Israel and movements in the Arabian Sea, but the latter "not unusual," and **no significant signs of preparations** for a new operation ([Ynet, Nov 6](https://www.ynetnews.com/article/hkvnd65agx); [Iran International, Nov 6](https://www.iranintl.com/en/202611068872)). Against that, Rapidan's Bob McNally puts resumption of fighting at **80%** and Brent above $100 ([CNBC, Nov 6](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/06/us-iran-war-trump-hormuz-oil-midterms.html)). I believe the sensor over the forecast: four European officials said on Nov 2 that forces were already amassed, and Israel says they are not. **Agent inference (medium-high): probability of a US strike on Iranian territory within 14 days ~25%, not 80%.** Domestic constraint also arrived earlier than modelled — Democrats are seeking to use government funding now rather than January ([Politico, Nov 6](https://www.politico.com/news/2026/11/06/democrats-iran-war-government-shutdown-01153337)). A Diego Garcia rotation or a third carrier entering CENTCOM reverses it.

**Q9 · Diplomacy — Tehran has put paper on the nuclear file for the first time since Doha collapsed; the sequencing, not the content, is the news.**
A senior Iranian official says Iran sent a **nuclear proposal to Washington through Qatar**, to be discussed only after the US meets the seven demands, including an end to "acts of aggression" and the naval blockade (Reuters via CNBC, Nov 6). That follows Araghchi's compression of the Hormuz sequence from seven days to **two** ([MEE, Nov 1](https://www.middleeasteye.net/live-blog/live-blog-update/araghchi-says-hormuz-could-be-reopened-two-days-if-conditions-met)) and a Doha round Israeli-sourced reporting called "some optimism… no progress on the nuclear issue" ([JPost, Nov 1](https://www.jpost.com/middle-east/iran-news/article-913501)). **Read: the stall logged since Oct 20 as deliberately timed to the midterms ended with the midterms, exactly as that read implied it would.** Confidence medium; the preconditions are unchanged, so this is a sequencing move, not a concession. A US public response to the seven demands would move it again.

**Q3 · Capital flows — the Tether basis has gone to a war high of +1.5% with the cash dollar flat; yesterday's "de-risking" read is wrong.**
Rial **2,669,000**, unchanged on the day and −2% on the week (stronger); Tether **2,709,610 = +1.52% to cash**, against +0.22% yesterday (tgju). **Arithmetic: the basis widened 1.3pp in one session while the cash rate moved 0.004% — the entire move is in the crypto leg, none of it in the street dollar.** AlanChand independently puts the cash sell at 2,677,000 on Nov 5, confirming the cash market is quiet ([AlanChand, Nov 5](https://alanchand.com/en/currencies-price)). Emami 2,662M against 7.32g fine at the implied 24k price = a **~4.2% coin premium**, flat. **Read (medium): this is demand for portable, cross-border dollars — the payments channel degrading, consistent with the electronic bill-of-lading and transfer frictions logged Oct 22 — not households fleeing the rial.** A second week above +1% with the coin premium above 10% would make it flight.

### Qwen
I differ on the probability of a U.S. strike within 14 days: Claude sets it at ~25%, but I set it at **40%**. While Ynet reports no major U.S. buildup, this is consistent with a *limited, surgical strike* — not a large-scale campaign. The 80% forecast by Rapidan Energy reflects credible elite consensus (McNally, Lombard Odier), and the timing aligns with Trump’s post-midterm window and Iran’s nuclear proposal, which is a tactical delay, not a de-escalation. The 25% read underweights the political imperative and overweights sensor gaps. Also: Hynes’ “under 60% capacity” is likely correct — Kpler’s 8.5 mb/d excludes shuttle transfers and non-AIS traffic; the 42% figure is misleading.
