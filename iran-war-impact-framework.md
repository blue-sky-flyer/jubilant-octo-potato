Fees are waived for 60 days per the MOU. However, all vessels must submit a detailed transit application via PGSA.ir at least 48 hours before entering the Strait, providing: vessel name, flag, IMO number, type, deadweight capacity, draft, cargo type, cargo quantity and VALUE, dangerous-goods declarations, origin and destination ports and countries, crew numbers and nationalities, insurance details, intended route, and expected crossing date. Permits are valid for a single transit up to five days. All vessels must use the northern route south of Iran's Larak Island; alternative routes are strictly prohibited. This is not dissolution of the PGSA — it is institutionalization of Iranian administrative sovereignty over Hormuz without the yuan/crypto fee that the MOU suspended. The cargo value and origin/destination fields give Iran a commercial intelligence database of every vessel attempting transit. The routing mandate means Iran unilaterally dictates physical passage parameters.
