**Key developments:**
- **PAKISTAN ARMY CHIEF MUNIR TO TEHRAN WITH DRAFT FINAL AGREEMENT**: Field Marshal Asim Munir traveled personally to Tehran carrying a draft text framework, per Iranian media (Voice of Emirates, corroborated by regional sources). His visit is "aimed at bringing Iranian and US positions closer together… with the goal of reaching the moment for the official announcement of the memorandum of understanding." A military principal physically carrying a draft — rather than diplomatic officials exchanging proposals at a distance — is the highest-fidelity operational signal of a deal in progress since the war began. Iran confirmed no deal had been reached but described gaps as narrowing.

The IRGC's incentive to accept unfavorable deal terms declines as military capacity is restored.
