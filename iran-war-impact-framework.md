### Qwen
I disagree with Claude’s reduction of a pre-Nov-3 US strike probability to ~8%. The data supports a higher probability (~15%). While CNN reports Israel’s pre-election strike was refused, the USS George Washington’s active loading of bunker-busters and SLAM-ER missiles (NSJ/WSJ, Oct 26), CENTCOM’s dispersal of aircraft from regional bases (JFeed, Oct 25), and Trump’s “may strike tomorrow” comment (CBS, Oct 25) indicate active war preparation — not defensive posture. Dispersal is consistent with anticipated Iranian retaliation *after* a strike, not before one. The refusal of Israel’s request does not preclude a unilateral US action; it may even accelerate it to avoid post-election complications. The 96-hour readiness window (JPost) and absence of CENTCOM strike releases since Sep 1 are consistent with planning for a decisive, post-election blow — not de-escalation. The 8% assignment underweights operational readiness signals.
