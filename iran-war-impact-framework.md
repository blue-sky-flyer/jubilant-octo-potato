### The calls — where the read moved

**Q5 · US posture — "effectively over" is a policy of permanence, and the restraint now has an author outside Washington.**
After meeting Qatar's PM, Trump: "Iran, militarily, is effectively over. We're just keeping it the status quo… if we do have to go back in, we are locked and loaded" ([Armenpress, Nov 10](https://armenpress.am/en/article/1265215)). On Truth Social he named Qatar, Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Bahrain and Kuwait as preferring "a continuation of the Greatest Naval Blockade in History, and the new Operation Iron Gate — Therefore, Land and Sea," and told reporters he was acting "at the behest of countries that are their neighbors" ([Daily Sabah, Nov 10](https://www.dailysabah.com/world/americas/trump-steps-back-from-fresh-iran-strikes-after-regional-appeal/amp)). **Read: a Gulf-underwritten status quo is harder to reverse than a unilateral one — the trigger that breaks it is now an Iranian strike on Gulf infrastructure, not a White House mood swing.** Confidence medium-high. The contradiction: three Houthi missiles were intercepted over Riyadh and southern Saudi Arabia the same morning ([House of Saud, Nov 10](https://houseofsaud.com/houthi-missiles-riyadh-nov10)). "Effectively over" describes the US–Iran air campaign, not the war.

**Q2 · Inside Iran — the land squeeze breaks at the exact point Washington was tightening it.**
Iraqi Border Ports Authority chief Omar al-Waeli says **Al-Sheeb reopens today**, with Mandali closing Nov 13 for works ([Shafaq, Nov 9](https://shafaq.com/en/society/Iraq-to-reopen-Al-Sheeb-crossing-with-Iran); [Streamline, Nov 10](https://streamlinefeed.co.ke/news/iraq-reopens-al-sheeb-border-crossing-with-iran-amid-us-pressure-and-security-reviews)). That is the named trigger in this ledger, and it lands three days after Task Force Iron Gate. Against it: Maritime Executive, reading Google Earth imagery via CJRC, records a **five-day truck queue at Bazargan** and judges the sea blockade "completely effective," shutting out the 80% of Iranian trade that arrived by ship ([Maritime Executive, Nov 8](https://maritime-executive.com/article/centcom-mirrors-naval-blockade-with-iron-gate-for-iran-s-borders)). **Arithmetic: one crossing restores a route, not capacity — with land imports up 41% in H1 against southern ports that carried 85% of cargo, the land channel cannot absorb the gap.** I downgrade the "coordinated US-directed blockade" component: Baghdad is sequencing crossings on its own calendar. Confidence medium-high on the reopening, medium on the squeeze. Mandali staying shut past Nov 13 would reverse this.

**Q3 · Capital flows — Tehran's free market is pricing the ceasefire faster than Brent.**

The move is corroborated from the hard-asset side: Emami fell 2,588M → **2,548M** and the gold-implied ounce $4,011 → **$3,980**, so the rally is not a gold artefact (tgju, agent arithmetic). Tether at 2,611,570 is **−0.7% to cash**, the deepest discount in this run (−0.1% Nov 9). **Read: a negative crypto basis alongside a rising rial is dollar *supply* — holders selling hard currency into the peace headline, the first genuinely two-sided print since the October slide.** Brent fell 7% over two sessions; the rial moved 4% in five. **Agent inference (medium): this is a thin market that reverses on one strike.** Confidence medium. Tether back above +0.5% with the cash rate weakening would break it.
