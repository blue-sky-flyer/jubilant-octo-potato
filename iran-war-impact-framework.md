This is a classic Path C face-saving move: the IRGC uses the mine as a pretext to publicly reactivate the PGSA transit-authorization architecture at precisely the moment the US Treasury has sanctioned that institution. It commits no new kinetic act, but it directly contradicts the draft MOU's requirement for "unrestricted" Hormuz passage and signals that IRGC operational authority is not being stood down during the approval window. (LiveUAMap, House of Saud — May 31, 2026)
