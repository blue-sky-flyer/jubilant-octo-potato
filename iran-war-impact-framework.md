## Daily Briefing - 2026-11-05

**Day 251 / Week 36.** Brent fell more than 8% overnight to settle near $95 — the steepest single-day drop since May — which means the oil market has now unwound the entire October war premium on a political event that changed no physical fact in the Gulf. The arithmetic that matters today is not the price but the export series behind it: Gulf crude leaving the region is at or above pre-war volume while the strait itself is empty, and that gap is now large enough to be the whole story.

### The calls — where the read moved

**Q1 · Flows — the dark-flow framing is wrong; the correct framing is that Hormuz has been *disintermediated*, and the October export data now proves it at scale.**
Tanker-tracking compilations put combined October crude exports from Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Iraq and Kuwait at **15.53 mb/d, +10% m/m and roughly back to January levels**, with Saudi at 7.0 mb/d (+13%) and Yanbu loadings +35%; Iran logged a second consecutive month of zero loadings ([BigGo/Kpler-Vortexa, Nov 3](https://finance.biggo.com/news/a2065723-a9b5-462b-8b35-be70bc48f1e2)). Kpler separately puts September non-Iran Gulf exports at **16.5 mb/d** ([Tomorrow's Affairs, Nov 2](https://tomorrowsaffairs.com/iran-will-open-hormuz-only-if-it-keeps-the-key-to-it)). **Arithmetic: 7.0 mb/d Saudi for October against the 5.52 mb/d Kpler print for Oct 1–25 I adopted yesterday implies the last week of October ran near 11 mb/d — implausible. The two series are not reconcilable; I now believe the 5.52 figure was corridor-constrained and the 7.0 is the fuller month.** The ADNOC third-Hormuz-bypass pipeline story ([Anadolu/Bloomberg, Nov 4](https://aa.com.tr/en/economy/uae-mulls-3rd-oil-pipeline-to-bypass-strait-of-hormuz-report/4111761)) and Aramco's East–West expansion engineering ([Energy News Beat, Nov 3](https://energynewsbeat.co/crude-oil/saudi-arabia-looking-to-expand-exit-ports-for-oil)) are the capex confirmation: the bypass is being made permanent. Confidence medium-high. A Kpler print below 13 mb/d reverses it.

**Q5 · US posture — the midterm result did not open a strike window; the president closed it himself, and the oil market has now priced that.**
Trump told the FT on Nov 3 he has no plans to strike because "we seem to be getting everything we want," while calling the military "locked and loaded" ([JPost, Nov 3](https://www.jpost.com/middle-east/iran-news/article-913766)). Against that, the NYT reported CENTCOM ready to begin strikes **as early as Wednesday Nov 4** with three carriers in theatre ([New Arab, Nov 3](https://www.newarab.com/news/centcom-prepared-renewed-iran-war-americans-head-polls)). **Nov 4 passed with no strike reported and no CENTCOM release.** JINSA counts only **5 Iranian missiles/drones on Nov 2–4** against 613 since Jul 7 ([JINSA, Nov 4](https://jinsa.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Iran-War-Update-11.4.26.pdf)) — a near-zero tempo on the one date the whole framework pointed at. ISW reads Iranian leaders as believing the election changes nothing ([ISW, Nov 4](https://understandingwar.org/research/middle-east/iran-update-november-4-2026)). Agent inference (medium-high): the early-November anchor has failed; the window reverts to a deal-or-drift path. A CENTCOM-acknowledged strike reverses it.

**Q3 · Capital flows — the rial strengthened on the US election, the first war-period appreciation driven by an external political event.**
Rial **2,669,100, −1% vs Oct 29** — the first weekly strengthening in a month, off the 2,735,000 of Nov 3 (tgju). Tether 2,675,040 = **+0.2% to cash**, a second consecutive positive basis, which breaks the three-week supply-side read flagged in yesterday's cross-check. Emami 2,661M vs 2,642M (tgju); AlanChand independently shows Emami 2,645M with a **+3.39% bubble** against intrinsic ([AlanChand, Nov 4](https://alanchand.com/en/gold-price)). **Read: cash dollar down, coin bid up, stablecoin basis positive — that is a de-risking pattern, not distress. Tehran's street is pricing the same de-escalation the Brent tape is.** Confidence medium; a return above 2.73m with the basis back negative kills it.
