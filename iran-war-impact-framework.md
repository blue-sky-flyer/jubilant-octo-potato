### The calls — where the read moved

**Q5 · US posture — the November attack tempo is running *above* October, not 16% below it; yesterday's deceleration finding is withdrawn.**
The IMO, via the UN Security Council, counts **136 vessel security incidents in Hormuz and the wider Middle East between Mar 1 and Nov 11: 35 in October, 10 in November** ([Al Jazeera, Nov 14](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/14/un-says-25-seafarers-killed-in-around-hormuz-since-february)). *Agent arithmetic: 10 over 11 days = 0.91/day against October's 35 ÷ 31 = 1.13/day — a 20% fall on the IMO series. But the Washington Institute count this file adopted (37 in October, 10 in the first ten days) implies 1.0/day in November.* The two series disagree on the November numerator by zero and on the denominator by one day, which is the whole of the effect. **The deceleration was a measurement artefact of an 11-day window; there is no statistically meaningful slowdown.** Khiari's framing — "this situation cannot be allowed to become the new normal," October the worst month of the war — is the better summary. Confidence medium-high; a December IMO print below ~0.7/day would restore the deceleration read.

**Q8 · Mecca Pact — the treaty is in force in law as of today, and the deployments that preceded it are now retroactively covered.**
Türkiye's ratification law took effect on publication in the Official Gazette **today, Nov 14**; parliament passed it Nov 5 by 279–119; Saudi Arabia and Pakistan had already deposited their instruments, so the agreement enters into force on the last deposit ([Hürriyet Daily News, Nov 14](https://www.hurriyetdailynews.com/turkiye-enacts-law-approving-mecca-defense-pact-229381)). The memorandum is the analytically important part: the Article 1 commitment is **explicitly non-automatic**, requiring consultation and each state's own constitutional process, and Akar told parliament any Turkish troop movement still needs an Article 92 vote. *Agent inference (medium-high): this converts the pact from a deterrent promise into a consultation forum with a legal clock — and it legalises after the fact the Turkish, Pakistani and Egyptian presences already in the kingdom (Egypt's FM conceded on Nov 10 that its forces predated the Oct 26 authorising vote, [House of Saud Nov 10](https://houseofsaud.com/egypt-troops-saudi-arabia-nov10)).* Read moved from "deployment without ratification" to "ratification catching up to deployment." A first invocation over a Houthi strike would be the test.
