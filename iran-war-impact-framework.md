## Daily Briefing - 2026-11-18

**Day 264 / Week 38.** The Bahrain drone series became a Gulf-wide series overnight — Kuwait's air defences engaged, an ADNOC-owned ship was hit in Hormuz with a crew member missing, and US jets struck an IRGC missile site on Qeshm — while the prediction market on a ceasefire pause fell 28 points in a day. The two-week-old read that strangulation was stable just lost its cheapest assumption: that Iran's retaliation would stay confined to one host state and produce no casualties.

### The calls — where the read moved

**Q5 · US posture — the retaliation campaign has crossed from zero-damage theatre into a second host state and a casualty, and the strike loop is now daily rather than every two days.**
Kuwait says its air defences are countering "hostile" drone attacks and that explosions heard are intercepts; Iran says it hit a US base in Kuwait; Bahrain calls the attacks "criminal" after two danger alerts in quick succession ([Al Jazeera, Nov 18](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/liveblog/2026/11/18/iran-war-live-kuwait-says-intercepting-hostile-drone-attacks); [The Hindu, Nov 18](https://www.thehindu.com/news/international/bahrain-kuwait-attacked-after-us-strikes-iranian-island/article71714916.ece)). ADNOC L&S says **one crew member is missing, eleven injured** on a ship attacked in the strait Tuesday evening; US jets struck an IRGC missile site on Qeshm after the Bahrain attack ([Gulf News, Nov 18](https://gulfnews.com/uae/usisrael-iran-war-what-uae-residents-need-to-know-today-nov-18-2026-1.500751757)). *Agent arithmetic: the claimed-event cadence goes Nov 13 → 15 → 17 → 18, i.e. from one every two days to consecutive days; the target set goes al-Riffa → Isa → Kuwait.* The Nov 15 "trigger half-fired" formulation no longer holds: a second Gulf state is engaged and an Emirati-owned hull has casualties. Raise the probability of a major US strike on Iranian territory within 14 days from ~25% to ~35%. Confidence medium-high. A Gulf state publicly conceding damage on its own soil, or a Mecca Pact Article 1 consultation, would push it further.

**Q1 · Gulf flows — the bypass is no longer absorbing the diverted barrels; Fujairah is now exporting more than the entire inside-Gulf terminal complex.**
PortWatch week to Nov 13: inside-Gulf loadings collapse to **Ras Tanura 0 kt (prior 261), Ras Laffan 108 (428), Jebel Ali 302 (277) = 410 kt against 966 the prior week, −58%**, and 2.4% of 2025's 3,375 kt for the same three. Meanwhile **Fujairah exports jump to 1,198 kt from 874 (+37%) on 39 tanker calls** — above its own 2025 week (986 kt) for the first time in this series (PortWatch, agent arithmetic). Yanbu stays at zero for a third week. *Inference, medium-high: this is not a demand collapse but a re-plumbing — ADCOP and the Hormuz shuttle now load outside the strait while inside-Gulf terminals go quiet, which is exactly what the Nov 17 Fujairah congestion data implied.* Hormuz AIS tanker DWT at 18k/day (−99% vs 2025) remains a floor, not a measurement. Confidence high on the divergence; the read breaks if Fujairah exports fall back below ~900 kt while inside-Gulf loadings stay suppressed.

**Q3 · Capital flows — the sub-1% Tether regime just ended, on the day the rial hit a new war low.**
Rial **2,669,050** (+2% on the week), Tether **2,696,870 = +1.0% over cash**, the first print at or above the +1% threshold after five consecutive sub-1% weeks (tgju). Emami **2,505M**, down a further 17M; arzbin's own Nov 18 brief puts USD at 266,700 toman and the coin at 252m ([arzbin, Nov 18](https://www.arzbin.com/brief/iran-market-brief-2026-11-18-morning)). IranWire notes gold, the Emami coin and bitcoin all fell in the same week — unusual, because all three are the standard household exit from the rial ([IranWire, Nov 17](https://iranwire.com/en/features/160378-what-it-means-when-gold-the-emami-coin-and-bitcoin-fall-in-the-same-week)). *Inference, medium: a stablecoin premium rising while metal falls is a liquidity signal, not a flight signal — households are selling metal to raise rials, and the marginal buyer of dollars is now someone who needs transferable dollars, not a store of value.* The Q3 trigger needs two consecutive weeks above +1%; one week does not fire it. Confidence medium.
