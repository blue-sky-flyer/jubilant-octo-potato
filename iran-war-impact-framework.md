The Senate had already advanced a companion resolution 50-47, with four Republican defectors (Collins, Murkowski, Paul, Cassidy). Even if both chambers ultimately pass, Trump would veto. But the 213-214 House margin confirms Trump is one Republican away from a veto-bound bill that would signal politically untenable public opposition to the war — a ceiling on how hard he can drive the economic siege before legislative pressure forces a deal.

No retaliatory strike has been authorized. The restraint is a deliberate strategic choice — retaliating now would collapse the revived negotiating window. Its cost: the IRGC's "scale and nature" warning requires a credible US counter-signal. If talks stall again, the calculus reverses.
- **NETANYAHU: "IRAN'S TERROR REGIME IS DOOMED TO FALL" (June 2, Times of Israel)**: In a public statement on June 2, Netanyahu said the "foundations of Iran's terror regime have cracked" and it is "doomed to fall." This is Israel's information operation against Trump's deal narrative: framing any agreement as legitimizing a collapsing regime, targeting Trump's domestic GOP base and the four Senate defectors who just voted for war termination. The statement directly undercuts the "historic deal" framing Trump needs to sell a Hormuz-reopening agreement.
