**Key developments:**
- **HEZBOLLAH REJECTS LEBANON-ISRAEL FRAMEWORK AS "NULL AND VOID" — MOU ALL-FRONTS CLAUSE DIRECTLY THREATENED (Business Standard / New Arab / NPR, June 29):** Hezbollah Secretary-General Naim Qassem denounced the US-brokered Lebanon-Israel security agreement signed June 26 as "surrender" and declared it "null and void." Lebanon's parliamentary speaker Nabih Berri (Amal movement) simultaneously called the deal "unbalanced." The structural risk: the Versailles MOU requires "immediate and permanent termination of military operations on all fronts, including Lebanon." With Hezbollah rejecting the Lebanon settlement framework, Iran cannot credibly demonstrate all-fronts compliance and gains standing to invoke the MOU's Lebanon clause. Iran FM Araghchi has NOT formally done so as of June 29; the Burgenstock Lebanon de-confliction cell is the intended adjudication mechanism. But the Hezbollah rejection converts the Lebanon track from slow-moving implementation friction into an acute MOU termination trigger.
- **IRAN CYBERATTACKS ON ISRAEL TRIPLE — 4,800 INCIDENTS IN JUNE 2026 (Times of Israel / Rappler / New Arab, June 29):** Israeli National Cyber Directorate Director General Yossi Karadi confirmed to German newspaper Die Welt that Iran-attributed cyberattacks against Israel reached approximately 4,800 incidents in June 2026, up from roughly 1,600 in June 2025. Targets include critical infrastructure systems, central organizations, small-to-medium enterprises, law practices, and accounting firms. The shadow conflict track is intensifying sharply precisely as the kinetic track is under institutional standdown.

No formal WG ruling on the M/V Ever Lovely PGSA-clearance case was publicly announced as of June 29; technical talks are expected to produce an interim framework rather than a binding legal determination within the 60-day mandate.

Mohebi: disputes should route through PGSA and Iran FM, not a bilateral military line. This complicates the monitoring/dispute-resolution WG's ability to generate real-time de-escalation during the next kinetic incident — the mechanism that functioned partially in the June 25–27 three-step cycle has no real-time military-to-military backstop.
