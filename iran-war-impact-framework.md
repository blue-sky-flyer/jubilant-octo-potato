## Daily Briefing - 2026-09-30

**Day 215 / Week 31.** Bab el-Mandeb tanker tonnage printed **above** its 2025 level for the first time in this war, which tells you where Yanbu's restarted barrels are actually going — south, under the Houthi guns, not north through Suez. At the same time transponders are coming back on inside Hormuz, and tankers called at Iranian Gulf ports for the first time in five weeks.

### The calls — where the read moved

**Q7 · Red Sea — the "terminating basin" read is dead: the strait is a through-route again and it is carrying Saudi crude to Asia.**
Week to Sep 27: Bab el-Mandeb 27.1 transits/day, 9.4 tankers, **582k DWT/day — +82% w/w and +17% versus the same 2025 week**, the first positive year-on-year tanker print of the conflict. Suez 638k DWT, **−15% vs 2025, −10% w/w**. The Suez:BEM tanker-DWT ratio is now **1.10x against 1.50x in the 2025 week** (PortWatch, agent arithmetic). The +263k DWT/day increment is ~1.9 mb/d of laden capacity at ~7.3 bbl/t — within rounding of the ~2 mb/d Yanbu programme. Inference (high confidence): Yanbu is loading Asia-bound, so it exits via Bab el-Mandeb, while Suez falls because Saudi crude is cut off from Europe. PortWatch still shows Yanbu at **0 calls, 0 kt** — the cargoes are lifting at **Al Muajjiz**, outside its port set ([Reuters, Sep 29](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/saudi-resumes-yanbu-oil-loading-after-pipeline-restart-2026-09-29)). Believe the chokepoint series. Changes if BEM tanker DWT falls back under 400k/day.

**Q1 · Flows — the signal is the transponders, not the tonnage.**
Hormuz tanker DWT **18k/day from 1k** (+1152% w/w) on 9 visible tanker transits in seven days versus 4; Ras Laffan exports **243 kt, +208% w/w** on 6 tanker calls versus 3 (PortWatch). Corroborated from the vessel side: Qatar-linked LNG carriers resumed *visible* Hormuz transits after a blank August — GasLog Skagen off Sri Lanka Sep 27 with a Ras Laffan cargo, plus Al Shamal, Mesaimeer, Al Ghashamiya ([MarineLink/Kpler+LSEG, Sep 28](https://www.marinelink.com/news/qatarlinked-lng-tankers-resume-hormuz-543290)). At 0.9% of the 2025 1.96M DWT/day this is not volume; it is revealed preference — owners no longer believe AIS-on is what gets them hit. Meanwhile the published throughput spread **widened**: Kpler 10 mb/d strait + 6 mb/d bypass on a 19 mb/d pre-war base = 84% ([NYT, Sep 29](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/09/29/business/oil-exports-strait-of-hormuz.html)); 13.1 mb/d = 77% ([Oil & Gas ME, Sep 30](https://www.oilandgasmiddleeast.com/news/strait-of-hormuz-oil-flows)); 72% ([NY Post, Sep 28](https://nypost.com/2026/09/28/world-news/persian-gulf-oil-exports-hit-72-of-prewar-levels-as-us-says-iran-is-exhausted-by-blockade/)); 93.7% ([TV7, Sep 29](https://www.tv7israelnews.com/vod/series/563?episode-id=h4nn9NV9Zn0)). The denominators differ, not the flows. Confidence medium-high.

**Q2 · Inside Iran — the northern pivot went into reverse and Iranian Gulf ports took tanker calls under the blockade.**
Week to Sep 25: **Caspian 32 calls vs Gulf 28** — Caspian −32% w/w, Gulf +65% (2025: 14/30). Caspian imports 24 kt on 32 calls = **0.75 kt/call**, below the 0.9 that had held five weeks; Gulf 248 kt on 28 calls = 8.9 kt/call. Total visible imports **272 kt, +55% w/w**, and Caspian share of tonnage collapsed from 24% to **8.8%**. Six tanker calls across Iranian Gulf ports (Shahid Rajaee 4, Bandar Abbas 2) against zero in each of the prior four weeks; Bandar Khomeini imports 117 kt from 48 (PortWatch, agent arithmetic). Against that, UANI counts **122 commercial vessels redirected as of Sep 25**, from 115 on Sep 23 ([UANI, Sep 29](https://www.unitedagainstnucleariran.com/analysis/iran-shipping-update-september-29-2026)). Inference (medium): the blockade is a crude-export instrument first, and non-oil import traffic is being screened and passed. Alternative: one noisy week on a tiny base. Changes on a second consecutive week of Iranian Gulf tanker calls.
