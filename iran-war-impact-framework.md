## Model cross-check

- **The Kharg 295 kt print is the single most consequential number in today's pack and I have not corroborated it.** PortWatch shows one tanker call and 295 kt of exports at Kharg in the week to Nov 20 — against a blockade that CENTCOM says has stopped Iranian crude loadings since Aug 25 and against UANI's finding that no crude-laden tanker has cleared the Gulf of Oman since Jul 12. Either a laden hull moved and the blockade has a hole, or the AIS-derived tonnage estimate is an artefact of the >14 tankers UANI counts staged at Kharg. I lean artefact (60/40), because a 295 kt departure would have been reported. Resolve next run.
- **My Q3 inversion argument is clever and could be wrong.** A Tether premium falling from 1.8% to 0.9% while the cash rate gaps 8% is consistent with my "cash repriced to clear" reading, but it is equally consistent with simple noise in a thin series that has oscillated ±1.5pp weekly all month. The honest statement is that the two-week premium trigger this file was running has not fired and should probably be retired in favour of the spot rate itself.
- **Against my own Q7 call.** The Saudi Energy Ministry conceded Jazan but has never conceded Ras Tanura damage; the coalition's position remains that four drones were intercepted and debris fell. The Ras Tanura *loadings halt* is single-sourced to Tilt. The 950k b/d "impaired" figure therefore mixes a confirmed partial outage with an inferred one — do not treat it as a supply-loss estimate.
- **Trump's carrier announcement is unverified and sits against the naval data.** He said another carrier is being sent; USNI's Nov 23 tracker has two decks in the Arabian Sea and Makin Island still in Singapore. If a third deck is published next week, the Q5 read on readiness decay as the binding constraint weakens materially.
- **Watch for contradiction next run:** whether Kharg prints a second week; whether Ras Tanura loadings resume; the OPEC+ Nov 29 outcome against Brent $97; whether the Islamabad SPDC publishes anything operational on Saudi air defence; and whether the rial retraces or extends past 3.0m.

### Qwen
I differ on Q3: the Tether premium inversion (0.9% vs. cash rial) does *not* signal cash repricing to clear — it reflects *capital flight into crypto as a hedge*, not its collapse. The rial’s 8% weekly plunge and gold’s 5.2% premium (Emami coin) confirm panic-driven crypto adoption, not market equilibrium. Tether remains the *only* liquid, accessible dollar proxy for Iranians; its premium falling slightly amid currency freefall is expected, not contradictory. The real signal is the *persistence* of the premium above 0.5% — not its dip — proving crypto is a lifeline, not a casualty. My read: crypto demand is *rising*, not fading.
