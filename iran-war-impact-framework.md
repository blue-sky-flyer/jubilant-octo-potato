## Daily Briefing - 2026-11-11

**Day 257 / Week 37.** PortWatch came back and the deferred test resolved: Gulf terminal loadings nearly doubled in the week to Nov 6 while AIS-visible tanker tonnage through Hormuz fell to effectively zero — the widest gap between what is loaded and what is seen leaving in this war. Separately, the Secretary of State has given the status quo a name and an interagency coalition.

### The calls — where the read moved

**Q1 · Flows — inside-Hormuz loadings surged 70% in a week while visible strait tonnage fell 78%; the entire bridge is now dark or static.**
PortWatch week to Nov 6: Ras Tanura exports **372 kt** (67 prior week; 1,445 in 2025), Ras Laffan **425** (117; 1,418), Jebel Ali **274** (445; 2,247) — **1,071 kt against 629 prior, +70% w/w and 21% of the 2025 week** (PortWatch, agent arithmetic). Hormuz over the same window: 1.0 tanker/day, **3k DWT/day, −78% w/w** (PortWatch). Arithmetic: 1,071 kt ≈ 153 kt/day ≈ **~1.1 mb/d crude-equivalent** loading inside the Gulf against a visible outbound tanker flow under 0.3% of that. Fujairah ran **997 kt on 40 tanker calls — above the 893 kt of the 2025 week on 68 calls**, i.e. 24.9 kt per call versus 13.1. Sikka/Jamnagar took **15 tanker calls against 7 in 2025**. Corroboration: India imported ~2.8 mb/d Middle East crude in September, double August and the highest since February, via ship-to-ship transfer ([Upstox/Reuters tracking, Nov 5](https://upstox.com/news/upstox-originals/investing/oil-is-swapping-ships-mid-sea-to-reach-india-has-it-got-any-cheaper/article-203070)). **The Oct 24–30 Fujairah print was not backlog clearing.** Confidence high. A fall in Sikka tanker calls back toward 7/week would break it.

**Q7 · Red Sea — the tanker normalisation called on Oct 24 is dead, and it died with the Saudi front, not the Yemen ground war.**
Bab el-Mandeb: **343k tanker DWT/day, −61% vs 2025, −17% w/w**; 6.9 tankers/day against 8.6 the prior week and 15 in 2025 (PortWatch). Against the Oct 24 print of 554k (−5% vs 2025) that is a **38% fall in the tanker leg in three weeks**. Suez is unaffected — 780k DWT, **+1% vs 2025** — so the constraint is the southern gate alone. Yanbu records **zero tanker calls and zero exports for a sixth straight week against 24 calls and 674 kt in 2025**. The same window saw Houthi fire reach Riyadh's King Abdullah Financial District and damage an Aramco administrative building ([Le Monde/AFP, Nov 8](https://www.lemonde.fr/en/international/article/2026/11/08/saudi-capital-s-financial-district-targeted-in-attack-aramco-skyscraper-damaged_6774880_4.html)), and Saudi alerts issued over Yanbu itself ([JPost, Nov 6](https://www.jpost.com/middle-east/article-913945)). Confidence medium-high. One Yanbu tanker call, or two weeks of BAM tanker DWT above 500k, reverses it.

**Q5 · US posture — the status quo is now a named strategy with four principals behind it, while the European tanker node empties.**
Rubio told G7 foreign ministers in Paris, per three people present: "The President doesn't just have two options. It's not just return to war or sign a ridiculous peace deal. The third option we have is to continue to do what we do now" — adding Trump is under no pressure to decide because Iranians are damaging their own economy; **Rubio, Bessent, Ratcliffe and Kushner advocated it**, opposing renewed military action while sceptical of a deal ([Axios via Iran International, Nov 11](https://www.iranintl.com/en/202611116771)). **Read: yesterday's Gulf-authored restraint now has a Washington author too, and it is a cabinet bloc rather than a mood.** Physically consistent: at least **12 KC-135 tankers and three CV-22s left RAF Mildenhall over Nov 9–10** under raised security attributed to Iranian threats, a month after the B-1Bs left Fairford ([TWZ carrying WSJ, Nov 10](https://www.twz.com/air/usaf-aircraft-continue-to-reposition-away-from-raf-mildenhall-amid-reports-of-iranian-threats)). **Agent inference (medium): KC-135s are the enabler for any European-staged package; dispersing them for force protection lengthens the lead time.** Confidence medium-high. A Diego Garcia bomber rotation breaks it.
