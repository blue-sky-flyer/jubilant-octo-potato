## Daily Briefing — 2026-07-06

**Overall situation:** Day 129 / Week 19. The Khamenei state funeral enters its largest day — a 12-hour procession from Imam Hossein Square to Azadi Square in Tehran draws hundreds of thousands into the streets, with 15–20 million mourners expected across the six-day ceremony through July 9. The day produced three structurally significant developments: OPEC+ announced its fifth consecutive monthly production increase (+188,000 bpd for August), pulling Brent to $71.7/bbl — below any level since the war began; Houthi forces sank the bulk carrier M/V Magic Seas in the Red Sea using limpet mines (first ship sinking of the Red Sea escalation cycle, despite the Stimson Center's June confirmation that Houthis are "completely cut off" from Iranian weapons resupply); and Vahidi's IRGC moved to institutionalize military control over Iran's international diplomacy by coercing President Pezeshkian into appointing IRGC loyalist Mohammad Bagher Zolghadr as SNSC secretary — placing an IRGC loyalist at the formal intersection of Iran's nuclear negotiating mandate and Phase 2 talks. The diplomatic pause holds through July 9; no IRGC Hormuz kinetic action during the funeral period.

**Key developments:**
- **OPEC+ AGREES FIFTH CONSECUTIVE MONTHLY OUTPUT INCREASE — 188,000 BPD FOR AUGUST (Al Jazeera / Euronews, July 6):** Seven OPEC+ members (Saudi Arabia, Russia, Iraq, Kuwait, Kazakhstan, Algeria, Oman) agreed to increase combined output by 188,000 bpd in August, continuing the gradual unwinding of the 2023 voluntary cuts. Saudi Arabia adds 62,000 bpd (to 10.4M bpd total); Russia adds 62,000 bpd (to 9.88M bpd). This is the fifth consecutive monthly OPEC+ output increase since the Versailles MOU — the clearest collective signal from the alliance that its members expect Hormuz supply normalization to hold. Brent fell 0.5% to $71.7/bbl on the decision — the first time Brent has closed below $72 since the war began, driven by OPEC+ fundamentals rather than ceasefire hope alone.
- **HOUTHIS SINK M/V MAGIC SEAS — FIRST SHIP SINKING IN RED SEA ESCALATION CYCLE (gCaptain / Seatrade Maritime / UKMTO, July 6):** Houthi forces attacked the Liberia-flagged bulk carrier M/V Magic Seas (IMO 9736169, operated by Allseas Marine) with limpet mines and small-boat assault; the crew was forced to abandon ship and rescued by a passing commercial vessel; the ship subsequently sank from explosions below the waterline. Houthis also attacked the bulk carrier Eternity C shortly after, doubling the total ships sunk in the Red Sea since 2023 to four. This is the most significant Red Sea attack since the Versailles MOU and comes despite the Stimson Center's July 4 confirmation that Houthis are "completely cut off from Iranian weapons supplies" — meaning the sinking was executed from existing stockpiles, not new IRGC resupply. The Iran-MOU "all fronts" obligation is now directly tested: if the US holds Iran responsible for Houthi actions under the MOU's cessation language, it creates a new Iran-US Phase 2 dispute track.
- **VAHIDI COERCES APPOINTMENT OF IRGC LOYALIST AS SNSC SECRETARY — MILITARY SEIZES DIPLOMATIC CONTROL (IranWire / Outlook India, July 5-6):** IRGC Commander Ahmad Vahidi pressured President Pezeshkian into appointing Mohammad Bagher Zolghadr — an IRGC loyalist — as the new Supreme National Security Council secretary, giving the IRGC direct institutional control over Iran's international negotiations at the level below the Supreme Leader. The SNSC secretary manages the formal interface between Pezeshkian's diplomatic track (Araghchi/Ghalibaf) and the Supreme Leader's office. With Mojtaba Khamenei unable to exercise authority, Zolghadr becomes the effective gate between Pezeshkian's negotiating team and any authorization for Phase 2 nuclear concessions. This is Vahidi's most consequential post-Versailles institutional move — he now controls both the kinetic track (IRGC command) and the diplomatic authorization gate (SNSC secretary).
- **IDF STRIKES SOUTHERN LEBANON DURING FUNERAL — BEAUFORT/ALI TAHER HEZBOLLAH TUNNEL COMPLEX (Times of Israel / IDF statement, July 5):** IDF Chief of Staff Lt. Gen. Eyal Zamir toured the Beaufort Castle area in southern Lebanon on July 5, designating it "saturated with terror infrastructure." Lebanese media confirmed an IDF airstrike targeting the Ali Taher ridge area near Kfar Tebnit and Nabatieh al-Fawqa, where a Hezbollah underground complex hosts ~30 operatives. IDF also struck a Hezbollah cell near the southern Lebanon security zone. Israel is conducting targeted military operations in southern Lebanon during the precise window when Hezbollah Secretary-General Naim Qassem and senior Hezbollah leadership are physically in Tehran for the funeral — exploiting the command absence to degrade infrastructure before commanders return (~July 9-10).
- **MOJTABA KHAMENEI ABSENT FUNERAL DAY 3 — NO VIDEO, NO AUDIO IN 129 DAYS (CNN / NBC / Al Jazeera, July 6):** The 12-hour July 6 procession — the largest single day of the funeral — proceeded without Mojtaba's presence or any verified proof-of-life. Three of his brothers (Mostafa, Meysam, and Masoud) prayed behind the coffins. He has communicated only through written statements. The March 2026 IRIB video (teaching a religious class; assessed by analysts as archival footage) remains the only video-format release; there has been no post-injury audio or video. The Playbook hypothesis of a Day 3–5 audio release remains live — funeral Days 4–5 (July 7–8) are the remaining window.

**On the Ground in Iran:** The July 6 procession is the largest public event in Iran since the 1989 Khomeini funeral; streets visibly packed in Tehran while regime propaganda broadcasts continuous martyrdom framing and Khamenei's "defiant clenched fist" imagery dominates state media. Simultaneously — per NCRI (⚠️ MEK-affiliated) and IranWire — underground resistance units continued operations during the procession, and Kurdish-province IranWire reports confirm ongoing IRGC security mobilization in West Azerbaijan following the July 1-2 Piranshahr/Sardasht clashes. Iran International and Radio Farda report that June inflation hit 88.6% YoY (83.9% in May; food category +130%), bread prices doubled in Tehran after the June 23 subsidy rollback, and minimum wages cover approximately 37% of the minimum household basket. The funeral's grief-and-martyrdom frame suppresses open economic protest for the duration — political scientists surveyed by Iran International describe this as the most intense single-week domestic mobilization moment the regime has attempted since the 1979 revolution, but note that the same economic pressure that motivated the 2025-26 protests (described as the largest since 1979) reasserts immediately after July 9. Dastgerd Prison (Isfahan) torture conditions reported by OHCHR-linked monitors (⚠️ single source).

Saudi Arabia has more than doubled Hormuz shipments since the June 17 MOU; UAE's ADNOC (outside OPEC since May 1) continues producing above pre-war quota toward its 5M bpd 2027 target.
