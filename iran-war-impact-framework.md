A Reuters/AP carry, or a US official confirming attendance, settles it.

**Q1 · Flows — the port series returns and prices the bypass precisely: the outside-Hormuz/inside-Hormuz loading ratio has inverted roughly five-fold since 2025.**
Week to Oct 23 (PortWatch): Jebel Ali tanker calls **6 vs 26 in 2025 (−77%)**, exports **226 vs 1,469 kt (−85%)**, imports 477 vs 3,048 kt. Fujairah tanker calls **37 vs 68 (−46%)**, exports **735 vs 987 kt (−26%)**, imports **657 vs 693 kt (−5%)**. Agent arithmetic: Fujairah exported **3.3×** Jebel Ali's tonnage this week against **0.67×** in 2025 — a ~5× inversion. Fujairah calls fell 40→37 while tonnage was flat, so parcel size rose 18.3→19.9 kt/call. **Q1's bear trigger (Fujairah below ~30 calls) is not met; the read holds.** The mechanism also acquired a size: Windward counts Gulf NOCs and affiliates assembling **~230 tankers, ~80% VLCCs, many with AIS switched off** ([MarineLink, Oct 29](https://www.marinelink.com/news/gulf-oil-producers-build-tanker-fleet-544013)). Inference (medium-high): ~184 VLCCs on 40-day Asia round trips ≈ **9 mb/d** of self-insured long-haul capacity — this is not a shuttle, it is a replacement merchant fleet. Confidence medium-high.
*Caution: Ras Tanura, Ras Laffan, Yanbu and Kharg print zero in **both** 2026 and 2025 columns this week — that is a coverage gap, not a shutdown. Do not read it as a collapse.*

**Q3 · Capital flows — Iranians are buying dollars and selling gold in the same session; that is distress liquidity, not classic hoarding.**
Cash dollar **2,721,100 (+0.39% d/d, new war low)**; Tether 2,726,460, basis **+0.2%** — fourth consecutive positive session but narrowing from +0.5%. Meanwhile Emami fell to **2,598M (−2.6% d/d)**, i.e. **−3.0% in dollars**, against world gold −1.7% ([CNBC, Oct 30](https://www.cnbc.com/amp/select/the-price-of-gold-today-oct-30-2026/)). Agent arithmetic: 18k at 254.1M/g implies **$3,872/oz fine, a 2.0% discount** to world; Emami's 7.32g fine content gives an intrinsic ~$930 against a $955 price — **a ~3% coin premium, far below the 10% panic trigger.** Read: if this were household flight you would see the coin bid *with* the dollar bid. You see the opposite. Inference (medium): the dollar/Tether bid is dominated by trade and settlement demand in a degraded payments channel, while households are liquidating gold to meet living costs at 73.6% inflation. Confidence medium; changes if the coin premium exceeds 10% while cash keeps rising.
