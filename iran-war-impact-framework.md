## Daily Briefing - 2026-08-13

**Overall situation:** Day 167 / Week 24. The Hormuz deadlock hardened on two fronts simultaneously: Iran's new military command unveiled an "offensive doctrine" restructuring around Supreme Leader Mojtaba Khamenei — adding six hardliner appointees to implement a strategy of direct action beyond Iranian borders — while Hormuz traffic dropped to a near three-month low as Trump again claimed "total control" of the strait and Tehran again flatly denied it. At the second chokepoint, a Houthi ballistic-missile strike on the vessel Tihama at Bab el-Mandeb killed four to six crew (reports vary), the second confirmed lethal Houthi shipping strike in 72 hours and the highest-casualty single incident since Aug 11. Saudi Arabia is now actively weighing whether to back a Yemeni ground campaign to retake the Red Sea coast from the Houthis.

**What changed in the last 24 hours:**

1. **Iran announces new "offensive doctrine" with six hardliner military appointments** ([Samaa TV](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UO89tQnVg_M), Aug 13): IRGC General Mohammad Reza Naqdi announced that Supreme Leader Mojtaba Khamenei has directed a shift from a defensive military posture to an "offensive doctrine" aimed at taking direct action against enemy territory. Six veteran military and security figures were appointed to implement the strategy. ⚠️ Single-sourced via Pakistani broadcaster; independent confirmation not yet in the pack. If accurate, this is the most significant formal doctrinal shift of the conflict and the first confirmed exercise of Mojtaba's authority at the strategic-military level — directly challenging the US-Israeli intel assessment that he is incapacitated.

2. **Hormuz traffic falls to near three-month low; US-Iran opposing claims harden** ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/13/us-iran-war-trump-hormuz-irgc.html), Aug 13; [Al Jazeera liveblog](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/liveblog/2026/8/13/iran-war-live-us-and-iran-exchange-opposing-claims-over-hormuz-control), Aug 13 — double-sourced): Trump again insisted the US has "total control" of the Strait of Hormuz; Tehran remained resolute that the strait is closed until US conditions are met. Traffic dropped to the lowest level in roughly three months. Trading Economics (Aug 12) confirmed Brent fluctuating near $89/bbl as investors "weighed conflicting signals," with Pakistan's defense minister saying Washington and Tehran are "close to some sort of arrangement" — a framing that, as yesterday, lacks Iranian institutional backing.

3. **Houthi ballistic missile kills four to six crew on MV Tihama at Bab el-Mandeb** ([The Jerusalem Post / Reuters](https://www.jpost.com/middle-east/article-905168), Aug 12; [The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/news/gulf/2026/08/13/why-are-the-houthis-escalating-attacks-on-saudi-backed-targets), Aug 13 — double-sourced): Three Pakistanis and one Indonesian killed according to Saudi state-owned Al Hadath (Reuters confirmed with two Yemeni coast guard sources); a separate coast guard account put the toll at six dead and ten wounded. Three successive missiles struck the vessel in Bab el-Mandeb. The Houthis did not immediately claim responsibility for this specific vessel, though the pattern is consistent with their stated Saudi-port blockade. Saudi Arabia is now weighing support for a Yemeni ground offensive to retake the Houthi-held Red Sea coast — the first reported consideration of a significant escalation at the second chokepoint.

4. **Saudi Arabia considers backing Yemeni ground push against Houthis** ([The Jerusalem Post / The Media Line](https://www.jpost.com/middle-east/article-905414), Aug 13): Saudi Arabia is weighing whether to back a Yemeni ground campaign by assembled militias to retake the Red Sea coast from the Houthis. The Houthis claim 29 Saudi tankers have been prevented from passing or forced to turn back since the July 20 blockade declaration; eight Saudi tankers targeted since then (Anadolu Agency, Aug 5). Yanbu ships ~4 million bpd; a Houthi drone claimed to strike the Aramco refinery at Jazan on Aug 9. A Saudi ground-support decision would be the largest Saudi kinetic escalation of the conflict.

**Corrections:** No prior briefing facts require correction today. The Mojtaba "offensive doctrine" claim (Item 1) is flagged single-sourced pending independent confirmation and will be re-verified or dropped tomorrow per evidence rules.
