(Iran War Fuel Crisis Wikipedia, BBC Persian context)

- Iranian food imports (soybeans, meat) heavily dependent on Persian Gulf routes Iran cannot substitute via land corridors; regime has lost the financial capacity to maintain large-scale food imports at pre-war levels. (CFR, IFPRI)
- ⚠️ No verified reports today of fresh civilian protests or new internal political crises; last major protest wave peaked January 2026. Regime crackdown (live fire, thousands killed) appears to have suppressed visible unrest. Pressure building structurally via shortages and blackouts but no street-level action confirmed today. (BBC Persian context, Al Jazeera)
