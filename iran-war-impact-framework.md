## Model cross-check

- **The Ras Tanura claim is load-bearing and rests on Argus via one outlet, plus a FIRMS signature relayed through a Facebook summary of Maritime Executive.** The coalition has confirmed Jizan and the Jeddah interception but, in what I can see, has not confirmed Ras Tanura damage. The independent check I trust most is the Brent tape: a $5.32 intraday range and a 3.69% settle on the day is not what a denied, unconfirmed strike produces. If Aramco publishes nothing and next week's Ras Tanura print holds near 109 kt, I have over-weighted it.
- **My Emami bubble arithmetic contradicts alanchand's and I have not reconciled the methods.** tgju's 18k gram may embed dealer markup that NerkhAra's ounce-and-dollar construction does not. I used the direction only, which is the defensible use, but two gold-premium series running 5pp apart is the same structural error that produced Monday's Brent mistake. Flagged for resolution next run.
- **Against my own Q6 call.** "Home Front guidance unchanged" is a statement by the same spokesman making the readiness claim, so using it as the independent control is weaker than it reads. The alert count (1 in seven days) is genuinely independent; the guidance line is not.
- **The halving arithmetic on the rial is an extrapolation of a one-week rate and should not be read as a forecast.** Iranian FX has mean-reverted hard before. Its value is as a boundary: if the current rate persists for eight weeks it becomes a different kind of event, and nothing in the pack suggests a policy instrument that would stop it.
- **Watch for contradiction next run:** whether Aramco or the coalition concedes Ras Tanura damage; whether Kharg prints a second week; the Nov 27–28 Islamabad SPDC against Fidan's non-automatic language and Güler's no-combat line; whether the Nov 26 Israeli cabinet publishes anything; and whether Brent holds above $94 into the Nov 29 OPEC+ ministerial.
