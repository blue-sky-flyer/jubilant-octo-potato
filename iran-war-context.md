### The "Project Freedom" Strategic Architecture (from May 4)

**The Omani Waters Legal Trap.** Routing convoys along the Omani coast places US-escorted vessels in waters where Iran has the weakest jurisdictional claim — far from the mid-strait "maritime regime" Iran asserts. Any IRGC interdiction of a convoy in Omani near-territorial waters is simultaneously: (1) an attack on US warships; (2) a violation of Oman's sovereignty — and Oman is the primary US-Iran diplomatic back-channel host, so Iran would be destroying its own mediator; (3) a loss of Chinese diplomatic cover (China cannot maintain "neutral mediator" positioning if Iran attacks neutral-country waters); (4) unambiguous Iranian aggressor status under international law, giving the US and any coalition partner full legal justification to respond with overwhelming force. This mirrors the legal and strategic positioning that preceded Operation Praying Mantis in 1988.

**Trump's Dealmaker Incentive Structure.** $4.65/gal US gas is Trump's single largest domestic political liability — he ran on cheap energy and the war produced the opposite. His "47 years of torment" framing is a transactional bid, not a commitment to regime destruction: it sets the psychological floor so high that any deal Trump accepts looks like a historic reckoning, allowing him to claim "I did in 3 months what nobody could do in 47 years." He needs a deal that *looks* transformative — formal Hormuz reopening under international law (not Iran's "maritime regime"), visible nuclear constraints, some proxy curtailment — not one that actually destroys the regime. Project Freedom is the coercive mechanism to force that deal, not a precursor to a new sustained air campaign. "Very positive discussions" running concurrently = the off-ramp Iran can take at any moment.

**The Praying Mantis Scenario.** Trump may be deliberately recreating Reagan's 1988 template: apply coercive pressure (naval escorts) → limited IRGC face-saving provocation → decisive but proportionate US naval response (destroy IRGC assets, possibly gunboats or a frigate) → offer Iran the deal from a position of demonstrated naval dominance → Iran accepts framework. This scenario simultaneously: humbles the regime visibly (satisfying "47 years"), drops oil sharply on resolution news (satisfying his domestic political need), and gives Trump a historic foreign policy win. It does not require resuming the full air campaign — a one-day naval action like Praying Mantis (US destroyed one-third of Iran's surface fleet in 24 hours in 1988) achieves the same psychological effect.
