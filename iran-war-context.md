## KNOWN FACTUAL CORRECTIONS — Read before writing any section

### Qatar LNG and force majeure
QatarEnergy declared **force majeure on ALL LNG export agreements on March 29-30, 2026**. Qatar's full contracted LNG supply is offline. Do NOT write that "Qatar's Cape-route LNG fleet is functional" as if Qatar is actively exporting at scale — it is not. A small number of Qatari LNG tankers that were outside the Gulf at war outbreak may continue on spot/residual cargoes, but this is not meaningful export capacity. The primary Qatar LNG impact is LOSS of supply to Europe and Asia, not rerouting.

### Three chokepoints — geography is critical
These three chokepoints are **mutually exclusive routes**, not the same route:

- **Hormuz** — controls exit from the Persian Gulf. Primary blockade. Everything inside the Gulf (Qatar LNG at Ras Laffan, Gulf crude) is blocked here first.
- **Bab el-Mandeb** — controls entry to the Red Sea from the Arabian Sea, between Yemen and Djibouti. This is already Houthi-threatened. Activating it closes the Red Sea/Suez Canal route.
- **Cape of Good Hope route** — goes around the southern tip of Africa, bypassing the Red Sea entirely. Ships take this route specifically to AVOID Bab el-Mandeb. Ships on Cape route do NOT pass through Bab el-Mandeb.

**Critical rule:** Bab el-Mandeb and the Cape route are mutually exclusive alternatives. If a ship is on the Cape route, Bab el-Mandeb is irrelevant to it. Never write that Bab el-Mandeb activation "threatens" ships on the Cape route — it does not.

**Never write:** "Bab el-Mandeb activation threatens Qatar's Cape-route LNG fleet" — this is geographically incoherent.

**Correct framing for Bab el-Mandeb activation:** It would close the Red Sea/Suez route for ships coming from Asia and non-Gulf regions that are still using Suez, forcing another wave of vessels onto the longer Cape route, compounding global shipping costs and transit times. It does not affect ships already on Cape route.

**Correct framing for Qatar LNG:** Qatar declared force majeure on all LNG contracts (March 29-30). Its contracted supply is offline. The European energy crisis impact is a SUPPLY LOSS, not a rerouting story.

### The "Project Freedom" Strategic Architecture (from May 4)

**The Omani Waters Legal Trap.** Routing convoys along the Omani coast places US-escorted vessels in waters where Iran has the weakest jurisdictional claim — far from the mid-strait "maritime regime" Iran asserts. Any IRGC interdiction of a convoy in Omani near-territorial waters is simultaneously: (1) an attack on US warships; (2) a violation of Oman's sovereignty — and Oman is the primary US-Iran diplomatic back-channel host, so Iran would be destroying its own mediator; (3) a loss of Chinese diplomatic cover (China cannot maintain "neutral mediator" positioning if Iran attacks neutral-country waters); (4) unambiguous Iranian aggressor status under international law, giving the US and any coalition partner full legal justification to respond with overwhelming force. This mirrors the legal and strategic positioning that preceded Operation Praying Mantis in 1988.

**Trump's Dealmaker Incentive Structure.** $4.65/gal US gas is Trump's single largest domestic political liability — he ran on cheap energy and the war produced the opposite. His "47 years of torment" framing is a transactional bid, not a commitment to regime destruction: it sets the psychological floor so high that any deal Trump accepts looks like a historic reckoning, allowing him to claim "I did in 3 months what nobody could do in 47 years." He needs a deal that *looks* transformative — formal Hormuz reopening under international law (not Iran's "maritime regime"), visible nuclear constraints, some proxy curtailment — not one that actually destroys the regime. Project Freedom is the coercive mechanism to force that deal, not a precursor to a new sustained air campaign. "Very positive discussions" running concurrently = the off-ramp Iran can take at any moment.

**The Praying Mantis Scenario.** Trump may be deliberately recreating Reagan's 1988 template: apply coercive pressure (naval escorts) → limited IRGC face-saving provocation → decisive but proportionate US naval response (destroy IRGC assets, possibly gunboats or a frigate) → offer Iran the deal from a position of demonstrated naval dominance → Iran accepts framework. This scenario simultaneously: humbles the regime visibly (satisfying "47 years"), drops oil sharply on resolution news (satisfying his domestic political need), and gives Trump a historic foreign policy win. It does not require resuming the full air campaign — a one-day naval action like Praying Mantis (US destroyed one-third of Iran's surface fleet in 24 hours in 1988) achieves the same psychological effect.

**Market Interpretation — Destination vs. Path.** The market pricing WTI at ~$99 on Project Freedom news may correctly price the *30–60 day destination*: Trump's incentives make some form of resolution likely, and his creativity in finding coercive-but-deniable mechanisms (Omani routing, "not an escort mission" framing, "war over" legal declaration) is real. What VIX at ~17 underprices is the *5–10 day execution window*: IRGC hardliner Vahidi cannot be seen to silently accept US warship-escorted convoys transiting past IRGC gunboats — Khamenei's May 1 "permanent Hormuz retention" declaration makes quiet capitulation politically impossible within the regime. Some face-saving IRGC response is probable; each such response is a trigger point under the live "shoot and kill" ROE.

**The IRGC Trilemma.** Vahidi's military track faces three paths, none comfortable: (A) *Silent acceptance* — convoys transit freely; Iran's "maritime regime" is exposed as unenforceable; Khamenei's declaration humiliated within days of being made; politically impossible for the hardliner track; (B) *Direct confrontation in Omani waters* — triggers Praying Mantis response; Iran loses a significant portion of remaining naval assets; US has full legal and coalition justification; (C) *Face-saving symbolic response* — mine-laying ahead of convoy routes, Kilo-class submarine positioning near escort destroyers, IRGC gunboats shadowing at close range, GPS jamming — tests US ROE without triggering full escalation. Path C is most likely IRGC choice, but each Path C action is a potential "shoot and kill" ROE execution trigger. The sequence: Path C → US ROE execution → Iran escalation decision → deal or war.

**Key Unknown.** Whether the "very positive discussions" diplomatic track (Araghchi channel) can produce a framework agreement *before* the IRGC hardliner track makes its Path C response. If Araghchi can get a deal signed in the next 5–10 days, Vahidi is defanged. If the IRGC acts first, the deal becomes much harder to reach even if both sides ultimately want one.
