# Iran War — Standing Sections
*Slow-moving sections of the daily briefing live here, in full. The daily briefing carries ONE bullet + link per section, and reprints a section in full only on the day it materially changes. Each header carries a "last materially changed" date so readers can see staleness at a glance.*

**Update protocol (daily agent):** For each section below, ask one question each run: *did anything material change in the last 24 hours?* If no — touch nothing here, and the briefing bullet reads "unchanged since [date]". If yes — update the section here, bump its date, and summarize the delta in the briefing's "What changed" list. Never copy a full section back into the briefing.

## US Military Posture & Munitions
*Last materially changed: 2026-11-09 (the three-carrier claim contradicted by the Roosevelt's reported position and relief role; the president names bridges and power plants himself; the Nov 9 window expires)*

**Nov 9 UPDATE — the hull count is rhetorical, and the target list is now stated by the principal:**

- **"I've got three aircraft carriers here" (Nov 8–9):** in a 20-minute CNN interview Trump said renewed strikes were "highly unlikely" if Tehran signs "a very strong agreement," repeating the point five times, and added: "We're ready for it. I've got three aircraft carriers here. The other one just arrived. We have three. USNI News reports the Roosevelt would **replace** *George Washington*, which reached the region on Aug 19; *George H.W. Bush* is at **220 days deployed** ([TWZ Nov 6](https://www.twz.com/sea/uss-theodore-roosevelt-arrives-in-singapore-last-stop-before-third-strike-group-reaches-the-middle-east); [Marine Insight Nov 9](https://www.marineinsight.com/three-us-aircraft-carriers-middle-east-theodore-roosevelt)). **Agent arithmetic and read: the operative deck count today is two — George Washington and George H.W. Bush — exactly as it has been since Oct 6, and the steady state after the relief may also be two. The end-November three-CSG package that has organised this file since Oct 20 is now in question as a simultaneous presence rather than a rotation. This resolves itself by mid-November without new reporting; until then, treat "three carriers" as a declaratory figure, not a posture fact.**
- **The principal names civil infrastructure (Nov 8):** in the same interview Trump said the US has identified **38 bridges inside Iran** that could be destroyed within an hour, "along with power plants that could be targeted if fighting resumes" (Iran International Nov 8). **Read: every prior naming of civil energy and transport targets in this file — the Oct 14–15 CNN MOAB reporting, the Oct 21 FT "power plants" category, the Ynet account of a planned Israeli operation — was single-originator leak material and was never adopted into scenario weights. It is now stated on the record by the commander-in-chief. That is a declaratory reversal of the July 9 Kharg doctrine, under which CENTCOM struck Kharg's military targets and deliberately preserved export infrastructure. It is not evidence of a target list in execution, and it is cheap to say; but the category can no longer be described as unattributed.**
- **The capability claim, attributed not adopted (Nov 8):** Trump said Iran retains "about 10% of its missile capacity and 15% of its drone capabilities" after US strikes destroyed much of its military production infrastructure, and that Tehran is "withering away financially" (Iran International Nov 8). **This is irreconcilable with the ISW May baseline of ~70% of pre-war stockpiles, with the Oct 10 Haaretz report of IDF officials assessing that Iran has replenished much of its ballistic missile stockpile and is accelerating production, and with Iran's own >75% claim. All four are claims. Logged as a presidential claim; not adopted into scenario weights, and not used to revise the munitions-exchange arithmetic.**
- **The Nov 9 window expires (Nov 9):** the new-moon date flagged by [WSWS (Nov 7)](https://www.wsws.org/en/articles/2026/11/07/jnxh-n07.html) and logged not adopted on Nov 8 has passed with no reported strike on Iranian territory. **Retired. The standing working frame is unchanged: a prepared package with no scheduled execution, held against a live but currently immobile negotiation; probability of a major strike within 14 days held at ~25%.**
- **Iran rejects the land file (Nov 8):** IRGC spokesman Hossein Mohebbi told Fars that "the Iron Gate plan is merely a wordplay, the Americans cannot achieve their objectives of imposing severe economic pressure to cause economic collapse in Iran through this approach," saying Tehran has made preparations and communicates closely with neighbours ([JPost/Reuters Nov 8](https://www.jpost.com/middle-east/iran-news/article-914180)). **Attribute as an Iranian claim. It is, however, directionally consistent with this file's own Nov 8 inference that a 20-person coordination staff in Tampa is a designations cell rather than interdiction capacity.**

**Nov 8 UPDATE (carried) — the blockade acquires a land dimension, and the sea ledger slows:**

- **Task Force Iron Gate (Nov 7):** Adm. Brad Cooper directed the establishment of an internal CENTCOM staff of **approximately 20 civilian and military personnel based in Tampa, Florida**, to coordinate with other US government agencies — expected to include Treasury, State, Justice and the intelligence community per a US military official — on "preventing the movement of advanced weapons systems, dual-use technologies, sanctioned materials, and illegal revenue streams across Iran's land borders and the Caspian Sea." Cooper framed it as reinforcing "the success of our ironclad blockade against Iran at sea," and CENTCOM repeated the **$25bn of prevented crude exports** figure ([CENTCOM Nov 7](https://www.centcom.mil/MEDIA/PUBLIC-RELEASES/Article/4644250/centcom-launches-task-force-to-disrupt-iranian-ground-transit-routes-supporting); [Reuters Nov 7](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/expanding-naval-blockade-us-military-looks-restrict-iranian-land-commerce-2026-11-07); [JPost Nov 7](https://www.jpost.com/middle-east/iran-news/article-914130); [Al Jazeera Nov 8](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/8/us-launches-task-force-to-disrupt-irans-land-based-supply-routes)). **Read: the reason for the task force is in the same Reuters story — an investigation finding Iran built a secret network of military agencies importing at least $2bn in hardware, explosives and heavy-duty trucks since January 2025, by cargo ship across the Caspian from Russia, by truck from Central Asia and by freight plane from China, evading both the naval blockade and Western sanctions. Agent inference (medium): a 20-person coordination staff in Florida is a designations and interagency-targeting cell, not interdiction capacity. Its measurable output will be OFAC and State designations against third-country truckers, Caspian shippers and air-freight operators, on the Oct 8 shadow-fleet template. It does not close a border and it cannot board a truck.**
- **The sea ledger, revised and slowing (Nov 7):** after disabling the **St Kitts-flagged M/V Huda** in the Gulf of Oman — a US aircraft put precision munitions into the stern after the crew ignored repeated warnings, having departed an Iranian port — CENTCOM states that enforcement since July has produced **147 commercial vessels redirected, five merchant ships disabled, and a previously destroyed 10 Iranian commercial oil tankers** ([CENTCOM Nov 7](https://www.centcom.mil/MEDIA/PUBLIC-RELEASES/Article/4644273/us-forces-disable-cargo-vessel-attempting-to-violate-naval-blockade)). **Agent arithmetic: redirections ran 130 (Oct 5) → 135 (Oct 12) → 147 (Nov 7) = 12 in 26 days, 0.46/day, against 0.7/day over Oct 5–12 and 1.0/day over Sep 30–Oct 5. The destroyed count has not moved in 26 days. Read: either far fewer hulls are attempting the run, or enforcement has been deliberately throttled during the Qatari-mediated negotiation. The second is the better fit, because the attack rate on commercial shipping rose over the same period. The Oct 12 caution stands — these counters are re-stated rather than strictly accumulated.**
- **Mutual enforcement on one calendar day (Nov 7):** 21 minutes after the *Huda* strike, the IRGC announced that two oil tankers had struck mines and caught fire in the Strait of Hormuz; daily Hormuz movements are reported at roughly **31 under American escort against approximately 138 pre-war** per the Joint Maritime Information Center, with brokers cited by the Financial Times putting war risk at **6–10% of hull value** ([House of Saud Nov 8](https://houseofsaud.com/hormuz-mutual-enforcement-nov7)) — single carrier for the FT and JMIC figures, logged. **Attribute the mine claim as Iranian. Read: this is the first day of the war on which both navies announced disabling commercial vessels in overlapping waters under rival legal frameworks. The war-risk figure, if it holds, is a widening from the 3–8% standing range and is the cleanest commercial evidence that the de-escalation rally in crude has not reached the insurance market.**

It belongs beside the Oct 16 ISIS finding that Pickaxe Mountain is sealed and unobserved from inside.**

- **Agent inference (medium-high) — the attrition loop.** Nov 2 was a counter-battery response to an IRGC SAM launch at a US aircraft; Nov 6 destroyed a newly built anti-aircraft system on the same island. The pattern is Iranian reconstitution of air-defence coverage over the transit corridor, met by rapid, cheap, unannounced removal. Each cycle costs the US a handful of munitions and no political capital, and costs Iran a launcher. **This substitutes for the prepared three-day package rather than preceding it: it protects the air umbrella over the tanker corridor, which is the status quo the president described on Nov 3 as "getting everything we want." The standing working frame — a prepared package with no scheduled execution — is unchanged; the probability of a major strike within 14 days is held at ~25%.**
