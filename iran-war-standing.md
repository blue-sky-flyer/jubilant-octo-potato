## Strategic Reserve Countdown — US Endurance Ceiling (Day 0)
*Last materially changed: 2026-09-12 (Saudi East-West pipeline shutdown + Bab el-Mandeb near-zero = simultaneous three-pathway Saudi export blockage — most severe Saudi supply disruption of conflict; EIA raises 2027 US crude production to 14.3 mb/d — 2027 relief, no 2026 impact; Monday Sep 14 Oman GCC-Iran meeting is primary near-term SPR clock variable)*

**One-line arithmetic (updated Sep 12):** SPR **286.6M bbl** (EIA w/e Aug 28, GEF Sep 10) minus political floor ~200M bbl = ~86.6M bbl usable buffer. At the **3.7M bbl weekly draw** rate, political floor arrives approximately **~23 weeks → Day 0 approximately early-to-mid February 2027**. At a 3.1M bbl/week pace (two-week trend Aug 14–28), Day 0 slides to ~early March 2027. **Operative range: early January – early March 2027.** *(Low-to-moderate confidence on precise timing.)*

**Sep 12 update — Saudi three-pathway export blockage; EIA 2027 production raise; Oman Monday meeting:**

- **Saudi Arabia's three export pathways simultaneously impaired (Sep 12, new):** (1) Hormuz — ~2 vessels/day; (2) Bab el-Mandeb — ~1 vessel/day, Houthis control Mayun Island; (3) East-West pipeline to Yanbu — shutdown, attack confirmed Iraq-originating (AJ Sep 12). This is the first time all three Saudi export pathways have been simultaneously impaired in the conflict. The duration of the pipeline shutdown is the most consequential unknown: a precautionary-only closure (no physical damage) could reverse within days; confirmed infrastructure damage extends the export blockage by weeks to months. Saudi Arabia's inability to export at scale tightens global supply further, accelerating the SPR draw rate beyond the 3.7M bbl/week baseline if US and IEA reserves continue to compensate.

- **EIA raises 2027 US crude production forecast to 14.3 mb/d (TradingEconomics Sep 12):** This is a 2027 relief signal — it does not affect the 2026 SPR draw trajectory. It confirms the US shale production base can expand to partially compensate for Persian Gulf supply loss, but only after the critical 2026 clock window has already elapsed.

- **Monday Sep 14 Oman meeting as primary SPR clock variable:** A "temporary arrangement" for Hormuz shipping management — even a limited one — would be the single most consequential near-term relief event for the SPR draw rate. Even partial Hormuz normalization (from ~2 vessels/day toward 20–30/day) would reduce the rate of IEA and US strategic reserve drawdown. The Oman meeting outcome (Monday Sep 14, 48 hours from now) is therefore the nearest-term binary for the SPR clock.

- **IEA Sep 11 OMR supply/demand signals (carried):** IEA forecasts 2.5 mb/d demand contraction 2026 (TradingEconomics/IEA summary). Demand contraction partially offsets supply tightening in SPR draw arithmetic: lower global demand means refiners process less crude, potentially slowing draw rate even as supply tightens. The IEA's simultaneous inventory-depletion warning ("buffers shrinking") indicates supply disruption is currently dominating the demand-destruction effect.

- **China crude import recovery signal (Sep 12, carried):** IEA Sep OMR: China on track to import same volume Sep as Aug, "recovering from June's decade-low" (TradingEconomics/IEA summary). ⚠️ Single-sourced (TradingEconomics/IEA); not independently confirmed by Kpler/Vortexa in pack. If confirmed, partially offsets supply-side deterioration by keeping Chinese demand for non-Gulf crude stable, reducing pressure on Atlantic Basin supply that US/IEA reserves have been compensating for.

**Prior data points (carried):**
- Commercial crude BUILD of 17.422M bbl (GEF Sep 10, w/e Aug 28) — demand-destruction signal; does not alter strategic draw trajectory.
- Salt cavern structural risk (Fortune Aug 17): drawdown threatens physical damage to 60 underground caverns; DOE safe operating minimum ~70M bbl (⚠️ directionally confirmed).
- IEA August OMR: 2026 demand −1.6 mb/d; global supply 101.5 mb/d in July, 6.3 mb/d below year-ago; 8.3 mb/d Gulf output still shut in; full-year supply shortfall 4.3 mb/d. 290M bbl released by IEA members since Mar 11.
