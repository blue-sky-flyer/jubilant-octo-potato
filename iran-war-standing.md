## US Military Posture & Munitions
*Last materially changed: 2026-08-12 (CENTCOM strike-status correction)*

- **CENTCOM strike status: PAUSED.** The most recent official CENTCOM strike release is **July 22** (confirming 12 consecutive nights of strikes through that date — maritime capabilities, missile/drone storage, coastal surveillance, air defense). **No official release or dated independent confirmation documents any US strike on Iranian territory after July 22.** Aug 10 reporting (CNN; Haass) confirms the administration deliberately "backed off renewed hostilities," citing depleted munitions stocks, in favor of economic pressure ("low-keying"). Prior briefings describing "rolling/nightly CENTCOM strikes" as ongoing into August were extrapolating from the stale July 22 release — corrected Aug 12.
- **Blockade enforcement remains active** (distinct from strikes on Iran): 48 commercial vessels redirected, 2 disabled, 2 boarded as of Aug 5 (UANI); US forces fired on a blockade-running cargo vessel Aug 11 (Al Jazeera).
- **Munitions:** ~2,080 Tomahawks expended (~$3.9B restock; 18–24 mo lead time; Pentagon rationing to preserve Indo-Pacific reserve); key missile interceptors low (CNN, Aug 4); UAE/Kuwait ~75% of Patriot stocks spent; Pentagon asked industry to ramp production (Aug 10). Jul 12 CENTCOM release confirmed first combat use of one-way attack sea drones (novel-munition debut consistent with the combat-as-qualification framework).
- **Posture:** three CSGs + SSGN + Boxer ARG/11th MEU; 50,000+ troops in theater.
- **Kinetic-window projection** (structural analysis, Aug 10): primary window mid-Oct 2026 – Feb 2027; secondary Apr–May 2027 (SPR floor); Israeli unilateral action is the Sep–Oct wildcard. Precursor watch list in [iran-war-context.md](https://github.com/blue-sky-flyer/jubilant-octo-potato/blob/main/iran-war-context.md).

## Strategic Reserve Countdown — US Endurance Ceiling (Day 0)
*Last materially changed: 2026-08-13 (SPR revised to ~304.8M bbl per GEF Aug 11 update)*

**One-line arithmetic:** SPR ~304.8M bbl (GEF, updated Aug 11 — 2.841M bbl draw that week, fresh multi-decade low) minus political floor ~200M bbl = ~104.8M bbl usable buffer ÷ ~0.38M bbl/day observed draw = **~276 days → Day 0 approximately mid-May 2027.**

Note: the large US commercial crude inventory BUILD of 17.4M bbl (EIA, week reported Aug 12 — largest since early 2023) is a domestic commercial stock event, not an SPR event, and does not alter the strategic draw trajectory. It may reflect demand destruction and refinery run reductions consistent with IEA's −1.6 mb/d demand downgrade. SPR draw continues independently of commercial builds. IEA (Aug 12): 2026 demand now projected −1.6 mb/d (from −1.0 in July); global supply 101.5 mb/d in July, 6.3 mb/d below year-ago; full-year supply shortfall averaging 4.3 mb/d — largest peacetime shortfall in agency history. Political floor ~200M bbl is a consensus estimate, not confirmed by Treasury/DOE. Day 0 = the outer bound of US strategic patience; the Oct 2026–Feb 2027 kinetic window arrives before it. *(Low-to-moderate confidence.)*

## Iranian State Disintegration Tracker — Iran's Endurance Ceiling
*Last materially changed: 2026-08-13 (offensive doctrine restructuring claim, single-sourced)*

**Economic (confirmed):** IMF −5.4% GDP projection 2026 (NCRI citing Jahan-e Sanat/IMF, Jul 11); war damage ~$270B (~90% pre-war GDP); inflation 88.6%+ (rural >100%); $27B capital flight 2025 vs $1.676B FDI; autos −47%, washing machines −42%, TVs −36%, petrochemicals −17%; approved foreign investment −80%; 20M+ bbl stranded afloat; China imports halved to 654K bpd (June); TGL revoked (dark-fleet channel only). Trump's "difficulty paying troops" (Axios, Aug 10) is an unverified US-government claim — state-media pay-raise reports contested; treat as disputed.

**Military reconstitution:** Iran **claims** >75% of pre-war missile/drone capability retained (CBS citing Iranian state sources, Aug 12 — **Iranian claim, not independent confirmation**; independent baseline: ISW May ~70% stockpiles; retention likely reflects pre-war stockpile, not replenishment — launcher and production-infrastructure losses documented). 30/33 Hormuz missile sites restored (ISW, May). Air defenses rebuilding with Chinese QW-12/FN-16 and Russian Verba MANPADS; heavier Chinese systems reportedly staged via third countries; Shahed airframes returned from Russia with upgrades.

**Doctrinal/command restructuring (⚠️ SINGLE-SOURCED — Aug 13):** IRGC General Mohammad Reza Naqdi announced Aug 13 that Supreme Leader Mojtaba Khamenei has directed a shift from defensive to "offensive doctrine," with six hardliner military/security figures appointed to implement a strategy of direct action against enemy territory (Samaa TV, Aug 13). If confirmed by an independent source, this would be: (a) the first confirmed exercise of Mojtaba's authority at the strategic-military level, directly challenging the US-Israeli intel assessment of incapacitation; and (b) a doctrinal hardening that forecloses near-term pragmatist diplomatic space. **To be re-verified Aug 14 — confirm with independent source or flag as Iranian information operation.**

**Political cohesion:** IRGC troika controls all levers — Vahidi (kinetic), Zolghadr (SNSC gate), Rezaei (national security chief; his Aug 12 formal Hormuz-closure declaration to China's ambassador is the strongest institutional exercise of that authority to date). Succession vacuum: Mojtaba's status uncertain — US-Israeli intel assessed incapacitation (no independent proof-of-life through Day 166); Pezeshkian's Aug 11 "perfect health" attestation is an unverified government claim. The Aug 13 "offensive doctrine" announcement, if genuine, would be the first strategic-military attribution to Mojtaba's direction. No confirmed elite defections. Pragmatist track (Oman single-corridor talks) directly undercut by Rezaei's Aug 12 SNSC declaration.

**Trajectory:** managed attrition trending toward a tipping point in **Q1–Q2 2027** (same window as the US SPR floor) — a fiscal forcing function (troop pay, rial, food/fuel) triggering elite bargaining, not near-term popular collapse. Hardliner consolidation makes a sudden-crisis endpoint likelier than a negotiated offramp. ⚠️ NCRI data is MEK-affiliated — directionally indicative only.

## Red Sea / Bab el-Mandeb — Baseline
*Last materially changed: 2026-08-13 (MV Tihama fatalities; Saudi ground-push deliberations)*

Houthi Saudi-port naval blockade in force since Jul 20; ~40% tanker-transit drop since (JMIC). Eight Saudi tankers targeted since Jul 22; 29 Saudi tankers reported prevented or turned back (Houthi claim, Anadolu Agency, Aug 5 — unverified independently). 

**Aug 12–13 escalation:** Houthi ballistic missile strike on MV Tihama in Bab el-Mandeb killed four to six crew (three Pakistanis and one Indonesian per Saudi Al Hadath/Reuters, confirmed by two Yemeni coast guard sources and two government military officials; coast guard put toll at six dead, ten wounded — double-sourced, JPost Aug 12 / The National Aug 13). Three successive missiles struck the vessel. This follows the Aug 11 fatality incident (4–6 crew killed on Egyptian cargo vessel) reported yesterday. Two lethal Houthi shipping strikes in 72 hours represents the highest-intensity cluster at Bab el-Mandeb of the conflict.

**Saudi ground-push deliberations (new Aug 13):** Saudi Arabia is weighing whether to back a Yemeni ground campaign to retake the Red Sea coast from the Houthis, using militias assembled over seven months (The Jerusalem Post/The Media Line, Aug 13). Yanbu ships ~4M bpd; Houthi drone claimed to strike the Aramco refinery at Jazan Aug 9 (unverified independently). A Saudi ground-support decision would be the largest Saudi kinetic escalation of the conflict and the first attempt to roll back the Houthi Red Sea position since the war began.

Cargo ship sunk by unmanned surface vessel Aug 4 (UKMTO/UANI). Houthis cut off from Iranian resupply since ~Jul 4 (Stimson) — operating on stockpiles. Some Saudi tankers transit with AIS dark; others divert via Cape. War-risk ~0.75% hull. **Geography rule: the Cape route does not pass through Bab el-Mandeb — vessels on the Cape route are unaffected by Bab el-Mandeb activation.**

## On the Ground in Iran
*Last materially changed: 2026-08-13 (offensive doctrine claim; Mojtaba status contested)*

Domestic economic deterioration without break (see Disintegration Tracker for figures). Internet costs tripled; filtering intensified; social media restricted since the partial May 27 restoration. No confirmed large-scale protest activity in recent packs — underlying pressure structurally unresolved but unverifiable under suppression.

**Mojtaba status — contested as of Aug 13:** US-Israeli intel had assessed Mojtaba incapacitated (spinal lesions, brain trauma) with no proof-of-life through Day 166. Pezeshkian's Aug 11 claim of a seven-hour meeting with a healthy Supreme Leader was assessed as an information operation. The Aug 13 Samaa TV report attributing an "offensive doctrine" restructuring to Mojtaba Khamenei's direction (⚠️ single-sourced) is the first instance in the evidence pack of a named Iranian military figure attributing a strategic command decision directly to Mojtaba. If independently confirmed, it would require downgrading the incapacitation assessment. **To be re-verified Aug 14.**

IRGC bandwidth consumed by Hormuz enforcement, proxy coordination, air-defense rebuilding, and domestic security. Business Insider (Aug 13 context) notes nearly six months of war have hammered the Iranian economy — consistent with Disintegration Tracker figures.
