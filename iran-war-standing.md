## Iranian State Disintegration Tracker — Iran's Endurance Ceiling
*Last materially changed: 2026-09-19 (rial 2,279,000/USD and flat day-on-day; Tehran gold-coin premium ≈ zero and Tether at a 0.2% discount to the cash dollar — hard-asset scramble absent; internet and civil airspace stable through the Mahsa Amini anniversary)*

**Economic (confirmed, carried):** IMF −5.4% GDP projection 2026; war damage ~$270B (~90% pre-war GDP); inflation 88.6%+ (rural >100%); $27B capital flight 2025; autos −47%, petrochemicals −17%; approved foreign investment −80%; 20M+ bbl stranded afloat; China imports halved to 654K bpd (June); TGL revoked; dark-fleet channel only. Unemployment 9.1% (from 7.3%), 450,000 jobs lost.

**Sep 19 UPDATE — the hard-asset panic that should be present is not:**

- **Rial 2,279,000 IRR/USD, unchanged day-on-day (tgju, Sep 19).** Against the ~2.0M/USD cited in late August (RFE/RL Aug 25), that is ~14% depreciation in roughly three and a half weeks — steady erosion, no gap move.

- **Tehran gold-coin premium ≈ 0% (new, Sep 19 — agent arithmetic on tgju data):** Emami coin 2,320M IRR = ~$1,018 at the open-market rate. The coin contains 7.32g of fine gold; at world gold ~$4,310/oz that is ~$1,014 intrinsic. The 18k gram at 233.6M IRR = $102.5/g versus ~$104 world — a slight discount. Historically Iranian coin premiums ("hobab") have run 20–40% in acute stress. **Inference (medium confidence):** households liquidating gold to fund consumption are absorbing capital-flight demand; the full adjustment is running through the exchange rate rather than through a hard-asset scramble. This argues for a managed, grinding deterioration rather than an imminent monetary break.

- **Tether at a 0.2% discount to the cash dollar (2,273,750 IRR, tgju Sep 19):** No crypto exit premium. Relevant because US Treasury has now publicly tied a Tehran crypto exchange to the handling of part of Iran's $2M Hormuz passage fees ([eGamers/Treasury Sep 18](https://egamers.io/treasury-tehran-crypto-exchange-handled-part-of-irans-2-million-hormuz-passage-fees)) — sanctions exposure is rising on exactly the rail that a panic would use.

- **State functioning: no connectivity or aviation stress (IODA, OpenSky Sep 19):** 11,180 reachable /24s, above the prior six-day mean of 11,115; BGP prefixes stable at 41,674. Iranian civil ADS-B at 92 aircraft, identical to a week ago. The regime did not throttle the network for the Mahsa Amini anniversary despite reported shop strikes in western Iran ([Iran International Sep 16](https://www.iranintl.com/en/202609167981)). Either confidence in control or an unwillingness to bear the economic cost of a shutdown; the second is more consistent with the currency data.

- **Iran-Oman route agreement: signing did not occur Sep 14 (correction adopted Sep 17, carried):** Framework announced but unsigned. Pragmatist track weakened but structurally present.

- **Dual-track bifurcation status (carried from Sep 18):** Hardliner track reinforced by continuing IRGC maritime enforcement; Rezaei's "no talks with the US until demands met" remains the last confirmed SNSC position. No Iranian principal has confirmed Trump's "direct talks" claim.

**IAEA Sep 9 resolution and UNSC referral (confirmed, carried):** Board resolution adopted Sep 9; UNSC referral confirmed. Russia/China veto blocks enforcement.

**Military reconstitution:** Iran **claims** >75% of pre-war missile/drone capability retained (CBS, Aug 12 — **Iranian claim**). International reporting indicates Iran has resumed assembling ballistic missiles in underground facilities from pre-war components, at a reduced scale ([Alma Sep 16](https://israel-alma.org/the-iranian-arena-monthly-review-august-september-2026)). Chinese air defense equipment delivered (ISW Sep 15).

**Fleet attrition (carried):** Eight Iranian tankers struck/sunk Sep 5–8. Dark-fleet channel only for oil exports.

**Trajectory:** Tipping point Q1–Q2 2027 (operative baseline), unchanged. The Sep 19 currency and hard-asset data argue the near-term path is grinding erosion rather than discontinuity; nothing in today's data pulls the tipping point forward.

## Red Sea / Bab el-Mandeb — Baseline
*Last materially changed: 2026-09-19 (CORRECTION: "structural near-zero for commercial shipping" withdrawn — PortWatch and Lloyd's List both show Bab el-Mandeb functioning; Suez at its busiest since December 2023; Italy prepares national escorts)*

**CORRECTION (Sep 19):** The line carried in this section since Sep 14 — that Bab el-Mandeb is at "structural near-zero for commercial shipping" — was wrong and is withdrawn. Houthi territorial control of the Yemeni Red Sea coast, Mocha and Perim/Mayyun Island is confirmed and unchanged; control of the shoreline has not translated into closure of the waterway.

- **PortWatch, week to Sep 13:** Bab el-Mandeb **27.0 transits/day, of which 7.4 tankers, 405k DWT/day**. Same week 2025: 36 transits / 12 tankers / 0.67M DWT. Tanker DWT −39% vs 2025 and −11% week-on-week. Suez 36.6 transits/day, 15.2 tankers, 684k DWT/day — −16% vs 2025. For contrast, Hormuz tanker DWT is −99% vs 2025. The southern chokepoint is impaired, not closed.

- **Lloyd's List Intelligence, Red Sea Brief (Sep 18):** Suez transits totalled **1,232 in August, +28% YoY**, with tonnage +43% month on month — the busiest month for the canal since December 2023. Bab el-Mandeb recorded **1,165 voyages, +6% YoY but −49% month on month** (−14% by transit count and −19% by dwt on the alternative series). **44 ultra-large containerships have transited Bab el-Mandeb this year, 39 of them since July**, versus one in all of 2025. Total containership capacity through the strait has more than doubled YoY; activity remains 70% below pre-Houthi-crisis levels ([Lloyd's List Sep 18](https://www.lloydslistintelligence.com/resources/blog/red-sea-brief-18-september-2026)). **Read:** renewed Houthi threats against Saudi shipping have erased the traffic growth that the Hormuz crisis generated, but liner operators are still reintegrating the corridor. Saudi-affiliated transits are the segment actually being suppressed.

- **Italy to escort national tonnage independently (Sep 18, new — single-sourced):** Defence minister Crosetto said Rome will not wait for the EU's Aspides mission; planners are preparing a national protection framework for at least 10 Italian-flagged or Italian-owned ships waiting in the region ([Splash247 Sep 18](https://splash247.com/iran-claims-fresh-hormuz-tanker-strike-italy-readies-red-sea-escorts)).

- **Houthis say they will not attack US, Israeli or commercial vessels — except Saudi-linked (Reuters via Iran International Sep 16, carried):** US officials met Houthi representatives at the US Embassy in Muscat over the weekend of Sep 12–13, Oman facilitating; the delegation included Mohammad Abdulsalam and Abdelmalik al-Ajiri. The Houthis said they remain committed to the 2025 ceasefire with Washington and would exempt all shipping except Saudi vessels ([Iran International Sep 16](https://www.iranintl.com/en/202609169530)). **This is the mechanism that reconciles Houthi coastal control with continuing Suez/BEM throughput.**

Houthis deny. A confirmed strike on Mecca would force a direct Saudi military response.
