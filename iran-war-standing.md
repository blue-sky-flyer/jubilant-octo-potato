# Iran War — Standing Sections
*Slow-moving sections of the daily briefing live here, in full. The daily briefing carries ONE bullet + link per section, and reprints a section in full only on the day it materially changes. Each header carries a "last materially changed" date so readers can see staleness at a glance.*

**Update protocol (daily agent):** For each section below, ask one question each run: *did anything material change in the last 24 hours?* If no — touch nothing here, and the briefing bullet reads "unchanged since [date]". If yes — update the section here, bump its date, and summarize the delta in the briefing's "What changed" list. Never copy a full section back into the briefing.

## US Military Posture & Munitions
*Last materially changed: 2026-10-06 (USS George H.W. Bush leaves the Arabian Sea for a week's port call in Phuket, leaving one carrier within reach of Iran; Trump confirms the Fairford bomber withdrawal was threat-driven; CENTCOM logs its 130th vessel redirection and 13 vessels destroyed in 12 weeks)*

**Oct 6 UPDATE — one carrier on station, and the blockade ledger gets its first destruction count:**

- **George H.W. Bush is in Thailand (Oct 3–4, new):** the carrier arrived at Phuket for a one-week R&R call after about six months at sea, with ~4,800 crew aboard; analyst tracking placed her off Sumatra on Oct 3 and photographs show her entering port Oct 4. **Her departure from the Arabian Sea leaves only USS George Washington operating within reach of Iran**, with Theodore Roosevelt under way in the Pacific ([Maritime Executive Oct 4](https://maritime-executive.com/article/uss-george-h-w-bush-pulls-into-phuket-for-long-awaited-shore-leave)). **Agent inference (medium-high): the three-CSG end-November planning case is unchanged, but the *present* theatre is thinner than the headline count implies. A strike package generated in the next two weeks would rest on one carrier air wing plus CONUS global strike. This cuts the near-term tail further.**
- **Fairford, third-channel confirmation and attribution (Oct 4–5, new):** Maritime Executive independently reports that most of the 12-strong B-1B force at RAF Fairford flew home over the weekend, "reducing the U.S. Air Force's ability to conduct short-notice heavy bombardment in the Mideast," and that all suspects in the Sep 27 plot have been released pending investigation (Maritime Executive Oct 4). Trump has publicly confirmed a threat drove the withdrawal ([Anadolu Oct 5](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/world/trump-confirms-threat-led-us-to-pull-bombers-from-uk-base/4079035); [The Week Oct 6](https://www.theweek.in/news/middle-east/2026/10/06/the-real-reason-why-us-withdrew-strategic-bombers-from-uks-fairford-base.html)). **The Oct 5 read stands: this is the inverse of the early-strike indicator. A Diego Garcia rotation remains the only one left.**
- **Blockade ledger, Oct 5 (new — CENTCOM official release):** CENTCOM forces redirected the **130th commercial vessel** on Oct 5; since the blockade resumed **July 14** they have redirected 130 vessels and **disabled three** that refused to comply, and **destroyed 13 commercial vessels over the past 12 weeks** that were violating the blockade or part of the IRGC's shadow network. Adm. Brad Cooper: "We will swiftly act against any vessels trying to run the blockade" ([CENTCOM Oct 5](https://www.centcom.mil/MEDIA/PUBLIC-RELEASES/Article/4619553/centcom-maintains-strict-enforcement-of-us-blockade-against-iran); [Anadolu Oct 6](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/us-israel-iran-war/us-says-130th-commercial-vessel-redirected-during-iran-blockade/4079092); [Gulf News Oct 6](https://gulfnews.com/world/mena/us-navy-redirects-130-vessels-amid-iran-naval-blockade-1.500699657); [IranWire Oct 6](https://iranwire.com/en/news/158535-centcom-destroys-13-vessels-and-reroutes-130-ships-enforcing-maritime-blockade-on-iran)). **Agent arithmetic:** 125 redirections on Sep 30 → 130 on Oct 5 = **1.0/day**, up from 0.6/day across Sep 25–30 but far below the 3.5/day of Sep 23–25. **The 13 destroyed vessels are a new disclosure — the first cumulative destruction figure of the blockade. Note the release confirms enforcement at sea only; it makes no claim of strikes on Iranian territory.**
- **Additional US forces to Israel (Oct 4, carried — single-sourced):** JPost reports the US will send additional forces to Israel for potential escalation as both countries' elections near, with ~3,000 US troops currently in Israel operating air defence, flying, logistics and headquarters roles ([JPost live Oct 4](https://www.jpost.com/middle-east/iran-news/2026-10-04/live-updates-910489)).

**Oct 5 UPDATE (carried) — the European bomber node is empty:**

- **All US bombers withdrawn from RAF Fairford (Oct 4):** a Defense Department official confirmed that "all U.S. bombers that were deployed to RAF Fairford have re-deployed to their home stations in the United States," one week after arrests near the base over an alleged plot ([CNN Oct 4](https://www.cnn.com/2026/10/04/politics/us-air-force-bombers-removed-fairford-iran); [Defense News/Reuters Oct 5](https://www.defensenews.com/air/2026/10/05/us-removes-b-1-bombers-from-uk-base-amid-security-concerns/); [JPost Oct 4](https://www.jpost.com/international/article-910589)). The Pentagon stressed that long-range strike from US territory is unaffected; the UK MoD declined to comment.
- **Agent arithmetic:** the European forward operating location went from roughly 18 airframes to zero in about two weeks.
- **Agent inference (medium-high):** this inverts the Oct 2 watch item. A bomber rotation *into* Fairford or Diego Garcia was named as the indicator of a strike before the force flow completes; the observed move is the opposite. Strike-package generation now rests on CONUS global-strike sorties and carrier air — which is what the three-CSG/two-ARG package delivers by end-November. **Kinetic-window projection unchanged at late Nov 2026 – Feb 2027, anchored at late November, and the near-term tail is thinner than it was on Oct 3.** The remaining early-strike indicator is a Diego Garcia rotation.

**Oct 7 UPDATE — an operator puts numbers on the split, and the inventory buffer is quantified:**

- **Vitol's count (Oct 6–7, new):** the head of Vitol says **around 12 million barrels a day of crude oil and 2 million b/d of refined products** were leaving the Middle East on tankers in the last **7 to 10 days**. Saudi Energy Minister Prince Abdulaziz bin Salman said on Oct 6 the **East-West pipeline has hit 5.8 mb/d** ([Reuters Oct 7](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/oil-prices-rise-storms-air-strikes-threaten-supply-2026-10-07); [Al Jazeera Oct 6](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/6/saudi-arabia-says-east-west-pipeline-pumping-5-8-million-bpd)). **Agent arithmetic:** 2 mb/d of product against the 3.6 mb/d pre-war baseline is **56% of normal**, within a point of JPMorgan's 58%; the implied shortfall of **~1.6 mb/d on a tanker-only basis** sits just below the ~2.2 mb/d all-routes gap carried since Oct 1. **Denominator discipline: Vitol's 12 mb/d is seaborne departures over a 7–10 day window and is not comparable to Kpler's 16.5 mb/d September tanker-plus-pipeline average, Goldman's dark-inclusive 23.3 mb/d, or the AIS-visible Hormuz series. Never net them.**
- **The buffer is now quantified (Oct 5–6, new):** Aramco CEO Amin Nasser told the Energy Intelligence Forum that world oil stocks have fallen from about **10 billion barrels at the start of the war to under 6 billion, of which only about 10% is practically available** because of technical restrictions, and that rebuilding inventories will add **at least 2 mb/d of extra demand over the next two years**. The IEA records **more than 10 mb/d of Gulf output shut in during August** and global observed inventories down **95m bbl in the month, 507m bbl since February** ([Benzinga/Bloomberg/IEA Oct 6](https://www.benzinga.com/markets/commodities/26/10/62187427/global-oil-stockpiles-hit-scarily-thin-deadzone-3-us-plays-shielded-from-hormuz)). **Agent inference (medium-high): a 507m-bbl draw in seven months is ~2.4 mb/d of average destocking. That is the hidden supply that has kept Brent near $100 rather than far above it, and Nasser's "scarily thin" is the statement that it is nearly spent.**
- **A US weather shock lands on an already-tight product market (Oct 6–7, new):** US forecasters say a Gulf of Mexico storm will become the first Atlantic hurricane of 2026 within two days and will likely hit oil and gas producing facilities; the offshore areas in its path produce **15% of US crude and 5% of US natural gas**, and the storm could affect **six refineries** in a region holding about **50% of the 18.2 mb/d national refining capacity**. API data show US crude stocks **−2.09m bbl** in the week to Oct 2, gasoline lower, distillates marginally higher (Reuters Oct 7). X Analysts' Mukesh Sahdev: attacks and refinery outages are "likely to keep the cracks elevated and scarcity will transmit to crude… prices will stay elevated near a $100 level without any material de-escalation."
- **OPEC+ holds (Oct 4):** Saudi Arabia, Russia, Iraq, Kuwait, Kazakhstan, Algeria and Oman agreed to keep November production steady, meeting again Nov 1 ([AP Oct 4](https://apnews.com/article/oil-production-opec-iran-diesel-2b1f98d5fc11048ff65fa93952d86844); [Washington Post/AP Oct 4](https://www.washingtonpost.com/business/2026/10/04/oil-production-opec-iran-diesel/c3b20a7e-c01b-11f1-8170-681419af1cc9_story.html)). **Read:** no supply-side relief is coming from the group before December.
- **Iraq wants a fleet, not a charter (Oct 3–5, new detail):** the Iraqi Oil Tankers Company's director general says the VLCC transit was executed under direct instruction from the prime minister and the oil minister, and that the company is now pursuing **outright ownership of dedicated crude supertankers** rather than spot charters, to give SOMO pricing flexibility and to absorb buyers' transit-risk premium ([Iraqi News Oct 4](https://www.iraqinews.com/iraq/iraqi-oil-tankers-company-transits-two-million-barrels-vlcc-hormuz-2026); [Marine Insight Oct 5](https://www.marineinsight.com/iraq-moves-2-million-barrels-of-crude-beyond-strait-of-hormuz-in-first-such-operation-in-decades); [The National Oct 4](https://www.thenationalnews.com/business/energy/2026/10/04/iraq-transports-its-oil-past-hormuz-for-first-time-in-decades-as-war-with-iran-chokes-exports)). **Oct 6 UPDATE (carried) — the refining gap acquires a two-year horizon, and the exporters change their own logistics:**

- **Goldman puts a date on normalisation, and it is late 2027 (Oct 6):** Nikhil Bhandari, Goldman's co-head of Asia-Pacific natural resources research, says Hormuz flows **gradually normalise late next year** and Brent stabilises at **approximately $80**, but that **diesel and jet-fuel crack spreads will average above $40/bbl in 2027 — more than twice the usual ~$20** — because "we need to keep product prices high enough to have a certain level of demand destruction continuing next year." Roughly **2 mb/d of Middle Eastern refining capacity remains offline**; damaged Russian facilities further restrict diesel; 2026 is another year of **negative refining capacity growth**, with non-China capacity contracting about **300,000 b/d**; product inventories could **end 2026 below the lowest days-of-supply since 2015**; global refinery runs may hit maximum stretch by **February or March 2027**; replenishing inventories while meeting demand could take **up to two years** (CLSA's Baden Moore) ([CNBC Oct 6](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/06/diesel-oil-refinery-price-capacity-demand.html); [CNBC video Oct 6](https://www.cnbc.com/video/2026/10/06/strait-of-hormuz-flows-to-normalize-next-year-crude-stabilize-at-80.html)).
