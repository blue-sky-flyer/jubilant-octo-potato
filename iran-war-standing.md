# Iran War — Standing Sections
*Slow-moving sections of the daily briefing live here, in full. The daily briefing carries ONE bullet + link per section, and reprints a section in full only on the day it materially changes. Each header carries a "last materially changed" date so readers can see staleness at a glance.*

**Update protocol (daily agent):** For each section below, ask one question each run: *did anything material change in the last 24 hours?* If no — touch nothing here, and the briefing bullet reads "unchanged since [date]". If yes — update the section here, bump its date, and summarize the delta in the briefing's "What changed" list. Never copy a full section back into the briefing.

## US Military Posture & Munitions
*Last materially changed: 2026-10-10 (Pentagon plan for a three-day campaign reported; US officials say the timing decision falls in 7–10 days; the Oct 8 sanctions tranche reconciles to 22 vessels, 27 companies and 6 individuals)*

**Oct 10 UPDATE — a shape for the strike, and a clock for the decision:**

- **Three days, not a campaign (Oct 8–9):** the New York Times reports Trump has ordered the Pentagon to develop options for renewed military action, with possible targets including Iran's missile and drone arsenal, energy facilities and other military sites, and that the Pentagon has developed a plan for a **three-day campaign** ([Times of Israel liveblog Oct 9](https://www.timesofisrael.com/liveblog-oct-9-2026/amp/); [JPost Oct 9](https://www.jpost.com/middle-east/iran-news/article-911125); [The Media Line Oct 9](https://themedialine.org/headlines/president-trump-rules-out-iran-strike-before-midterms-despite-reports-of-military-plans)). **Read: a three-day package is materially smaller than the end-November three-CSG posture implies — it is consistent with the munitions-rationing constraint logged since July and with a punitive rather than disarming objective.**
- **The decision clock (Oct 9):** senior US officials told N12 that a final decision on the timing of a US attack will be made **in the next seven to ten days**, and that Trump is considering resuming fighting before both the US and Israeli elections (JPost Oct 9). **This sits directly against the Oct 8 written pledge. Agent inference (medium): the pledge constrains the *date*, not the *decision*; expect the authorisation to be taken inside October and executed after Nov 3. Kinetic-window projection unchanged: late Nov 2026 – Feb 2027, anchored at late November.**
- **Sanctions tranche reconciled (Oct 8–9):** Treasury's OFAC designated **17 vessels** and their owners under Executive Order 13902; the State Department separately designated **five further vessels, ten entities and six individuals** — a consolidated **22 vessels, 27 companies and 6 individuals** across jurisdictions including India, Türkiye, China, the UAE, the UK and the Marshall Islands ([Marine Insight Oct 9](https://www.marineinsight.com/u-s-sanctions-22-vessels-and-27-companies-tied-to-irans-shadow-fleet-and-oil-sales); [Ritz Herald Oct 9](https://ritzherald.com/treasury-sanctions-17-tankers-in-iran-shadow-fleet-crackdown); [CiberCuba Oct 9](https://en.cibercuba.com/noticias/2026-10-09-u1-e13-s27061-nid342374-eeuu-sanciona-22-buques-flota-fantasma-iran-refuerza); [Iran International Oct 8](https://www.iranintl.com/en/202610085342)). Treasury says the action "effectively neutralizes most of Iran's remaining shadow fleet," and **delisted the Hakuna Matata and Pinocchio** after they left the network and were sold to non-sanctioned operators. **The Oct 9 flag — 17 versus 22 — is resolved: both counts were correct for different agencies.**
- **Neither side will admit the calendar matters (Oct 10):** Trump and Iran's leadership each insist the US election calendar is not motivating them; persistent inflation and high gas prices have driven widespread American disapproval of the war, while Tehran holds that there will be no deal until the blockade is lifted and sanctions relieved ([AP/US News Oct 10](https://www.usnews.com/news/politics/articles/2026-10-10/trump-and-iran-agree-on-one-thing-the-us-election-calendar-isnt-driving-their-decisions-on-the-war)). **Read: the denial is itself the tell; both sides are managing a calendar they say is irrelevant.**
- **Cooper's channel, for the record (Oct 9):** the Jerusalem Post reports CENTCOM commander Adm. Brad Cooper routes strategy views to Gen. Dan Caine, Rubio or Hegseth rather than directly to Trump, viewing that as the proper role of a theatre commander ([JPost Oct 9](https://www.jpost.com/middle-east/iran-news/article-911111)). **Read: relevant to how fast an authorisation can convert into a strike package — one more node between the president and the theatre.**

**Oct 9 UPDATE (carried) — a dated presidential commitment, against a completed military plan:**

- **Agent inference (medium-high): the pledge and the plan are sequenced, not contradictory — finish the planning now, execute after Nov 3.** The written, dated commitment is the strongest public constraint on pre-midterm action yet issued and narrows the pre-Nov-3 tail that the Oct 7 readiness order had widened.
