# Iran War — Standing Sections
*Slow-moving sections of the daily briefing live here, in full. The daily briefing carries ONE bullet + link per section, and reprints a section in full only on the day it materially changes. Each header carries a "last materially changed" date so readers can see staleness at a glance.*

**Update protocol (daily agent):** For each section below, ask one question each run: *did anything material change in the last 24 hours?* If no — touch nothing here, and the briefing bullet reads "unchanged since [date]". If yes — update the section here, bump its date, and summarize the delta in the briefing's "What changed" list. Never copy a full section back into the briefing.

## Iranian State Disintegration Tracker — Iran's Endurance Ceiling
*Last materially changed: 2026-09-22 (rial 2,334,800/USD, sharpest single-session fall of the run; Tehran gold-coin premium collapses to ~0.1% as bullion underperforms a 1% currency move; Tether discount widens to 1.4%; Treasury cuts Iranian airlines off the global distribution system, giving a policy cause for the ADS-B decline)*

**Economic (confirmed, carried):** IMF −5.4% GDP projection 2026; war damage ~$270B (~90% pre-war GDP); inflation 88.6%+ (rural >100%); $27B capital flight 2025; autos −47%, petrochemicals −17%; approved foreign investment −80%; 20M+ bbl stranded afloat; China imports halved to 654K bpd (June); TGL revoked; dark-fleet channel only. Unemployment 9.1% (from 7.3%), 450,000 jobs lost.

**Sep 22 UPDATE — the adjustment is still running entirely through the exchange rate:**

- **Rial 2,334,800 IRR/USD (tgju, Sep 22):** 1.07% weaker than Sep 21 and 2% weaker than Sep 18 — the largest single-session depreciation of this run and a fourth consecutive down session. Cumulative depreciation against the ~2.0M/USD of late August is now ~17%.

- **Gold-coin premium collapses to ~0.1% (agent arithmetic on tgju, Sep 22):** Emami coin 2,370M IRR = ~$1,015 at the open-market rate against ~$1,014 intrinsic (7.32g fine; world-gold assumption carried from Sep 19). The coin rose only **0.2% in rial while the currency fell 1.07%** — bullion materially underperformed the dollar on a bad currency day. 18k gram 239.7M IRR. **Read:** this is stronger evidence than the flat-premium readings of Sep 19–21. In a genuine monetary panic the coin leads the dollar; here it lags it. The most consistent explanation remains net household liquidation of gold to fund consumption, absorbing whatever capital-flight bid exists. The managed-bleed characterisation is reinforced, not weakened, by the faster currency move.

- **Tether discount widens to 1.4% (tgju, Sep 22):** 2,301,400 IRR versus the 2,334,800 cash dollar, from 1.2% on Sep 21, 0.7% on Sep 20 and 0.2% on Sep 19. Four consecutive widenings. The crypto rail is progressively *cheaper* than cash dollars — the opposite of an exit bid — and sanctions pressure on it continues to build ([JPost/Reuters Sep 18](https://www.jpost.com/middle-east/iran-news/2026-09-18/live-updates-909020)); the DOJ is separately seeking forfeiture of $61.19M in USDT tied to Iranian oil proceeds ([BigGo/SDNY Sep 15](https://finance.biggo.com/news/ba72ac04-2e5f-4fe3-85e5-da6514c4521b)).

- **Civil aviation isolation now has a policy cause (Sep 22, new):** US Treasury announced secondary sanctions cutting Iranian airlines off from the global distribution system under Operation Economic Outcast ([JPost Sep 22](https://www.jpost.com/middle-east/iran-news/2026-09-22/live-updates-909229)). OpenSky shows **64 Iranian aircraft** broadcasting ADS-B, against 71 on Sep 21, 83 on Sep 20 and 92 a week ago — a 30% weekly decline. **Inference (medium confidence, revised):** the multi-day slide is now better explained by booking/distribution and insurance strangulation than by pre-strike airspace caution. Treat further declines as a sanctions-effect series unless a discontinuity appears.

- **Connectivity firm (IODA Sep 22):** 11,271 reachable /24s against a prior six-day mean of 11,193 — 0.7% **above** the mean, with a 24-hour minimum of 11,187. BGP prefixes 41,698 versus a 41,673 mean. No throttling; the network layer is the healthiest reading in the Iranian dataset.

## Red Sea / Bab el-Mandeb — Baseline
*Last materially changed: 2026-09-22 (CORRECTION: Yanbu was loading 2.9 mb/d in early September, not 5% of 2025 — the East-West pipeline outage, not structural irrelevance, is what zeroed the port before the Sep 19 Houthi strike; G7 statement; UK and France commit defensive support; Saudi strikes on Mokha)*

**Sep 22 CORRECTION — Yanbu was alive until Sep 11:**

- **Yanbu crude and condensate loadings were estimated at 2.9 mb/d in early September, up from 1.5 mb/d in August; the Sep 21 tracking update reported no observed crude loadings since Sep 16** ([Breakwave Sep 22](https://www.breakwaveadvisors.com/insights/980026wetreportkju458-ook57-j4w3y)). **This supersedes the framing used in this section on Sep 20–21**, which relied on PortWatch's AIS-visible Yanbu export tonnage (37 kt in the week to Sep 11, ~5% of the 2025 week) to argue the Red Sea outlet was already structurally dead. It was not: it was running at roughly four times its August rate until the East-West pipeline was damaged, and went to zero in the following five to ten days. **What survives:** the conclusion that the Sep 19 Houthi strike on Yanbu removed no incremental barrel, and therefore that crude's failure to hold a bid was rational. **What changes:** the mechanism. Yanbu is a pipeline-dependent node whose availability is set by the East-West line, not by Houthi missiles; Aramco's expectation of restoring about half of daily flow "soon" ([Reuters Sep 17](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/three-pumping-stations-along-saudi-east-west-pipeline-were-hit-recent-attack-2026-09-17/)) is therefore a live upside catalyst for Red Sea loadings, and a live target set for the Houthis.
