# Iran War — Standing Sections
*Slow-moving sections of the daily briefing live here, in full. The daily briefing carries ONE bullet + link per section, and reprints a section in full only on the day it materially changes. Each header carries a "last materially changed" date so readers can see staleness at a glance.*

**Update protocol (daily agent):** For each section below, ask one question each run: *did anything material change in the last 24 hours?* If no — touch nothing here, and the briefing bullet reads "unchanged since [date]". If yes — update the section here, bump its date, and summarize the delta in the briefing's "What changed" list. Never copy a full section back into the briefing.

## US Military Posture & Munitions
*Last materially changed: 2026-10-29 (a Patriot battery moves from Texas into theatre and troops shift off exposed bases; CENTCOM publicly rebuts Iranian mine claims; day 58 with no release covering strikes on Iranian soil after Sep 1)*

**Oct 29 UPDATE — the first air-defence battery movement into theatre of this cycle:**

- **Patriot from Texas to the Middle East (Oct 27–28):** the US military is moving a **Patriot air and missile defence system — radar, engagement control station, launchers and interceptors — from Texas to the Middle East** as Washington and Israel prepare for possible renewed combat operations after Nov 3; the US has also **relocated troops from bases assessed as vulnerable to Iranian attack**, is replenishing munitions stockpiles, and is preparing bomber and fighter aircraft, per officials familiar with the movements ([JPost carrying WSJ Oct 28](https://www.jpost.com/israel-news/defense-news/article-913105)). The same reporting repeats that Gulf Arab governments have been warned to expect a **short, intense campaign**, which upgrades the Oct 28 Israel Hayom item from single-outlet to two independent carriers. **Read: this is the closest approach yet to the Q5 trigger "THAAD redeploying into theatre." A Patriot is a lower-tier interceptor than THAAD and its movement is defensive, consistent with the Oct 25 aircraft-dispersal read — the command expects to absorb a retaliation. It is an indicator of intent to act after Nov 3, not of a pulled-forward date.**
- **CENTCOM rebuts the mine claims (Oct 28):** responding to Iranian-controlled media reporting that commercial vessels had struck sea mines, CENTCOM said on X that **"all internationally recognized primary transit routes through the Strait of Hormuz have been cleared of sea mines by U.S. forces and remain so,"** and that thousands of commercial vessels continue to use the lanes ([Iran International Oct 28](https://www.iranintl.com/en/202610286050)). **Attribute as a CENTCOM claim; it restates the Oct 25 Cooper assertion and is the second rebuttal of the same Iranian claim in a week.**
- **The warning is now carried by a US local-news syndication of the same sourcing (Oct 29):** US officials warn of possible strikes on Iran after the elections, with retaliation expected against American personnel and facilities ([Spectrum News Oct 29](https://spectrumlocalnews.com/nc/coastal/international/2026/10/29/us-iran-possible-strikes-retaliation-midterm-elections)).
- **What has NOT changed:** the European bomber node remains empty since Oct 4; no Diego Garcia rotation; no THAAD redeployment into theatre; and **no dated CENTCOM release covers strikes on Iranian territory after Sep 1 — day 58.**
