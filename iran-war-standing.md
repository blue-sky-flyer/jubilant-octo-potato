## US Military Posture & Munitions
*Last materially changed: 2026-08-17 (CENTCOM last confirmed strike date corrected to July 29 — re-verified against Reuters Jul 30 article and official CENTCOM release Jul 29; prior "Jul 22" language in this section corrected)*

- **CENTCOM strike status: PAUSED (last confirmed strike July 29).** The most recent official CENTCOM strike release is **July 29** (Reuters Jul 30 / CENTCOM official release Jul 29 — re-verified Aug 17): CENTCOM struck dozens of IRGC targets on the night of July 29 "in response to yesterday's attempted missile attacks on US forces," hitting military command centers, missile and drone facilities, coastal surveillance and defense sites, and maritime capabilities. **No official release or dated independent confirmation documents any US strike on Iranian territory after July 29.** Aug 10 reporting (CNN; Haass) confirms the administration deliberately "backed off renewed hostilities," citing depleted munitions stocks, in favor of economic pressure ("low-keying"). *(Prior briefings stating "Jul 22" as the last confirmed release date are corrected here — the Jul 29 CENTCOM release post-dates Jul 22.)*
- **Blockade enforcement: INDEFINITE (declared Aug 14); territorial-claim framing Aug 15.** Secretary of Defense Pete Hegseth stated Aug 14 that the US can sustain its blockade "indefinitely." On Aug 15, Trump stated the US will make the Strait of Hormuz a US territory "pretty soon" — the most expansive US territorial-claim statement of the conflict (Al Jazeera liveblog, Aug 15; SAMAA TV, Aug 15 — double-sourced). A White House official later reportedly said Trump was joking (WION, Aug 15). Trump also warned Iran that any new attack would be met with a response "100 times" stronger. Iran's Deputy FM Gharibabadi flatly rejected the territorial claim; PGSA posted that Hormuz "remains blocked and will not be reopened until Iran's conditions are accepted." **Enforcement count as of Aug 12: 59 commercial vessels redirected, 3 disabled, 2 boarded** (UANI Aug 14 — re-verified). US forces fired on a blockade-running cargo vessel Aug 11 (Al Jazeera). CNBC Aug 17 confirms Hormuz shipping has "ground to a halt" ahead of MOU expiry.
- **MOU expiry (Aug 18):** Trump ruled out any extension on Aug 18, demanding Iran "put up the white flag of surrender" (Al Jazeera liveblog, Aug 18). Trump claimed his team is in discussions with the IRGC directly — the IRGC denied this (Al Jazeera liveblog, Aug 18). The Versailles MOU is now formally lapsed with no US-acknowledged replacement framework.
- **Bessent "never seen" measures preview:** Treasury Secretary Bessent told Newsmax on Aug 14 to "watch this space for more announcements coming next week" on economic pressure against Iran (CNN, Aug 14). Specific measures not yet disclosed; treat as forward-looking signal.
- **Carrier rotation (CORRECTED 2026-08-16):** A carrier is en route to **replace** the USS Abraham Lincoln on a 1-for-1 rotation — confirmed by Trump's own words: "That ship is moving right now or very shortly. And it's being replaced with another very similar ship" (NBC News, Aug 14). This is a planned rotation addressing crew-welfare concerns, **not** the addition of a fourth carrier group. Theater posture remains three-CSG minimum. Boxer ARG/11th MEU (~2,200 Marines) also in theater.
- **Munitions:** ~2,080 Tomahawks expended through Jul 22 (~$3.9B restock; 18–24 mo lead time; Pentagon rationing to preserve Indo-Pacific reserve); Jul 29 reactive strike package consumed additional inventory (quantity unspecified in CENTCOM release); key missile interceptors low (CNN, Aug 4); UAE/Kuwait ~75% of Patriot stocks spent; Pentagon asked industry to ramp production (Aug 10). Jul 12 CENTCOM release confirmed first combat use of one-way attack sea drones (novel-munition debut consistent with the combat-as-qualification framework).
- **Kinetic-window projection** (structural analysis, Aug 10): primary window mid-Oct 2026 – Feb 2027; secondary Apr–May 2027 (SPR floor); Israeli unilateral action is the Sep–Oct wildcard. Precursor watch list in [iran-war-context.md](https://github.com/blue-sky-flyer/jubilant-octo-potato/blob/main/iran-war-context.md).

## Strategic Reserve Countdown — US Endurance Ceiling (Day 0)
*Last materially changed: 2026-08-13 (SPR revised to ~304.8M bbl per GEF Aug 11 update)*

**One-line arithmetic:** SPR ~304.8M bbl (GEF, updated Aug 11 — 2.841M bbl draw that week, fresh multi-decade low) minus political floor ~200M bbl = ~104.8M bbl usable buffer ÷ ~0.38M bbl/day observed draw = **~276 days → Day 0 approximately mid-May 2027.**

Note: the large US commercial crude inventory BUILD of 17.4M bbl (EIA, week reported Aug 12 — largest since early 2023) is a domestic commercial stock event, not an SPR event, and does not alter the strategic draw trajectory. It may reflect demand destruction and refinery run reductions consistent with IEA's −1.6 mb/d demand downgrade. SPR draw continues independently of commercial builds. IEA August OMR (Aug 8): 2026 demand now projected −1.6 mb/d (from −1.0 in July); global supply 101.5 mb/d in July, 6.3 mb/d below year-ago; 8.3 mb/d of Gulf output still shut in; full-year supply shortfall averaging 4.3 mb/d — largest peacetime shortfall in agency history. OPEC August report (Aug 13) cut 2026 demand growth to 600K bpd from 800K bpd, independently corroborating demand destruction. IEF (Aug 14) identified a 2.2 mb/d demand gap from a demand-side angle, further corroborating the shortfall. Business Insider Aug 18 confirms "America's oil reserves are the lowest in 43 years" (directionally corroborates standing SPR data; no new EIA print as of Aug 18). Political floor ~200M bbl is a consensus estimate, not confirmed by Treasury/DOE. Day 0 = the outer bound of US strategic patience; the Oct 2026–Feb 2027 kinetic window arrives before it. *(Low-to-moderate confidence.)*

## Iranian State Disintegration Tracker — Iran's Endurance Ceiling
*Last materially changed: 2026-08-18 (ISW Aug 17 special report: IRGC used MOU period to coordinate attack plans with proxies — WSJ via ISW; bifurcated Iranian strategy confirmed)*

**Economic (confirmed):** IMF −5.4% GDP projection 2026 (NCRI citing Jahan-e Sanat/IMF, Jul 11); war damage ~$270B (~90% pre-war GDP); inflation 88.6%+ (rural >100%); $27B capital flight 2025 vs $1.676B FDI; autos −47%, washing machines −42%, TVs −36%, petrochemicals −17%; approved foreign investment −80%; 20M+ bbl stranded afloat; China imports halved to 654K bpd (June); TGL revoked (dark-fleet channel only). Trump's "difficulty paying troops" (Axios, Aug 10) is an unverified US-government claim — state-media pay-raise reports contested; treat as disputed. IEA August OMR (Aug 8) confirms the siege depth: 8.3 mb/d of Gulf output still shut in; 6.3 mb/d below year-ago supply levels globally, driven primarily by Iranian shut-in. RFE/RL Aug 2026 reporting frames Iran as "lurching toward severe economic collapse" with Iranians describing disappearing food items from household tables. Business Insider Aug 18 "5 charts" confirms near-six-months of economic hammering — consistent with known indicators.

**Military reconstitution:** Iran **claims** >75% of pre-war missile/drone capability retained (CBS citing Iranian state sources, Aug 12 — **Iranian claim, not independent confirmation**; independent baseline: ISW May ~70% stockpiles; retention likely reflects pre-war stockpile, not replenishment — launcher and production-infrastructure losses documented). 30/33 Hormuz missile sites restored (ISW, May). Air defenses rebuilding with Chinese QW-12/FN-16 and Russian Verba MANPADS; heavier Chinese systems reportedly staged via third countries; Shahed airframes returned from Russia with upgrades.

**Doctrinal/command restructuring — BIFURCATED STRATEGY CONFIRMED (Aug 18):** ISW's Aug 17 special report (entered pack Aug 18) assessed that "the Iranian regime is signaling that it would prefer to continue or even escalate fighting… rather than make any serious concessions," and that the IRGC "used the calm created by the MOU to coordinate attack plans with its partners and proxies" (WSJ via ISW, Aug 17). ⚠️ IRGC proxy-coordination detail is single-sourced (WSJ cited by ISW; WSJ not independently in pack). This finding confirms a bifurcated Iranian strategy: the IRGC hardliner track is preparing for escalation during the diplomatic pause, while the pragmatist FM track (Araghchi/Oman channel) pursues a draft Hormuz agreement. The two tracks are structurally in tension. IRGC Brigadier General Naqdi's Aug 13 "offensive doctrine" announcement (Samaa TV/Suno News HD, confirmed by two Pakistani sources — re-verified Aug 14) is consistent with the ISW escalation-preparation assessment.

**Political cohesion:** IRGC troika controls all levers — Vahidi (kinetic), Zolghadr (SNSC gate), Rezaei (national security chief; Aug 12 formal Hormuz-closure declaration to China's ambassador). Succession vacuum: Mojtaba's status uncertain — US-Israeli intel assessed incapacitation; Pezeshkian's Aug 11 "perfect health" attestation is an unverified government claim. No confirmed elite defections. Pragmatist track (Oman single-corridor talks, Iran-Oman Hormuz agreement reportedly in "final drafting" per AP Aug 17) is the most live diplomatic channel — directly constrained by Rezaei's Aug 12 SNSC declaration, Hegseth's Aug 14 "indefinite" blockade statement, Trump's Aug 15 territorial-claim framing, and Trump's Aug 18 "white flag" statement. The IRGC's denial of Trump's claimed direct IRGC discussions (Al Jazeera, Aug 18) further narrows the pragmatist space.

**Trajectory:** managed attrition trending toward a tipping point in **Q1–Q2 2027** (same window as the US SPR floor) — a fiscal forcing function (troop pay, rial, food/fuel) triggering elite bargaining, not near-term popular collapse. Hardliner consolidation makes a sudden-crisis endpoint likelier than a negotiated offramp. ISW's bifurcated-strategy finding suggests the IRGC is actively managing the clock to its own advantage during any diplomatic pause. ⚠️ NCRI data is MEK-affiliated — directionally indicative only.

## Red Sea / Bab el-Mandeb — Baseline
*Last materially changed: 2026-08-17 (Mokha port name confirmed as al-Makha/Mokha — Gemini Aug 16 challenge adopted; Houthis fire 2 ballistic missiles at Mokha Aug 17 — Al Arabiya English, Aug 17)*

Houthi Saudi-port naval blockade in force since Jul 20; ~40% tanker-transit drop since (JMIC). Eight Saudi tankers targeted since Jul 22; 29 Saudi tankers reported prevented or turned back (Houthi claim, Anadolu Agency, Aug 5 — unverified independently).

**Aug 17 escalation — Houthi ballistic missile strikes on Mokha:** Houthis fired two ballistic missiles at the port of Mokha (al-Makha), per Yemeni government forces ([Al Arabiya English Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/p/DcI3U6dlqyI), Aug 17). ⚠️ Single-sourced on Aug 17 strike; Houthi attribution via Yemeni government forces per the post text; flagged pending additional confirmation. This is the third consecutive escalation event at Mokha in 72 hours: port suspension (Aug 16) → continued strikes (Aug 17).

**Aug 16 escalation — Mokha (al-Makha) port suspension (port name confirmed):** Yemen's internationally recognized government suspended operations at the port of al-Makha (Mokha) following recent Houthi attacks that killed seven people and caused approximately $16 million in losses ([CNN liveblog](https://www.cnn.com/2026/08/16/world/live-news/iran-war-trump), Aug 16). **Port name confirmed as al-Makha/Mokha** — Gemini Aug 16 cross-check challenge adopted, corroborated by Al Arabiya English Aug 17 post naming Mokha explicitly. This was the first confirmed port-operations suspension at Bab el-Mandeb of the conflict.

**Bab el-Mandeb transit data (JPost/Kpler, mid-Aug):** Just 1 vessel crossed Bab el-Mandeb on a recent day, down from 20 the previous day (JPost/Kpler, mid-Aug period) — corroborating near-nadir transit conditions consistent with Hormuz data.

**Aug 12–13 escalation (prior, confirmed):** Houthi ballistic missile strike on MV Tihama in Bab el-Mandeb killed four to six crew (three Pakistanis and one Indonesian per Saudi Al Hadath/Reuters, confirmed by two Yemeni coast guard sources and two government military officials; coast guard put toll at six dead, ten wounded — double-sourced, JPost Aug 12 / The National Aug 13). Three successive missiles struck the vessel. The Aug 11 fatality incident (4–6 crew killed on Egyptian cargo vessel) preceded this.

**Saudi ground-push deliberations (Aug 13):** Saudi Arabia is weighing whether to back a Yemeni ground campaign to retake the Red Sea coast from the Houthis, using militias assembled over seven months (The Jerusalem Post/The Media Line, Aug 13; Al Jazeera analysis, Aug 13 — double-sourced). Yanbu ships ~4M bpd; Houthi drone claimed to strike the Aramco refinery at Jazan Aug 9 (unverified independently); Houthis also claimed drone attack on Saudi Aramco refinery in Jizan Aug 13 (Middle East Monitor Instagram, Aug 14 — ⚠️ Houthi claim, not independently confirmed). A Saudi ground-support decision would be the largest Saudi kinetic escalation of the conflict.

Cargo ship sunk by unmanned surface vessel Aug 4 (UKMTO/UANI). Houthis cut off from Iranian resupply since ~Jul 4 (Stimson) — operating on stockpiles. Some Saudi tankers transit with AIS dark; others divert via Cape. War-risk ~0.75% hull. **Geography rule: the Cape route does not pass through Bab el-Mandeb — vessels on the Cape route are unaffected by Bab el-Mandeb activation.**

## On the Ground in Iran
*Last materially changed: 2026-08-18 (ISW bifurcated-strategy finding added — IRGC escalation prep vs. pragmatist Oman track; Business Insider Aug 18 Iran economy corroboration)*

Domestic economic deterioration without break (see Disintegration Tracker for figures). Internet costs tripled; filtering intensified; social media restricted since the partial May 27 restoration. No confirmed large-scale protest activity in recent packs — underlying pressure structurally unresolved but unverifiable under suppression. IEA August OMR (Aug 8) confirms the macro siege context: 8.3 mb/d of Gulf output still shut in; Iranian oil export position remains the most constrained of the conflict. RFE/RL Aug 2026 reporting frames Iran as "lurching toward severe economic collapse," with Iranians describing disappearing food items from household tables — consistent with the known inflation (88.6%+) and purchasing-power collapse trajectory. Business Insider Aug 18 "5 charts" confirms near-six-month economic damage consistent with all known indicators.

**ISW bifurcated strategy (Aug 18 addition):** ISW's Aug 17 special report assessed that the IRGC "used the calm created by the MOU to coordinate attack plans with its partners and proxies" (WSJ via ISW), while the pragmatist FM track (Araghchi/Oman) pursues a draft Hormuz agreement. This bifurcation — hardliner IRGC escalation preparation running in parallel with pragmatist diplomatic engagement — is the defining structural feature of the Iranian posture in the post-MOU period. The operative question is which track controls the nuclear and treaty-authorization gate; the Zolghadr SNSC appointment maintains IRGC control of that gate.

**Post-MOU posture (Aug 18):** Trump's "white flag of surrender" framing and the IRGC's denial of direct US-IRGC discussions (Al Jazeera, Aug 18) confirm that the Iranian side faces a maximalist US public position with no acknowledged backchannel. The Oman draft Hormuz agreement (AP Aug 17; GEF Aug 11) remains the only live pragmatist output — but it awaits US acknowledgment that has not come.

**Mojtaba status — contested, incapacitation assessment maintained:** US-Israeli intel assessed Mojtaba incapacitated (spinal lesions, brain trauma) with no proof-of-life through Day 166+. Pezeshkian's Aug 11 claim of a seven-hour meeting with a healthy Supreme Leader was assessed as an information operation. Incapacitation assessment maintained. The operative nuclear and treaty-authorization question — no authorized Iranian signatory exists for treaty-level commitments — is unchanged.

IRGC bandwidth consumed by Hormuz enforcement, proxy coordination (Houthi stockpile-era operations), air-defense rebuilding, and domestic security. The Hegseth "indefinite blockade" declaration (Aug 14), Trump's Hormuz territorial-claim (Aug 15), and the Aug 18 "white flag" posture remove any near-term commercial transit normalization that could ease Iran's fiscal position.
