## Strategic Reserve Countdown — US Endurance Ceiling (Day 0)
*Last materially changed: 2026-08-12 (IEA demand revision)*

**One-line arithmetic:** SPR ~293M bbl (Aug 12 estimate, low-confidence) minus political floor ~200M bbl = ~93M bbl usable buffer ÷ ~0.38M bbl/day observed draw = **~245 days → Day 0 approximately mid-April 2027.**

Inputs: SPR ~307.7M bbl confirmed EIA w/e Jul 10; "below 300M" (Trading Economics, Aug 10); next EIA weekly print is the key refinement. IEA (Aug 12): 2026 demand now projected −1.6 mb/d (from −1.0 in July); global supply 101.5 mb/d in July, 6.3 mb/d below year-ago; full-year supply shortfall averaging 4.3 mb/d — largest peacetime shortfall in agency history. The demand downgrade marginally extends Day 0; a Q4 demand recovery (IEA's assumption) would re-accelerate it. Political floor ~200M bbl is a consensus estimate, not confirmed by Treasury/DOE. Day 0 = the outer bound of US strategic patience; the Oct 2026–Feb 2027 kinetic window arrives before it. *(Low-to-moderate confidence.)*

## Iranian State Disintegration Tracker — Iran's Endurance Ceiling
*Last materially changed: 2026-08-12 (Iranian >75% capability claim; Rezaei declaration)*

**Economic (confirmed):** IMF −5.4% GDP projection 2026; war damage ~$270B (~90% pre-war GDP); inflation 88.6%+ (rural >100%); $27B capital flight 2025 vs $1.676B FDI; autos −47%, washing machines −42%, TVs −36%, petrochemicals −17%; approved foreign investment −80%; 20M+ bbl stranded afloat; China imports halved to 654K bpd (June); TGL revoked (dark-fleet channel only). Trump's "difficulty paying troops" (Axios, Aug 10) is an unverified US-government claim — Qwen challenge noted state-media pay-raise reports; treat as contested.

**Military reconstitution:** Iran **claims** >75% of pre-war missile/drone capability retained (CBS citing Iranian state sources, Aug 12 — **Iranian claim, not independent confirmation**; independent baseline: ISW May ~70% stockpiles, and ISW/June assessed heavy losses to launchers and production infrastructure — retention likely reflects pre-war stockpile, not replenishment). 30/33 Hormuz missile sites restored (ISW, May). Air defenses rebuilding with Chinese QW-12/FN-16 and Russian Verba MANPADS; heavier Chinese systems reportedly staged via third countries; Shahed airframes returned from Russia with upgrades.

**Political cohesion:** IRGC troika controls all levers — Vahidi (kinetic), Zolghadr (SNSC gate), Rezaei (national security chief; his Aug 12 formal Hormuz-closure declaration to China's ambassador is the strongest institutional exercise of that authority to date). Succession vacuum: Mojtaba unconscious at Qom per US-Israeli intel, no independent proof-of-life through Day 166 (Pezeshkian's Aug 11 "perfect health" attestation is an unverified government claim); nuclear-treaty commitments have no authorized signatory. Pragmatist track (Oman single-corridor talks "final stages," Aug 10) directly undercut by Rezaei's declaration. No confirmed elite defections.

**Trajectory:** managed attrition trending toward a tipping point in **Q1–Q2 2027** (same window as the US SPR floor) — a fiscal forcing function (troop pay, rial, food/fuel) triggering elite bargaining, not near-term popular collapse. Hardliner consolidation makes a sudden-crisis endpoint likelier than a negotiated offramp. ⚠️ NCRI data is MEK-affiliated — directionally indicative only.

## Red Sea / Bab el-Mandeb — Baseline
*Last materially changed: 2026-08-12 (ballistic-missile fatalities threshold)*

Houthi Saudi-port naval blockade in force since Jul 20; ~40% tanker-transit drop since (JMIC). Aug 11: ballistic missile killed crew (4–6 dead) on an Egyptian cargo vessel at Bab el-Mandeb — first confirmed Houthi shipping-strike fatalities of the war (Al Jazeera Aug 11; ABC News Australia Aug 12 — double-sourced) and a caliber escalation over the prior drone/ASM pattern. Mocha struck Aug 10 (7 killed) and Aug 11; Marib struck Aug 11. Cargo ship sunk by unmanned surface vessel Aug 4 (UKMTO/UANI). War-risk ~0.75% hull. **Geography rule: the Cape route does not pass through Bab el-Mandeb — vessels on the Cape route are unaffected by Bab el-Mandeb activation.**
