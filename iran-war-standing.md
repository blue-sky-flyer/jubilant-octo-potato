**Update protocol (daily agent):** For each section below, ask one question each run: *did anything material change in the last 24 hours?* If no — touch nothing here, and the briefing bullet reads "unchanged since [date]". ## Red Sea / Bab el-Mandeb — Baseline
*Last materially changed: 2026-09-30 (Bab el-Mandeb tanker tonnage turns POSITIVE versus 2025 — +17% — and the Suez:BEM ratio collapses to 1.10x: the "terminating basin" finding is reversed and Yanbu's barrels are going south to Asia)*

**Sep 30 UPDATE — the corridor is a through-route again, and it is Saudi crude that reopened it:**
