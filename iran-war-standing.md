# Iran War — Standing Sections
*Slow-moving sections of the daily briefing live here, in full. The daily briefing carries ONE bullet + link per section, and reprints a section in full only on the day it materially changes. Each header carries a "last materially changed" date so readers can see staleness at a glance.*

**Update protocol (daily agent):** For each section below, ask one question each run: *did anything material change in the last 24 hours?* If no — touch nothing here, and the briefing bullet reads "unchanged since [date]". If yes — update the section here, bump its date, and summarize the delta in the briefing's "What changed" list. Never copy a full section back into the briefing.

## US Military Posture & Munitions
*Last materially changed: 2026-10-15 (CNN reporting that US strike options include the MOAB against Iranian energy infrastructure and nuclear sites including Pickaxe Mountain; MI5 says Iran is targeting UK military sites and Fairford was "not unique"; Netanyahu says a strike is "not convenient" now; CENTCOM's Oct 12 throughput claim contradicted on its own date by Kpler; day 44 with no CENTCOM release covering strikes on Iranian soil after Sep 1)*

**Oct 15 UPDATE — the target set widens to energy, and the Israeli channel cools:**

- **MOAB enters the reported option set (Oct 14–15):** CNN reports, via multiple US officials, that US strike plans include the **largest non-nuclear weapon in the inventory**, to be used against **Iranian energy infrastructure and nuclear sites, including Pickaxe Mountain** ([JPost liveblog carrying CNN Oct 15](https://www.jpost.com/middle-east/iran-news/2026-10-15/live-updates-911752); [CNN video Oct 15](https://edition.cnn.com/2026/10/15/world/video/school-roof-collapses-in-pakistan-killing-dozens-vrtc)). **Two readings, both material. First, the weapon's own designation — Massive Ordnance Air Blast — describes a surface-blast effect, which sits oddly against the deeply buried tunnel complex at Pickaxe Mountain and fits better against the tunnel *entrances* that last month's earthworks were built to protect (agent inference, medium). Second, and more consequential for markets: naming *energy infrastructure* as a target category would reverse the July 9 Kharg doctrine, under which CENTCOM struck Kharg's military targets while deliberately preserving oil export infrastructure.** Single-channel reporting carried by an aggregator; **not yet adopted into scenario weights.**
- **MI5 escalates the UK attribution (Oct 13–14):** Director General **Ken McCallum** said Iran is trying to hit military sites in Britain and that the suspected September plot against the US-run air base was **"not unique,"** framing Iranian and Russian hybrid activity in the UK as expanding ([Haaretz/AP Oct 14](https://www.haaretz.com/world-news/europe/2026-10-14/ty-article/mi5-chief-iran-targeting-u-k-military-sites-raf-fairford-plot-not-unique/000001a1-394f-d57f-abbb-3fcfff380000)). **Read: this converts the Fairford episode from a single incident into a declared campaign, and retrospectively strengthens the case that the bomber withdrawal from Fairford was threat-driven rather than posture-driven.**
- **The Israeli channel cools (Oct 10–14):** Netanyahu said an attack on Iran is **"not convenient" right now** ([Times of Israel Oct 14](https://www.timesofisrael.com/liveblog_entry/netanyahu-attack-on-iran-not-convenient-right-now-ben-gvir-wont-be-defense-minister-in-next-government/)). Ben Caspit reports Washington pressed Israel over the past two weeks to carry out the strike itself — rather than a US or joint operation — and has since **put the initiative on hold** after Zamir's election-postponement warning ([i24 Oct 10](https://www.i24news.tv/en/news/israel/artc-report-us-pressed-israel-to-strike-iran-ahead-of-elections)). Haaretz reports IDF officials assess Iran has **replenished much of its ballistic missile stockpile and is accelerating production**, but that a pre-election strike **"could be perceived as politically motivated and erode public trust"** ([Haaretz Oct 10](https://www.haaretz.com/israel-news/israel-security/2026-10-10/ty-article/.premium/idf-officials-warn-pre-election-iran-strike-could-seem-politically-motivated/000001a1-2711-d5af-a3b7-2fb18fe70000)). **Read: three independent channels now describe the pre-Oct-27 window as closed by politics on both sides. The kinetic-window anchor at late November is reinforced, not challenged.**
- **The throughput claim is contradicted on its own date (Oct 14):** CENTCOM's assertion that US forces helped move **more than 19 million barrels** through Hormuz on Monday Oct 12 is contradicted by Kpler, which records **3 million barrels of crude** transiting that Monday (CNBC Oct 14). **Read: CENTCOM's public throughput figures are an instrument of the information fight and should not be used as a flow series.**
- **What has NOT changed:** the European bomber node remains empty since Oct 4; no Diego Garcia rotation; no THAAD redeployment into theatre; and **no dated CENTCOM release covers strikes on Iranian territory after Sep 1 — day 44.**

**Oct 14 UPDATE (carried) — Iran is fortifying against a raid, not only a bombing, and CENTCOM is fighting the throughput narrative in public:**

- **Passive defences at Natanz and Pickaxe Mountain (Oct 13):** the Institute for Science and International Security, assessing **Vantor** satellite imagery, concludes Iran built **makeshift dirt barriers around the Natanz and Pickaxe Mountain (Kuh-e Kolang Gaz La) sites last month**, designed to stop **vehicles and personnel reaching deeply buried tunnel entrances** — a defence against infiltration rather than against bombs. Pickaxe Mountain sits about a mile south of the Natanz enrichment plant, was never struck during the war, and remains under construction ([WSJ Oct 13](https://www.wsj.com/world/middle-east/iran-is-fortifying-nuclear-sites-and-rallying-its-people-for-a-return-to-fighting-c4cc3c28); [ISIS report](https://isis-online.org/isis-reports/imagery-update-passive-defenses-identified-at-natanz-and-pickaxe-mountain); [SOFX Oct 14](https://www.sofx.com/iran-fortifies-nuclear-sites-and-threatens-saudi-oil-facilities); [New Arab Oct 13](https://www.newarab.com/news/iran-fortifies-nuclear-sites-prepares-renewed-war-report); [JPost Oct 13](https://www.jpost.com/middle-east/iran-news/article-911555)). **Read: Tehran's engineering effort is pricing a ground or special-forces element that the reported three-day air package does not contain. Either Iran is hedging against a capability the US has not signalled, or it reads the Pentagon's options differently than the press does.**
- **Iranian deterrent messaging to Arab capitals (Oct 13):** Iranian officials warned Arab counterparts that **Saudi oil facilities, Emirati infrastructure and US warships** could be targeted if Washington strikes again ([Türkiye Today Oct 13](https://www.turkiyetoday.com/region/iran-warns-of-ground-incursions-saudi-oil-strikes-if-us-attacks-again-report-3230224); WSJ Oct 13). **Attribute as reported Iranian private warnings. It pairs with Rezaei's Oct 11 threat to cables, data centres and AI facilities and with the Oct 5 Fadavi threat tied explicitly to a ground attack.**
- **CENTCOM in the information fight (Oct 13–14):** "IRGC's navy commander has no navy. American forces and regional partners clearly control the Strait of Hormuz," CENTCOM posted, saying US forces helped Gulf partners move **more than 19 million barrels of crude through the strait on Monday Oct 12** ([Anadolu Oct 14](http://www.aa.com.tr/en/americas/us-rejects-iran-s-claim-of-control-over-strait-of-hormuz/4087883); [Iran International Oct 13](https://www.iranintl.com/en/202610133999)). **Attribute as a CENTCOM claim.** It is the second near-pre-war throughput assertion in a week after the president's Oct 8 "22 million barrels" and cannot be reconciled with the AIS record except by assuming the overwhelming majority of movement is dark.

**Oct 12 UPDATE (carried) — the blockade ledger is revised downward, and it is not a monotonic counter:**

- **The new cumulative figures (Oct 12, CENTCOM via CBS):** since the blockade resumed **July 14**, US forces have **disabled four commercial vessels**, **turned around 135 ships**, and **destroyed 10 tankers** believed to be part of the shadow fleet carrying sanctioned Iranian oil ([CBS Oct 12](https://www.cbsnews.com/live-updates/iran-war-us-trump-saudi-arabia-houthi)). The *Ocean Molica* (also "Arika Sun") strike of Oct 10 is confirmed in the same release: a fighter put a precision munition into the stern, disabling propulsion without crew injury, after the ship departed an Iranian port ([MiGFlug Oct 11, reproducing the CENTCOM account](https://migflug.com/afterburner/ocean-molica-us-fighter-jet-disables-cargo-ship-iran-blockade-gulf-of-oman-2026)).
- **Agent arithmetic and caution:** redirections 130 (Oct 5) → 135 (Oct 12) = **0.7/day**, against 1.0/day over Sep 30–Oct 5. But the **destroyed-vessel count moved 13 → 10**, which is impossible for a true cumulative. **Read: CENTCOM's public counters are re-stated, not strictly accumulated, and differencing them for tempo carries error bars of several units. The Oct 5 "13 destroyed over the past 12 weeks" is treated as superseded by the Oct 12 "10 destroyed."**
- **US embassy raises the Saudi aviation warning (Oct 12):** the mission urged Americans to exercise "extreme caution" at **all** major Saudi airports after additional attacks on King Khalid International and a targeted attack on King Fahd International in Dammam on Oct 11 (CBS Oct 12). **Read: the first blanket, country-wide US civil-aviation advisory of the Saudi front.**
- **IDF three-year data release (Oct 12):** ~**100,000 targets** struck since Oct 7 2023 across Gaza (~65,000), Lebanon and Syria (~28,000), **Iran (~4,500)** and **Yemen (~80)**; Israel absorbed ~**1,200 projectiles from Iran** and ~90 from Yemen; 970 personnel killed in operational circumstances; >70% of wounded returned to service ([Breaking Defense Oct 12](https://breakingdefense.com/2026/10/three-years-of-war-100000-targets-struck-idf-releases-new-conflict-data); [NewsCord summary of the IDF release Oct 7](https://newscord.org/watchdog/article/3f883c7a-7f41-449b-83e0-61ba0442ed60)). **Read: the first official Israeli denominator for the Iran campaign. 4,500 targets in Iran against 65,000 in Gaza quantifies how secondary the Iran front has been for the IAF in target-count terms, and is consistent with the Home Front alert floor.**

**Oct 11 UPDATE (carried) — a dated strike, on a merchant hull, outside Iran:**

- **The *Ocean Molica* (Oct 10):** a US fighter struck the stern of the **Panama-flagged bulk carrier *Ocean Molica*** in the Gulf of Oman. CENTCOM says the vessel ignored repeated warnings after departing an Iranian port and was attempting to violate the blockade, and that the strike disabled its propulsion without injuring the crew ([Al Jazeera Oct 11](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/11/us-forces-disable-ship-attempting-to-run-iran-blockade-in-gulf-of-oman); [Sherafy Oct 11, reproducing the CENTCOM statement](https://sherafy.com/ocean-molica-strike-iran-blockade)). **Read: this is not evidence of a resumption of strikes on Iranian territory.** The legal question — a neutral-flag merchant may be *captured* for blockade running on widely cited rules, but *attacking* one requires additional grounds — is live and unresolved in public; the warnings, the crew's responses and the verified voyage origin are not independently established.
- **Congressional loss ledger (Oct 8):** an updated Congressional Research Service report says **81 US fixed- and rotary-wing aircraft, including drones, have been lost or damaged** during US military operations against Iran since February 28 ([RFE/RL live Oct 8](https://www.rferl.org/a/iran-live-blog-trump-middle-east/33803414.html)). **Read: the first consolidated airframe-attrition figure of the war from a non-partisan congressional source; pair it with the munitions-rationing constraint when assessing sortie-generation capacity for a three-day package.**
- **The Houthi question enters the Iran decision (Oct 10):** Trump said he will decide soon whether to join the Saudis in the fight against the Houthis (RFE/RL live Oct 8–10; [ISW Oct 10](https://understandingwar.org/research/middle-east/iran-update-october-10-2026)). **See the Red Sea section — this is now the most likely route to a US kinetic action before Nov 3, because it sits outside the letter of the Oct 8 pledge, which covered Iran only.**

- **Three days, not a campaign (Oct 8–9):** the New York Times reports Trump has ordered the Pentagon to develop options for renewed military action, with possible targets including Iran's missile and drone arsenal, energy facilities and other military sites, and that the Pentagon has developed a plan for a **three-day campaign** ([Times of Israel liveblog Oct 9](https://www.timesofisrael.com/liveblog-oct-9-2026/amp/); [JPost Oct 9](https://www.jpost.com/middle-east/iran-news/article-911125); [The Media Line Oct 9](https://themedialine.org/headlines/president-trump-rules-out-iran-strike-before-midterms-despite-reports-of-military-plans)). **Read: a three-day package is materially smaller than the end-November three-CSG posture implies — it is consistent with the munitions-rationing constraint logged since July and with a punitive rather than disarming objective.**

Agent inference (medium): the pledge constrains the *date*, not the *decision*; expect the authorisation to be taken inside October and executed after Nov 3. Kinetic-window projection unchanged: late Nov 2026 – Feb 2027, anchored at late November.**
- **Sanctions tranche reconciled (Oct 8–9):** Treasury's OFAC designated **17 vessels** and their owners under Executive Order 13902; the State Department separately designated **five further vessels, ten entities and six individuals** — a consolidated **22 vessels, 27 companies and 6 individuals** across jurisdictions including India, Türkiye, China, the UAE, the UK and the Marshall Islands ([Marine Insight Oct 9](https://www.marineinsight.com/u-s-sanctions-22-vessels-and-27-companies-tied-to-irans-shadow-fleet-and-oil-sales); [Ritz Herald Oct 9](https://ritzherald.com/treasury-sanctions-17-tankers-in-iran-shadow-fleet-crackdown); [CiberCuba Oct 9](https://en.cibercuba.com/noticias/2026-10-09-u1-e13-s27061-nid342374-eeuu-sanciona-22-buques-flota-fantasma-iran-refuerza); [Iran International Oct 8](https://www.iranintl.com/en/202610085342)). - **Neither side will admit the calendar matters (Oct 10):** Trump and Iran's leadership each insist the US election calendar is not motivating them; persistent inflation and high gas prices have driven widespread American disapproval of the war, while Tehran holds that there will be no deal until the blockade is lifted and sanctions relieved ([AP/US News Oct 10](https://www.usnews.com/news/politics/articles/2026-10-10/trump-and-iran-agree-on-one-thing-the-us-election-calendar-isnt-driving-their-decisions-on-the-war)). **Read: the denial is itself the tell; both sides are managing a calendar they say is irrelevant.**

Dan Caine, Rubio or Hegseth rather than directly to Trump, viewing that as the proper role of a theatre commander ([JPost Oct 9](https://www.jpost.com/middle-east/iran-news/article-911111)). **Read: relevant to how fast an authorisation can convert into a strike package — one more node between the president and the theatre.**

## Iranian State Disintegration Tracker — Iran's Endurance Ceiling
*Last materially changed: 2026-10-15 (the Tether premium and the coin bubble both reverse within a week — the supply-side read is reaffirmed and the Oct 14 household-bid inference withdrawn; Bandar Shahid Rajaee exports 257 kt, above the rate implied by the state's own six-month claim; Tehran equities rally as the remaining savings vehicle)*

**Economic (confirmed, carried):** IMF −5.4% GDP projection 2026; war damage ~$270B (~90% pre-war GDP); inflation 88.6%+ (rural >100%); $27B capital flight 2025; autos −47%, petrochemicals −17%; approved foreign investment −80%; 20M+ bbl stranded afloat; China imports halved to 654K bpd (June); TGL revoked; dark-fleet channel only. Unemployment 9.1% (from 7.3%), 450,000 jobs lost.

**Oct 15 UPDATE — the one-week hard-asset bid evaporates, and the southern ports come back:**

- **Both premia reverse (tgju, agent arithmetic):** the rate is **2,679,000 IRR/USD, flat (+0%) on Oct 8** — a second consecutive flat week — while **Tether at 2,674,680 is a 0.2% *discount* to the cash dollar**, from a +0.4% premium a week ago. **Emami 2,699M = $1,007.5, down 1.3% in dollar terms**; 18k at 262.5M implies **~$4,063/oz fine against a world ounce of $4,143 — a 1.9% discount, wider than last week's 0.7%.** **The Oct 14 inference that a household hedging bid had appeared in the stablecoin and the minted coin is withdrawn on its own stated test. Two consecutive weeks in which a premium print failed to survive a second observation. Operational rule going forward: single-week premium signals in Tehran instruments are noise. The supply-side reading of the FX collapse stands unqualified.**
- **Bandar Shahid Rajaee validates the state's own export claim (PortWatch, week to Oct 9, agent arithmetic):** **19 calls (4 the prior week), 5 tanker calls (0), exports 257 kt against 93 kt in the same 2025 week — 276%**; imports 118 kt against 34 kt. **257 kt ÷ 7 = 36.7 kt/day, 32% above the 27.8 kt/day implied by the Hormozgan Ports and Maritime Administration's claim of 5.14 Mt on 325 vessels between 21 March and 22 September.** **Read: the Oct 13 fork — "either inflated, or roughly three-quarters of the terminal's tanker traffic is dark" — resolves toward the claim being broadly credible. The terminal is running, with transponders on, above its own six-month average.**
- **The southern ports lead for a second week:** monitored split **Gulf 40 vs Caspian 25** (same week 2025: 34 vs 436). Gulf calls nearly doubled from 21; **Bandar Khomeini imports 243 kt against 48 kt the prior week and 266 kt in 2025 — 91% of the pre-war week.** Bandar Abbas 6 calls of which 5 tanker. Caspian recovered to 25 (Amirabad 4→11, Nowshahr 3→8) but Anzali fell 12→6. Kharg: 4 calls, 1 tanker, **zero exports.** **Inference (medium): the blockade binds on oil exports and the shadow fleet, not on Iran's general import trade; the Caspian pivot was a wartime detour at the margin, not a structural reorientation of the country's logistics.**
- **Equities as the last store of value (Oct 13–14):** Iran's stock market has rallied despite power outages, the FX shortage and the currency's decline; Iran International's framing is that investors either expect a deal or have no other way to protect savings, with Khabar Online citing a 35,000-toman rise in the free dollar over the past month ([JPost Oct 14](https://www.jpost.com/middle-east/iran-news/article-911670)). **Read: consistent with the gold and stablecoin discounts — domestic savings are rotating into the one asset that cannot be frozen, confiscated at a customs post or sold at a rationed official rate.**
- **Connectivity and airspace:** IODA **11,552** reachable /24s against a prior six-day mean of 11,468 (+0.7%); BGP 41,648 against 41,622. Iranian ADS-B **65** aircraft against 68 a week ago (OpenSky). No shutdown signature.

- **The rate goes flat (tgju, agent arithmetic):** **2,628,850 IRR/USD, −0.0% against Oct 7's 2,634,100**, ending three consecutive weeks of appreciation. **Read: the retracement did not reverse; it simply stopped. The policy could hold the rate down but could not continue pulling it in.**
- **Both suppressed premia turn in the same week:** **Tether 2,638,870 = +0.4% over the cash dollar**, from **−0.9%** a week ago — the widest discount of the series flipping to a premium in seven days. On alanchand's independent board the **Full Imami coin prints a +3.80% bubble and rose 3.10% on the day**, while **18k gold is −1.06%** and **Bahar Azadi −0.10%**; the fractional coins remain extreme (quarter +15.96%, gram coin +13.30%, half +8.77%). World ounce $4,143.15 ([alanchand Oct 14](https://alanchand.com/en/gold-price)). **Agent arithmetic on tgju: 18k at 260.7M with the rial at 2,628,850 → ~$4,113/oz fine, a 0.7% local discount — the narrowest of the run. Emami 2,683M = $1,020.6, +1.9% in dollar terms in a single session from $1,001.2.** *(Withdrawn Oct 15 — the signal did not survive a second week.)*
- **Iran's production has a number (Oct 13):** OPEC's Monthly Oil Market Report puts **Iranian crude output at about 2 mb/d in September, down 99 kb/d on August**, inside a group total of 25.1 mb/d that rose 920 kb/d — **Saudi Arabia +744 kb/d to ~8 mb/d, Iraq +386 kb/d to 3.9 mb/d, UAE +86 kb/d to 3.9 mb/d, Kuwait −140 kb/d** ([Anadolu/OPEC Oct 13](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/energy/oil/opec-crude-production-jumps-920-000-bpd-in-september/60222)). **Read: Iran is still producing roughly half of its ~3.8 mb/d pre-war rate and is the only significant decliner in the group apart from Kuwait. - **The workaround channels, described from inside Tehran (Oct 14):** Al Jazeera's Tehran correspondent sets out how Iranians now move money abroad — currency brokers, hawala-type intermediaries and crypto — each with its own failure mode, after sanctions cut conventional cross-border payment ([Al Jazeera Oct 14](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/14/how-do-iranians-move-money-abroad-to-avoid-us-sanctions)). **Context, not a new data point; it is the retail-side counterpart to the "trustee" ecosystem logged Oct 1.**
