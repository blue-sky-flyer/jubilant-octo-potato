## Strategic Reserve Countdown — US Endurance Ceiling (Day 0)
*Last materially changed: 2026-08-27 (SPR confirmed 289.7M bbl per EIA w/e Aug 21, GEF updated Aug 27 12:30 UTC — 3.7M bbl weekly draw; lowest since December 1982; Day 0 clock updated)*

**One-line arithmetic (updated Aug 27):** SPR **289.7M bbl** (EIA w/e Aug 21, GEF updated Aug 27 12:30 UTC — confirmed as lowest since December 1982; Energy Factbook Aug 28 corroborates: "lowest level since November 26, 1982") minus political floor ~200M bbl = ~89.7M bbl usable buffer. At the **3.7M bbl weekly draw** observed in the week ending Aug 21, the political floor arrives in approximately **~24 weeks → Day 0 approximately mid-February 2027** at that rate. However, the two-week average from w/e Aug 7 (298.7M bbl) to w/e Aug 21 (289.7M bbl) is 4.5M bbl/week, implying **~20 weeks → early January 2027**. At the prior longer-run ~2.7M bbl/week average, Day 0 would arrive ~mid-May 2027. The operative range is now **early January – mid-February 2027** at current draw rates. *(Low-to-moderate confidence on precise timing.)*

**Aug 31 kinetic upside risk:** The supertanker mine strike (⚠️ single-sourced) and Larak/Jordan/UAE exchange are upside risks to the draw rate — if Hormuz transits collapse further from the ~5/day AIS-visible baseline, SPR draw accelerates, compressing the Day 0 clock. The next EIA release (w/e Aug 28, expected ~Sep 3) will be the first data point post-Aug 31 kinetic resumption.

**New data point — commercial crude BUILD (GEF Aug 27):** US commercial crude inventories at approximately 428.9 million barrels — a BUILD of 17.422 million barrels, the largest weekly increase since January 2023. DOE safe operating minimum ~70M bbl (distinct from ~200M bbl political floor). ⚠️ DOE safe-operating-minimum figure sourced via CNBC via DOE spokesperson — treat as directionally confirmed but not independently verified against published DOE technical standards.

**Supply context (carried):** IEA August OMR (Aug 8/12): 2026 demand now projected −1.6 mb/d; global supply 101.5 mb/d in July, 6.3 mb/d below year-ago; 8.3 mb/d of Gulf output still shut in; full-year supply shortfall averaging 4.3 mb/d.
