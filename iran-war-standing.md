## Strategic Reserve Countdown — US Endurance Ceiling (Day 0)
*Last materially changed: 2026-08-27 (SPR confirmed 289.7M bbl per EIA w/e Aug 21, GEF updated Aug 27 12:30 UTC — 3.7M bbl weekly draw; lowest since December 1982; Day 0 clock updated)*

**One-line arithmetic (updated):** SPR **289.7M bbl** (EIA w/e Aug 21, GEF updated Aug 27 12:30 UTC — confirmed as lowest since December 1982) minus political floor ~200M bbl = ~89.7M bbl usable buffer. At the **3.7M bbl weekly draw** observed in the week ending Aug 21, the political floor arrives in approximately **~24 weeks → Day 0 approximately mid-February 2027** at that rate. However, the two-week average from w/e Aug 7 (298.7M bbl) to w/e Aug 21 (289.7M bbl) is 4.5M bbl/week, implying **~20 weeks → early January 2027**. At the prior longer-run ~2.7M bbl/week average, Day 0 would arrive ~mid-May 2027. The operative range is now **early January – mid-February 2027** at current draw rates, compressing materially from the prior Aug 19 estimate of "approximately early December 2026 at accelerated rate / mid-May 2027 at prior average." ⚠️ The Washington Post Aug 27 "four to five weeks" Iranian resilience estimate (if confirmed) would imply Iran reaches its endurance ceiling well before the US reaches its SPR floor — this is a scenario-structurally significant development to track.

**New data point — commercial crude BUILD (GEF Aug 27):** US commercial crude inventories at approximately 428.9 million barrels — a BUILD of 17.422 million barrels, the largest weekly increase since January 2023. This is a demand-destruction signal consistent with IEA's −1.6 mb/d downgrade and does not alter the strategic draw trajectory. SPR draw continues independently of commercial builds.

**Salt cavern structural risk (carried from Aug 19):** Fortune's Aug 17 article reports that the drawdown now threatens physical damage to the 60 underground salt caverns. A DOE spokesperson told CNBC the reserve's safe operating minimum is roughly 70M bbl — distinct from the ~200M bbl political floor — below which cavern structural integrity may be at risk. ⚠️ DOE safe-operating-minimum figure sourced via CNBC via DOE spokesperson — treat as directionally confirmed but not independently verified against published DOE technical standards.

**Supply context (carried):** IEA August OMR (Aug 8): 2026 demand now projected −1.6 mb/d; global supply 101.5 mb/d in July, 6.3 mb/d below year-ago; 8.3 mb/d of Gulf output still shut in; full-year supply shortfall averaging 4.3 mb/d. The Iran-Oman interim framework (Aug 26), if formalized with a transit restart, would reduce the SPR draw rate and extend the Day 0 clock — the most significant structural SPR implication of any near-term diplomatic development. The Aug 27 tanker strike inside Hormuz reduces the probability of near-term framework implementation. *(Low-to-moderate confidence on precise timing.)*
