# Iran War — Standing Sections
*Slow-moving sections of the daily briefing live here, in full. The daily briefing carries ONE bullet + link per section, and reprints a section in full only on the day it materially changes. Each header carries a "last materially changed" date so readers can see staleness at a glance.*

**Update protocol (daily agent):** For each section below, ask one question each run: *did anything material change in the last 24 hours?* If no — touch nothing here, and the briefing bullet reads "unchanged since [date]". If yes — update the section here, bump its date, and summarize the delta in the briefing's "What changed" list. Never copy a full section back into the briefing.

## Iranian State Disintegration Tracker — Iran's Endurance Ceiling
*Last materially changed: 2026-09-28 (largest single-session rial move of the run — 3.62% — while the Emami coin falls in dollar terms; Tether premium widens to 0.41%; official/open-market multiple at 1.41x)*

**Economic (confirmed, carried):** IMF −5.4% GDP projection 2026; war damage ~$270B (~90% pre-war GDP); inflation 88.6%+ (rural >100%); $27B capital flight 2025; autos −47%, petrochemicals −17%; approved foreign investment −80%; 20M+ bbl stranded afloat; China imports halved to 654K bpd (June); TGL revoked; dark-fleet channel only. **Sep 28 UPDATE — the rejection is priced in the currency and nowhere else:**

- **Largest single-session rial move of the run (tgju, Sep 28):** **2,440,000 IRR/USD**, **3.62% weaker** than Friday's 2,354,800, +5.6% versus Sep 23's 2,310,000 and roughly **+22% versus the ~2.0M of late August**. Independent corroboration of direction from arzbin, whose hourly note attributes the move to the UK arrests near a base used for Iran operations ([arzbin Sep 28](https://www.arzbin.com/blog/arzbin-hourly-2026-09-28-11h)).

- **Tether premium widens for a second session (tgju, Sep 28):** **2,450,010 = +0.41% over the cash dollar**, from +0.10% on Sep 27 and discounts of 0.14% (Sep 26), 0.78% (Sep 25) and 0.9% (Sep 23). **Read:** five observations, monotone, now two sessions into positive territory. Still an order of magnitude below the 3% sustained-premium trigger, but this is the single cleanest exit-demand series available and it has only moved one way.

- **The coin falls in dollars on a 3.6% devaluation day (agent arithmetic on tgju, Sep 28):** Emami **2,475M IRR ÷ 2,440,000 = $1,014.3**, against **$1,019.2** on Sep 25 and Sep 27 — **−0.5%**. 18k gram 245.3M IRR. arzbin separately logs the coin at 242.5M toman, +0.8% in local terms, with 18k gold "relatively stable" at 24,024,654 toman/gram. **Read:** the entire rial move passed through without a hard-asset bid; Tehran's bullion market is not participating in the devaluation. The anti-panic finding holds and strengthens — the adjustment is running through the exchange rate alone.

- **Official rate moves too; the gap is 1.41x (Trend/CBI, Sep 28):** the Central Bank set **1,728,507 IRR/USD**, from 1,717,400 on Sep 27 (+0.65% weaker), with 45 currencies stronger against the rial on the day ([Trend Sep 28](https://www.trend.az/iran/4228620.html)). **Agent arithmetic:** open market 2,440,000 ÷ official 1,728,507 = **1.41x**. **Read:** the managed rate is being walked down in step rather than defended, which is consistent with reserve conservation rather than reserve depletion.

- **Connectivity dip reversed (IODA Sep 28):** **11,094 reachable /24s against a prior six-day mean of 11,218 — only 1.1% below**, versus Friday's −4.2%; BGP prefixes 41,741 against a 41,709 mean. Friday's widest-of-run deviation was transient, not the onset of a shutdown. Iranian ADS-B **64 aircraft**, from 73 on Sep 27 and 71 a week ago.

**Sep 27 UPDATE (carried) — the stablecoin turns, the bullion market does not:**

- **CORRECTION — the coin premium is ~1.2%, not 2.4% (agent arithmetic on alanchand, Sep 26):** the gram-based method adopted Sep 26 assumed Tehran's 18k gram trades at intrinsic. It does not: alanchand's Sep 26 board shows **18k gold per gram 238.4M IRR against a 241.3M "real price" (−1.20%)** and raw 24k mesghal also at −1.20%, while the **Emami coin's own bubble is +1.16%** ([alanchand Sep 26](https://alanchand.com/en/gold-price)). The gram basis therefore overstates the coin premium by roughly 1.2pp. **Read:** Tehran's physical gold is trading *below* global intrinsic, which is the opposite of a bullion scramble, and the coin bubble is ~1.2%, two orders of magnitude from the 20–40% of past Iranian monetary panics.

## On the Ground in Iran
*Last materially changed: 2026-09-27 (the Caspian's capacity ceiling gets a physical mechanism: water levels down ~2m since the 1990s and falling more than 20cm a year, per Iran's own Department of Environment)*

**Sep 27 UPDATE — the northern route is draft-limited, not paperwork-limited:**

- **Caspian water levels are falling and constrain the trade (Al Jazeera, reporting from Tehran, Sep 27, new):** UNEP put Caspian levels about **2 metres lower than in the 1990s**, with projections of a further 8–21 metres of recession by the end of the century; Shahram Fadakar of Iran's Department of Environment told local media that levels have been dropping by **more than 20 cm a year over the past two to three years**, which "could affect trade between all Caspian countries." Iran is nonetheless looking to the Caspian as its portal for imports and oil export while the southern ports are blockaded; experts cited describe significant logistical challenges ([Al Jazeera Sep 27](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/9/27/can-iran-shift-trade-north-to-caspian-sea-as-war-impairs-strait-of-hormuz)).
- **Agent inference (medium-high confidence):** this supplies the missing mechanism for the **0.9 kt per call** efficiency ratio that has now held across five consecutive weekly PortWatch prints. Falling water caps usable draft at Anzali, Amirabad and Nowshahr, which caps parcel size regardless of how many calls the ports handle — and the call count has already risen (47 vs 15 in 2025) without tonnage following. Dredging and new tonnage are multi-year projects; nothing relieves this inside the war. **Consequence:** the Q2 trigger condition (Caspian tonnage above ~3 kt/call) is now unlikely to fire at all, and Iran's marginal import capacity is definitively set at the land borders, where Gürbulak dwell already exceeds five days.

**Sep 24 UPDATE (carried) — the land border, finally measured:**

- **Gürbulak/Bazargan dwell times up roughly two-thirds (AP, Sep 24):** AP reporters at Turkey's largest crossing into Iran describe hundreds of trucks idling and lines stretching for miles. A driver with 15 years on the route, Muhammed Rıza Shiri, 55, waited **four days** to carry a load of rubber into Iran, at the end of a **7-kilometre** queue: "We used to wait, maximum, for three days here. Because of the war, the waiting times exceed five days." Pezeshkian has pressed to accelerate overland trade as the blockade cuts off the majority of imports ([AP Sep 24](https://apnews.com/article/iran-turkey-border-trade-trucks-delays-war-11f510a93bdf56754f994faff99b3e02)).
- **Agent arithmetic and inference (medium-high confidence):** border dwell has gone from ≤3 days to >5, i.e. **+67% or more**. On a notional 10-day round trip of which three days were waiting, a move to five-plus days lengthens the cycle to 12+ days — a **17–20% reduction in trips per truck per year** for an essentially fixed regional fleet. Because the constraint is queue time rather than road capacity, extra trucks do not relieve it; only customs throughput or new gates do. This is the quantitative form of Fortune's Sep 20 "red tape and gridlock" finding and **fires the Q2 trigger condition carried since Sep 20**.
- **Read:** both of Iran's substitution channels are now demonstrably capacity-capped rather than scaling — the Caspian at a fixed 0.9 kt per call, the land border at a fixed number of customs slots per day. Turkish customs throughput and the two crossings under negotiation are the highest-leverage variables in Iran's import position.

**Sep 23 UPDATE (carried) — the inversion holds, the base keeps shrinking:**

- **Port-call split, week to Sep 18 (PortWatch):** Caspian **47 calls** (Amirabad 22, Anzali 15, Nowshahr 10) against **17** across all eleven monitored Gulf ports. Same week 2025: Caspian 15, Gulf 44. The inversion is now four weeks old and deepening on the Gulf side — Gulf calls fell from 23 to 17 week-on-week, with Bandar Shahid Rajaee down from 9 calls to 2 and zero tanker calls at every Iranian Gulf port in the set.
- **Tonnage (agent arithmetic on PortWatch):** Caspian imports **43 kt on 47 calls = 0.91 kt/call**; Gulf imports **133 kt on 17 calls = 7.8 kt/call**. Total visible seaborne imports **176 kt, −24% week-on-week** (from 231 kt) and now roughly half the 2025 level. Caspian share of visible import tonnage **24%**, from 20%.
- **Read:** the efficiency ratio is unchanged — the northern route has not become more capable, the southern route has simply shrunk further. The Caspian is absorbing share by default.
- **Exports remain immaterial in every direction:** Caspian exports 7 kt for the week; Iranian Gulf exports 152 kt, of which 136 kt is Bandar-e Pars Terminal. Kharg Island: zero calls, zero tanker calls, zero tonnage for a fourth consecutive week.

**Sep 20 UPDATE (carried) — the northern pivot, measured:**

- **Iran's port-call geography inverted (PortWatch, week to Sep 11):** Caspian 51 calls against 23 in the Gulf; same week 2025 Caspian 22 / Gulf 46. Caspian calls +132% YoY; Gulf calls −50%.
- **But the tonnage is small:** Caspian imports 46 kt on 51 calls; Gulf 185 kt on 23 calls. Caspian share of visible seaborne imports rose from ~4% to ~20%; the Caspian gain of 30 kt offset **19%** of the Gulf's 156 kt loss.
- **Read:** the substitution is real, high-frequency and roughly a ninth as efficient per movement — sufficient to sustain essential goods and structurally incapable of replacing export earnings.
