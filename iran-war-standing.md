# Iran War — Standing Sections
*Slow-moving sections of the daily briefing live here, in full. The daily briefing carries ONE bullet + link per section, and reprints a section in full only on the day it materially changes. Each header carries a "last materially changed" date so readers can see staleness at a glance.*

**Update protocol (daily agent):** For each section below, ask one question each run: *did anything material change in the last 24 hours?* If no — touch nothing here, and the briefing bullet reads "unchanged since [date]". If yes — update the section here, bump its date, and summarize the delta in the briefing's "What changed" list. Never copy a full section back into the briefing.

## US Military Posture & Munitions
*Last materially changed: 2026-10-09 (Trump commits in writing to no strike on Iran before Nov 3 while the Pentagon's order to CENTCOM to conclude strike preparations stands; IDF chief Zamir told to prepare for a massive attack within three weeks; USS Abraham Lincoln returns home after 321 days)*

**Oct 9 UPDATE — a dated presidential commitment, against a completed military plan:**

- **The pledge, in writing (Oct 8):** "We are having productive discussions with the Islamic Republic of Iran. We will not be attacking Iran at any time prior to the midterm elections to be held in the United States on November 3rd," Trump wrote on Truth Social, adding that the blockade "will remain in full force and effect" and that Iran "will not have a nuclear weapon" ([Times of Israel Oct 8](https://www.timesofisrael.com/trump-says-us-wont-hit-iran-this-month-as-zamir-said-to-warn-strike-could-delay-israels-election/amp/); [Anadolu Oct 9](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/energy/oil/oil-retreats-as-trump-rules-out-iran-attack-before-us-midterms/60128)). He also claimed **22 million barrels** passed through Hormuz "later Wednesday" with "not one barrel" from or to Iran. **Attribute as a presidential claim; irreconcilable with Kpler's 10.1 mb/d for the same period — see Gulf States Adaptation.**
- **The order it sits against (Oct 7):** the Pentagon instructed US Central Command **several days earlier to conclude preparations for resuming major combat operations in Iran**; the directive carried no launch date and no final decision had been taken ([Newsquawk/Axios Oct 7](https://www.newsquawk.com/headlines/us-military-has-been-ordered-to-be-ready-for-possible-iran-strikes-as-president-trump-weighs-the-timing-while-pentagon-instructed-us-centcom-several-days-ago-to-conclude-preparations-for-resuming-major-combat-operations-in-iran-according-to-axios); [ground.news aggregate Oct 8](https://ground.news/daily-briefing/sources-for-axis-trump-did-not-decide-whether-to-resume-strikes-against-iran-and-did-not-make-any-final-decision); [Plataforma Media Oct 8](https://www.plataformamedia.com/en/2026/10/08/us-military-instructed-prepare-potential-iran-strikes)). Officials said any resumption would likely be coordinated with Israel.
- **The Israeli channel says three weeks (Oct 8):** senior US officers told IDF Chief of Staff Lt. Gen. Eyal Zamir that the White House and Pentagon had instructed them to prepare for a **massive attack on Iran within three weeks** — a window ending just before the midterms. Zamir replied that such an attack could force postponement of Israel's **Oct 27** election, since Jerusalem assesses Iran would answer with massive missile fire at Israel; the IAF and Military Intelligence are on heightened readiness and building new Iranian target lists ([Ynet Oct 8](https://www.ynetnews.com/article/r1ybtvboml); [Times of Israel Oct 8](https://www.timesofisrael.com/trump-says-us-wont-hit-iran-this-month-as-zamir-said-to-warn-strike-could-delay-israels-election/amp/)). A senior White House official told Channel 12 Iran did not come up in Trump's Oct 7 call with Netanyahu.
- **Agent inference (medium-high): the pledge and the plan are sequenced, not contradictory — finish the planning now, execute after Nov 3.** The written, dated commitment is the strongest public constraint on pre-midterm action yet issued and narrows the pre-Nov-3 tail that the Oct 7 readiness order had widened. It is also cheap to reverse. **Kinetic-window projection unchanged: late Nov 2026 – Feb 2027, anchored at late November.** CNN's framing — that both sides are now waiting out the midterms, and that Iran has succeeded in imposing political cost without forcing a retreat, so an Iranian escalation in the next three weeks cannot be ruled out ([CNN Oct 9](https://www.cnn.com/2026/10/09/politics/trump-iran-war-midterms)) — is adopted as the working frame.
- **Hull count falls by one at the margin (Oct 8–9):** USS **Abraham Lincoln** returned to Naval Air Station North Island, ending a **321-day deployment including 265 consecutive days at sea, a Navy record**; Hegseth boarded at sea Oct 7 and awarded the Presidential Unit Citation for Operation Epic Fury. Summer reporting described food and supply shortages and two sailors attempting to go overboard ([Anadolu/USNI Oct 9](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/americas/uss-abraham-lincoln-returns-to-san-diego-after-supporting-iran-war/4082825)). **Read: confirms no fourth carrier is in the pipeline and quantifies the readiness cost of the three-CSG package.**
- **Sanctions, not strikes, remain the active instrument (Oct 8):** the US sanctioned **17 vessels** plus individuals, companies and petrochemical products for moving Iranian crude ([CNBC Oct 9](https://www.cnbc.com/amp/2026/10/09/oil-falls-as-trump-comments-on-iran-talks-ease-supply-concerns.html)); a separate maritime-intelligence tally puts the Oct 8 tranche at **22 tankers** ([Eagle Intelligence Oct 9](https://eagleintelmari.com/news)) — the two counts are not reconciled; use 17 as the wire-confirmed figure.
- **What has NOT changed:** the European bomber node remains empty since Oct 4; no Diego Garcia rotation; no THAAD redeployment into theatre; and **no dated CENTCOM release covers strikes on Iranian territory after Sep 1 — day 38.**
