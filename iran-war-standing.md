# Iran War — Standing Sections
*Slow-moving sections of the daily briefing live here, in full. The daily briefing carries ONE bullet + link per section, and reprints a section in full only on the day it materially changes. Each header carries a "last materially changed" date so readers can see staleness at a glance.*

**Update protocol (daily agent):** For each section below, ask one question each run: *did anything material change in the last 24 hours?* If no — touch nothing here, and the briefing bullet reads "unchanged since [date]". If yes — update the section here, bump its date, and summarize the delta in the briefing's "What changed" list. Never copy a full section back into the briefing.

## US Military Posture & Munitions
*Last materially changed: 2026-10-18 (CENTCOM commander Cooper's Oct 16 video ledger — $25bn of crude blocked, ~$350m of crypto frozen, Iranian and militia payroll arrears; a throughput claim that reconciles to the regional, not the strait, series; CENTCOM says most Iranian attacks fail; day 47 with no release covering strikes on Iranian soil after Sep 1)*

**Oct 18 UPDATE — the blockade ledger is now stated in dollars, and the throughput claim finally reconciles:**

- **Cooper's economic ledger (Oct 16):** in a video statement, CENTCOM commander Adm. Brad Cooper said US operations have **prevented Iran from exporting at least $25 billion of crude oil** and helped **freeze nearly $350 million in cryptocurrency**, and that "Iran's government is withholding salaries, pensions and benefits to workers in industries such as oil and energy, manufacturing, construction, security and the IRGC itself." He added that Iran-aligned militias are reportedly **withholding pay from their own fighters** to divert funds to the Revolutionary Guards, and linked IRGC "desperation" to the tanker attacks ([Iran International Oct 16](https://www.iranintl.com/en/202610162840); [Times of Israel Oct 17](https://www.timesofisrael.com/centcom-chief-reports-irans-proxies-holding-back-salaries-to-send-money-to-irgc)). **Attribute all of it as CENTCOM claims. The payroll-arrears line is the same category of assertion as Trump's August "Iran has difficulty paying its own troops" and remains uncorroborated by any Iranian source; the $25bn figure is a counterfactual (barrels not sold at an unstated price), not a measurement.**
- **The throughput claim, and its reconciliation (Oct 16):** a non-primary summary of the same video reports Cooper saying **about 400 ships and 100 million barrels** moved through the Strait of Hormuz in the past week ([YouTube summary Oct 16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJGzWZfGGlw)) — **single-sourced; the primary carriers do not repeat the line.** Agent arithmetic: 100m bbl ÷ 7 = **14.3 mb/d**, which is not Kpler's 8.5 mb/d strait figure but is almost exactly Kpler's **14 mb/d for the entire region including pipelines to the Gulf of Oman and Red Sea** ([CNBC Oct 14](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/14/container-ship-sinks-strait-hormuz-as-iran-war-attacks.html)). **Read: CENTCOM's public throughput numbers appear to be regional export totals presented as strait transits. That is the explanation for the Oct 8 "22 million barrels" and Oct 12 "19 million barrels" claims too, and it retires the assumption that the gap can only be dark tonnage.** 400 ships/week = 57/day against the AIS-visible 4.1/day, a 14x gap that no definitional fix closes; the vessel-count claim is not adopted.
- **CENTCOM on attack efficacy (Oct 17):** a CENTCOM spokesman says the "vast majority" of Iran's attempted attacks in the Strait of Hormuz are unsuccessful ([Fox News Oct 17](https://www.foxnews.com/world/vast-majority-irans-attempted-attacks-strait-hormuz-unsuccessful-centcom-spokesman-says)). **Read: this is a claim about a denominator neither side publishes. It sits against a record October incident count and three sunk hulls, and it is reconcilable only if the attempt rate is far above the verified-strike rate — which is also what the Oct 2 Asharq "30 drones + 10 missiles a week" figure implies. Treat both as the same unmeasured series.**
- **What has NOT changed:** the European bomber node remains empty since Oct 4; no Diego Garcia rotation; no THAAD redeployment into theatre; and **no dated CENTCOM release covers strikes on Iranian territory after Sep 1 — day 47.**

**Oct 17 UPDATE (carried) — the target that the MOAB reporting named is sealed and static:**

- **Pickaxe Mountain is dormant (Vantor imagery Oct 7, published Oct 16):** the Institute for Science and International Security reports the **eastern tunnel entrances remain blocked by earth** and the **earthen mound roadblocks to the western tunnel portals remain in place**, and concludes that "the two-month-long restricted access reinforces the assessment that the facility is currently **neither operational nor continuing major construction**." ISIS says this supports public reporting — Israeli intelligence via WSJ, July 2026 — that **centrifuges and centrifuge components were moved into the tunnel complex after the 12-Day War** ([ISIS Oct 16](https://isis-online.org/isis-reports/activity-remains-dormant-at-pickaxe-mountain)). **Read: the passive defences logged Oct 13 are not a prelude to activity; they are a seal. The site is a storage problem, not a production problem.**
- **ISIS on the strike options, in its own voice:** the report argues a combination of the June 2025 Natanz/Fordow approach and the Spring 2026 Shahid Boroujerdi approach "appears to make the most sense" — bunker busters against ventilation shafts, collapsing entrances, destroying external utilities — and states that ISIS has **identified a probable and a possible ventilation shaft** at Pickaxe from satellite imagery. It adds that the US government may instead judge the **MOAB** best, "according to CNN reporting," because Iranian tunnel blast traps are designed against far smaller shockwaves (ISIS Oct 16). **Read: this is the first named-analyst engagement with the MOAB option, but ISIS is reasoning from CNN's reporting, not corroborating it. The MOAB target-set claim remains single-originator and is still not adopted into scenario weights.** A successful strike, ISIS says, would place Pickaxe in the same category as Natanz and Esfahan: "forced, monitored closure, with lack of information about extent of damage inside."
- **The Saudi air-defence denominator (Oct 15–16):** the Saudi-led coalition states that **King Khalid International Airport in Riyadh has been attacked 20 times since Oct 1**, with a cumulative **17 killed and 333 injured**; Jazan, Abha and Najran airports have been closed repeatedly, and King Khalid is expected to remain closed until at least **21:00 UTC on Oct 17** ([Solace Global Oct 16](https://www.solaceglobal.com/news/2026/10/16/gulf-sitrep-1610)). The coalition struck Houthi oil facilities at Sanaa and Hodeidah on Oct 13. As of Oct 16 the airspaces of Jordan, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Bahrain, Qatar, the UAE and Oman are open. **Read: 20 attacks in 15 days against one airport is a sustained salvo rate that would consume interceptor stocks at a theatre-relevant pace; this is the clearest quantification yet of the Saudi-front magazine problem.**
- **Two cargo vessels sank on Oct 14 — the first confirmed sinkings since March** — and Iranian officials have stated they are using new tactics against shipping to raise pressure before Nov 3 (Solace Global Oct 16). **Attribute the intent as an Iranian claim; the sinkings are independently carried.**
- **What has NOT changed:** the European bomber node remains empty since Oct 4; no Diego Garcia rotation; no THAAD redeployment into theatre; and **no dated CENTCOM release covers strikes on Iranian territory after Sep 1 — day 46.**

**Oct 15 UPDATE (carried) — the target set widens to energy, and the Israeli channel cools:**

- **MOAB enters the reported option set (Oct 14–15):** CNN reports, via multiple US officials, that US strike plans include the **largest non-nuclear weapon in the inventory**, to be used against **Iranian energy infrastructure and nuclear sites, including Pickaxe Mountain** ([JPost liveblog carrying CNN Oct 15](https://www.jpost.com/middle-east/iran-news/2026-10-15/live-updates-911752); [CNN video Oct 15](https://edition.cnn.com/2026/10/15/world/video/school-roof-collapses-in-pakistan-killing-dozens-vrtc)). **Two readings, both material. First, the weapon's own designation — Massive Ordnance Air Blast — describes a surface-blast effect, which sits oddly against the deeply buried tunnel complex at Pickaxe Mountain and fits better against the tunnel *entrances* that last month's earthworks were built to protect (agent inference, medium). Second, and more consequential for markets: naming *energy infrastructure* as a target category would reverse the July 9 Kharg doctrine, under which CENTCOM struck Kharg's military targets while deliberately preserving oil export infrastructure.** Single-channel reporting carried by an aggregator; **not yet adopted into scenario weights.**
- **MI5 escalates the UK attribution (Oct 13–14):** Director General **Ken McCallum** said Iran is trying to hit military sites in Britain and that the suspected September plot against the US-run air base was **"not unique,"** framing Iranian and Russian hybrid activity in the UK as expanding ([Haaretz/AP Oct 14](https://www.haaretz.com/world-news/europe/2026-10-14/ty-article/mi5-chief-iran-targeting-u-k-military-sites-raf-fairford-plot-not-unique/000001a1-394f-d57f-abbb-3fcfff380000)). **Read: this converts the Fairford episode from a single incident into a declared campaign, and retrospectively strengthens the case that the bomber withdrawal from Fairford was threat-driven rather than posture-driven.**
- **The Israeli channel cools (Oct 10–14):** Netanyahu said an attack on Iran is **"not convenient" right now** ([Times of Israel Oct 14](https://www.timesofisrael.com/liveblog_entry/netanyahu-attack-on-iran-not-convenient-right-now-ben-gvir-wont-be-defense-minister-in-next-government/)). Haaretz reports IDF officials assess Iran has **replenished much of its ballistic missile stockpile and is accelerating production**, but that a pre-election strike **"could be perceived as politically motivated and erode public trust"** ([Haaretz Oct 10](https://www.haaretz.com/israel-news/israel-security/2026-10-10/ty-article/.premium/idf-officials-warn-pre-election-iran-strike-could-seem-politically-motivated/000001a1-2711-d5af-a3b7-2fb18fe70000)). **Read: three independent channels now describe the pre-Oct-27 window as closed by politics on both sides. The kinetic-window anchor at late November is reinforced, not challenged.**
- **The throughput claim is contradicted on its own date (Oct 14):** CENTCOM's assertion that US forces helped move **more than 19 million barrels** through Hormuz on Monday Oct 12 is contradicted by Kpler, which records **3 million barrels of crude** transiting that Monday (CNBC Oct 14). **Read: CENTCOM's public throughput figures are an instrument of the information fight and should not be used as a flow series.** *(Qualified Oct 18: the gap is now better explained as a regional-versus-strait definitional difference than as dark tonnage.)*

**Oct 14 UPDATE (carried) — Iran is fortifying against a raid, not only a bombing, and CENTCOM is fighting the throughput narrative in public:**

- **Passive defences at Natanz and Pickaxe Mountain (Oct 13):** the Institute for Science and International Security, assessing **Vantor** satellite imagery, concludes Iran built **makeshift dirt barriers around the Natanz and Pickaxe Mountain (Kuh-e Kolang Gaz La) sites last month**, designed to stop **vehicles and personnel reaching deeply buried tunnel entrances** — a defence against infiltration rather than against bombs. Pickaxe Mountain sits about a mile south of the Natanz enrichment plant, was never struck during the war, and remains under construction ([WSJ Oct 13](https://www.wsj.com/world/middle-east/iran-is-fortifying-nuclear-sites-and-rallying-its-people-for-a-return-to-fighting-c4cc3c28); [ISIS report](https://isis-online.org/isis-reports/imagery-update-passive-defenses-identified-at-natanz-and-pickaxe-mountain); [SOFX Oct 14](https://www.sofx.com/iran-fortifies-nuclear-sites-and-threatens-saudi-oil-facilities); [New Arab Oct 13](https://www.newarab.com/news/iran-fortifies-nuclear-sites-prepares-renewed-war-report); [JPost Oct 13](https://www.jpost.com/middle-east/iran-news/article-911555)). **Read: Tehran's engineering effort is pricing a ground or special-forces element that the reported three-day air package does not contain. Either Iran is hedging against a capability the US has not signalled, or it reads the Pentagon's options differently than the press does.** *(Qualified Oct 17: ISIS now reads the same earthworks as evidence of dormancy, not of preparation for activity.)*
- **Iranian deterrent messaging to Arab capitals (Oct 13):** Iranian officials warned Arab counterparts that **Saudi oil facilities, Emirati infrastructure and US warships** could be targeted if Washington strikes again ([Türkiye Today Oct 13](https://www.turkiyetoday.com/region/iran-warns-of-ground-incursions-saudi-oil-strikes-if-us-attacks-again-report-3230224); WSJ Oct 13). **Attribute as reported Iranian private warnings. It pairs with Rezaei's Oct 11 threat to cables, data centres and AI facilities and with the Oct 5 Fadavi threat tied explicitly to a ground attack.**
- **CENTCOM in the information fight (Oct 13–14):** "IRGC's navy commander has no navy. American forces and regional partners clearly control the Strait of Hormuz," CENTCOM posted, saying US forces helped Gulf partners move **more than 19 million barrels of crude through the strait on Monday Oct 12** ([Anadolu Oct 14](http://www.aa.com.tr/en/americas/us-rejects-iran-s-claim-of-control-over-strait-of-hormuz/4087883); [Iran International Oct 13](https://www.iranintl.com/en/202610133999)). **Attribute as a CENTCOM claim.** It is the second near-pre-war throughput assertion in a week after the president's Oct 8 "22 million barrels" and cannot be reconciled with the AIS record except by assuming the overwhelming majority of movement is dark.

But the **destroyed-vessel count moved 13 → 10**, which is impossible for a true cumulative. **Read: CENTCOM's public counters are re-stated, not strictly accumulated, and differencing them for tempo carries error bars of several units. The Oct 5 "13 destroyed over the past 12 weeks" is treated as superseded by the Oct 12 "10 destroyed."**
