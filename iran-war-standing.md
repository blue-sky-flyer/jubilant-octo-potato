## Strategic Reserve Countdown — US Endurance Ceiling (Day 0)
*Last materially changed: 2026-08-19 (SPR confirmed 298.7M bbl per EIA w/e Aug 7 via GEF Aug 18 / TradingEconomics; 6.1M bbl weekly draw — accelerated pace; salt cavern structural damage risk identified per Fortune Aug 17; Day 0 clock compressed toward ~Dec 2026 at current draw rate)*

**One-line arithmetic (updated):** SPR **298.7M bbl** (EIA w/e Aug 7, GEF updated Aug 18; TradingEconomics — confirmed below 300M bbl for first time since January 1983) minus political floor ~200M bbl = ~98.7M bbl usable buffer. At the **accelerated draw rate of ~6.1M bbl/week** observed in the week ending Aug 7 (down from 304.8M bbl to 298.7M bbl), the political floor arrives in approximately **~16 weeks → Day 0 approximately early December 2026** — materially earlier than the prior mid-May 2027 estimate based on the ~0.38M bbl/day (~2.7M bbl/week) longer-term average. ⚠️ The 6.1M bbl single-week draw may reflect a one-week anomaly rather than a sustained new rate; the prior ~0.38M bbl/day draw rate would imply Day 0 ~mid-May 2027. Both bounds are tracked; the range is now Dec 2026 – May 2027 depending on draw normalization.

**New structural risk — salt cavern integrity (Aug 19 addition):** Fortune's Aug 17 article reports that the drawdown now threatens physical damage to the 60 underground salt caverns storing American oil. A DOE spokesperson told CNBC the reserve's safe operating minimum is roughly 70M bbl — distinct from the ~200M bbl political floor — below which cavern structural integrity may be at risk. This introduces a hard physical floor materially above the political floor, potentially compressing the usable buffer further. ⚠️ The ~70M bbl DOE safe-operating-minimum figure is sourced via CNBC via DOE spokesperson — treat as directionally confirmed but not independently verified against published DOE technical standards.

**Commercial inventory context:** The large US commercial crude BUILD of 17.4M bbl (EIA, week reported Aug 12 — largest since early 2023; GEF Aug 18 confirms 424.4M bbl commercial stocks) is a domestic commercial stock event, not an SPR event, and does not alter the strategic draw trajectory. It likely reflects demand destruction and refinery run reductions consistent with IEA's −1.6 mb/d demand downgrade. SPR draw continues independently of commercial builds.

**Supply context:** IEA August OMR (Aug 8): 2026 demand now projected −1.6 mb/d (from −1.0 in July); global supply 101.5 mb/d in July, 6.3 mb/d below year-ago; 8.3 mb/d of Gulf output still shut in; full-year supply shortfall averaging 4.3 mb/d — largest peacetime shortfall in agency history. OPEC August report (Aug 13) cut 2026 demand growth to 600K bpd from 800K bpd, independently corroborating demand destruction. IEF (Aug 14) identified a 2.2 mb/d demand gap from demand-side angle. Political floor ~200M bbl is a consensus estimate, not confirmed by Treasury/DOE. The primary window for kinetic action (mid-Oct 2026 – Feb 2027) now arrives before even the accelerated Day 0 estimate. *(Low-to-moderate confidence on precise timing.)*

## Red Sea / Bab el-Mandeb — Baseline
*Last materially changed: 2026-08-20 (Houthi "second phase" threat — combined Red Sea/Gulf of Aden/Arabian Sea blockade + strikes inside Saudi Arabia + border ground operations — The Media Line, Aug 20)*

**Houthi "second phase" threat (Aug 20 — new):** Yemen's Iran-backed Houthis are threatening a formal "second phase" of their confrontation with Saudi Arabia, per Lebanon's Al-Akhbar newspaper citing military sources ([The Media Line](https://themedialine.org/headlines/houthis-threaten-second-phase-against-saudi-arabia-with-naval-blockade-strikes-inside-kingdom), Aug 20 — site header confirms "Thursday 08/20/2026"). The second phase would encompass: (1) restricting shipping across a combined Red Sea, Gulf of Aden, and Arabian Sea zone simultaneously — the most expansive Houthi maritime-blockade claim of the conflict; (2) targeting Saudi military mobilization centers inside Yemen; (3) retaliatory strikes "on strategic sites deep inside Saudi territory" if Saudi airspace is violated; (4) possible ground operations along the Saudi-Yemeni border. ⚠️ Single-sourced (The Media Line citing Al-Akhbar citing military sources); flagged. Directionally consistent with ACLED's Aug 19 assessment of escalating large-scale Yemen war risk. If operationalized, a combined three-sea-zone blockade would close the Cape-route alternative for tankers currently using the Arabian Sea leg of the Cape routing — a materially different threat than the current Bab el-Mandeb/Red Sea blockade alone.

**ACLED Aug 19 assessment — highest-risk Red Sea update of the conflict (carried from Aug 19):** ACLED's Aug 19 special report assesses: Houthis are conducting "an aerial campaign against Saudi Arabia's oil facilities and tankers in parallel with a surge in violence within Yemen." Saudi forces have conducted retaliatory strikes against Houthi military targets. ACLED concludes: "The persistent escalation on multiple fronts increases the risk of renewed large-scale violence in Yemen, not seen since 2022." The Houthis are "unlikely to cease their attacks without a concession from Saudi Arabia."

**Houthi Saudi military ship attack claim (Aug 18 — ⚠️ Houthi claim):** Houthis claimed an attack on a Saudi military landing ship and four escort vessels off Yemen's Mocha (Firstpost Live, Aug 18). ⚠️ Houthi claim; no Saudi confirmation in pack.

**Transit data:** UANI Aug 17: ~40% tanker transit drop since Houthi blockade declared Jul 20. JPost/Kpler (mid-Aug): just 1 vessel crossed Bab el-Mandeb on a recent day (near-nadir).

**Aug 17 escalation — Houthi ballistic missile strikes on Mokha:** Houthis fired two ballistic missiles at the port of Mokha (al-Makha), per Yemeni government forces ([Al Arabiya English Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/p/DcI3U6dlqyI), Aug 17). ⚠️ Single-sourced on Aug 17 strike; flagged.

**Aug 16 escalation — Mokha port suspension:** Yemen's internationally recognized government suspended operations at the port of al-Makha (Mokha) following Houthi attacks that killed seven people and caused approximately $16 million in losses ([CNN liveblog](https://www.cnn.com/2026/08/16/world/live-news/iran-war-trump), Aug 16).

**Aug 12–13 escalation (confirmed):** Houthi ballistic missile strike on MV Tihama in Bab el-Mandeb killed four to six crew (JPost Aug 12 / The National Aug 13 — double-sourced).

**Saudi ground-push deliberations (Aug 13):** Saudi Arabia is weighing whether to back a Yemeni ground campaign to retake the Red Sea coast from the Houthis (JPost/The Media Line, Aug 13; Al Jazeera, Aug 13 — double-sourced). The Houthi "second phase" threat (Aug 20) makes this decision more acute.

Cargo ship sunk by unmanned surface vessel Aug 4 (UKMTO/UANI). Houthis cut off from Iranian resupply since ~Jul 4 (Stimson) — operating on stockpiles. Some Saudi tankers transit with AIS dark; others divert via Cape. War-risk ~0.75% hull. **Geography rule: the Cape route does not pass through Bab el-Mandeb — vessels on the Cape route are unaffected by Bab el-Mandeb activation. However, a Houthi Arabian Sea blockade (as threatened in the "second phase") would affect the Cape route's Arabian Sea leg — a materially new threat geometry.**
